Summary

"Turns out you were officially considered dead, like, nearly five years ago now. Mom never told me." He scoffs bitterly and Rio can sense anger there. "She doesn't know that I know. I can't mention your name around her without her changing the subject or shutting me down anyway."

His expression shifted to one of absolute determination. "I know you're still out there, Mama. I've tried to explain to Mom about time dilation and how being near a black hole means time will pass slower for you and that you could be back any day now but every time I say shit like that she gets mad at me." He runs a hand through his hair, exasperated. "She's mad at you. I think that's why she wont let me go to Stanford. 'Cause I'm becoming too much like you."

Rio could understand that Agatha would be mad, but she couldn't understand why she'd hold their son back from something he wants. 

"I think she might love Wanda, Mama."

Hearing that, the only thing that stopped Rio from wishing their shuttle had just been swallowed up by the black hole is the fact she'll soon see her son again. Because really, what was the point of making it back from a mission like this if she'd lost Agatha regardless? It was almost more cruel than if she had died, because now she was returning to a world where Agatha, her entire world, was in love with someone else. 

"I need you to come back. If that means I have to figure out a way to come up there to you then that's what I'll do." He'd said determinedly. 

The video ended after that.

 

Rio, who's willingly allowed herself to be launched into space more times than she can count, is reluctant even to get on the plane back to Houston. If she could've walked it, she would've. She's never had such a debilitating fear of having her feet off of solid ground. However, the flight back is short enough, leaving from Orlando and landing at the space centre in a couple of hours. It was around four in the morning when the wheels hit the tarmac. The plane had been completely silent the entire time. This was a whole new Earth to the one they left behind a week ago.

Rio's helped off of the plan by the recovery crew and given a wheelchair on the tarmac. Walking after even just a week in space is incredibly difficult and Rio hasn't slept in days. She doesn't even know where the fuck she's going now. She should probably check into a hotel until she can find her bearings. She turns to look over her shoulder at the guy pushing her across the huge expanse of empty space towards the space centre in the distance.

"Hey, could someone get me an Uber to the nearest Motel or something? I don't have a phone or anything-"

"Huh? Oh sure. You know the command centre contacted all of your families so there's probably someone here already to take you home."

It's still pretty dark outside, but just as they approach the centre, she notices a sliver of brilliant, gold light on the horizon in the purple sky. No one was waiting for her. Everyone had moved on by now. She couldn't understand why no one they'd seen since climbing out of the ocean could understand that this wasn't something to celebrate; that it was a tragedy.

There really was so much space between where their plane had landed and the doors to the centre. As she squints to make out a figure in the distance, she realises quickly that they're running. Was it someone from the command centre to greet them? They come closer and closer into focus. 

This looked like a civilian. They really shouldn't be allowed out here so it didn't make sense. Rio could make out plaid pyjama pants and a faded grey shirt, wild unbrushed hair flying behind her as she runs. It's so strikingly familiar that when the realisation hits her, it knocks the air out of her lungs. 

Is that...?

"Stop."

The recovery guy pushing her chair slows and stops on the tarmac and it becomes crystal-clear who's sprinting towards her now.

Agatha. 

Rio pushes to her feet and hobbles forward like a zombie, stumbling as her legs shake beneath her.

"Dr Vidal, I really must insist that you sit back down. You're not ready to walk yet."

Her wife, breathing hard, her eyes wet with tears and her mouth hanging open, slows to a jog, then stops completely when they're about ten feet apart. She looks at her like she's staring at a ghost.

God, she's fucking breathtaking. Fifteen years older and somehow even more beautiful than the day she left. Rio takes another uneasy step towards her, willing her legs to work. She wants to run to her but she's just hobbling half a foot at a time like she's broken all the bones in her legs. If you've ever had a nightmare where you're trying to run from something and suddenly your legs become completely useless - your heart pumping full of adrenaline and you can hardly move -  that's exactly how Rio feels.

Agatha doesn't help her, doesn't come any closer. She just stands there, staring, frozen in shock.

Rio needs to get to her. After billions of miles between them for fifteen years, she can manage another ten feet. She drags her right foot forward again and stumbles another half-foot forward, grunting as she does. They tremble beneath her, threatening to give out. She pauses to catch her breath, her eyes still locked with her wife's. Neither of them have spoken yet. The sun climbs a little higher, backlighting Agatha's brown hair at the edges, making her look like she's glowing. Rio would've thought she was staring at a statue if she hadn't just watched Agatha clear what seemed to be a mile to get to this point. Her eyebrows are pinched in the middle of her face like she's trying to make sense of it all. Like she can't believe her own eyes.

Rio finally breaks the silence.

"Sorry I'm late." 

That breaks whatever spell Agatha is under. 

She screams, a loud frustrated cry of anger and she rushes forward. She shoves Rio hard and onto her ass. Yeah, that's probably about right. She feels like she hits the ground at a hundred miles an hour because the gravity shift is so intense. She groans in pain and rolls on to her side.

"You fucking asshole!" Agatha screams down at her, her eyes glowing red as tears rush down her face. Rio tries to push herself back up. "That's all you've got to say?! Fifteen years and you've got fucking jokes?!"

Rio laughs a little because she absolutely should've expected this response. She pushes herself up and the recovery team member that had been pushing her rushes forward to help her but she waves him away.

"Can you give us a second?" She asks him. He looks at her like she's insane. "I'm fine. I'll be fine." 

Horrified, he slowly nods then walks over to join the other crew members making their way to the station.

Rio kneels on the tarmac, looking up at her now forty-five year old wife. There's miles of empty space around them as the sun creeps higher above the horizon.

"I know you're angry." Rio starts. "But could you help me up here? You remember, the gravity makes it hard to-"

"-You're really alive." Agatha whispers, the anger dissipating slightly as she remains fixed in place, staring down at her.

"So it would seem, my love." 

Agatha's face flashes between heartbroken, terrified, enraged, then back to heartbroken over and over as she stares down at Rio like she's only a figment of her imagination.

Rio realises she's not about to get any help from her wife and tries to pull herself to her feet, managing to get one foot flat on the ground, straining to pull the rest of her body upright. She grunts, then feels Agatha's arms under hers, helping her up.

She doesn't let go when Rio gets to her feet.

She tightly pulls her closer and sobs, a noise that Rio will never forget so long as she lives. 

Rio clings to her, partially to hold herself up, mostly because she feels like she's felt every second of the past fifteen years even though it's only been a week. Her hand flies to the back of her head and she cries too. The scent of Agatha's hair hasn't changed in the slightest. She may look different but she feels exactly the same as she holds Rio to her like she might float away. Rio's heart aches and she wishes she could reach through time and undo everything. She wants to blame someone; some thief that's taken a decade and a half from her in the blink of an eye, but the only person there is to blame is herself. Agatha's face buries in her neck and Rio can feel it growing damp.

"I'm so sorry, Agatha. I'm so, so, so fucking sorry." Rio repeats it in a whisper, over and over again. "I should never have left. I should've listened to you."

They stand like that for a minute or so, Agatha crying into her neck until Rio's hair is drenched, Rio desperately trying to apologise like it can somehow make up for fifteen years.

Suddenly, Agatha pulls away and wipes her face, almost as though she's embarrassed, like she's pissed at herself for the display of emotion. She quickly goes and gets Rio's wheelchair and brings it to her, helping her to sit down, acting like nothing's out of place at all. 

 

 

"I need to tell you something." Agatha speaks up in the car after an awkward stretch of silence. Well, Rio's sure it was awkward for Agatha, but Rio's just been enjoying being back in her orbit. She hasn't taken her eyes off of her, trying to memorise every new crease in her face, to replace the thirty-year-old Agatha she pictures with this new one.

"Hmm?"

"I'm uh...since you've been gone, I've been..." Agatha hesitates, staring straight ahead at the road.

"Seeing someone. Wanda." Rio finishes for her. "I know."

Agatha nearly swerves off the road when she turns to look at her in total shock. "How the hell do you know that!?"

"Nicky told me." She smiles sadly at her. "I got all the videos when we reentered the solar system a few days ago."

Agatha scoffs in disbelief, sticking her tongue in her cheek and shaking her head. "Of course."

"You've done an amazing job with him, baby."

Agatha tears up in an instant, her eyebrows furrowing and Rio's glad that scowl is aimed at the road ahead and not at her. "Don't."

"Don't what?"

"Don't do that. Don't call me that. And don't talk about Nicky like you know him."

Ouch. Tears well up in Rio's eyes and she gulps down the lump in her throat. 

"He sent four hundred videos Agatha. I watched every one of them. I know him. I'm still his Mama, even if he's not the little baby I left a week ago."

Agatha barks out a laugh so humourless and loud that Rio's startled briefly.

"A week ago?"

"That's how long it's been for me. Nicky's been trying to explain it all to you; he was right on the money, Agatha you should've listened to him-"

"Oh? So now it's my fault?" She scoffs. 

"I didn't say that."

"So what? You've just been sitting up there for a few days while I soothed every tantrum; went to every game; held it all together for years and you have the fucking audacity to sit there and cry like you missed us?"

"I did miss you!"

"Not like I-" Agatha stops herself, blinks hard to clear her eyes, then breathes out slowly. "Not like we did."

The car falls into silence again. Rio knows her wife well enough to know she's putting walls up and it breaks her fucking heart. She doesn't trust Rio anymore.

Rio's the one to break the silence. "So what are you going to tell Wanda?" 

"What do you mean?"

Rio watches her carefully. "Your wife's home. You don't think she'll have some thoughts-?"

"-You're not my wife. Not anymore. You died."

Rio's chest aches violently. 

"I clearly fucking didn't, Agatha."

Agatha seethes silently behind the wheel, exhaling loudly. 

"There's nothing for her to worry about."

Oh. Agatha really did love this woman. Her marriage was over. She'd lost everything. 

Rio turns to look out the window to hide the effect Agatha's words had. 

"That answers that then." She forces out.

Agatha hesitates for a second before she speaks again. "It's been fifteen fucking years Rio. Did you expect me to wait for you?"

Every word is like a dagger. She knows it's her own fault, and she knows Agatha had every right to move on, but she's fucking angry. She had Agatha in her arms a week ago, and though she understands that it doesn't feel that way to her, Agatha moving on feels like a huge betrayal. Agatha may have gotten over her, but Rio is still as in love with her as she always has been.

"No. I guess not." Her voice comes out wobbly and she knows Agatha can hear the pain in it because in the reflection of the window, she spots her head turn to look at her quickly. There's silence for a moment like she's trying to think of what to say.

"I'm engaged, Rio." Fuck. Rio squeezes her eyes closed, still facing out the passenger window. "You should know that now. I know it's difficult-"

"Difficult?" Rio laughs bitterly, shaking her head.

"You don't have any fucking right to be pissed about this. I didn't cheat. I waited thirteen years. Did you want me to be miserable forever?"

Every hour next to that black hole, fifteen of them, translated to one Earth-Year. If they'd taken just two hours less, would she still have her wife right now?

"I'm not pissed." Rio sighs. It's not true, at least not completely; she's pissed, but not at Agatha. This is all on her. She betrayed her family. She left. "I'm happy for you."

That part is a lie. 

Agatha's head turns again to look at her, she can see it in the reflection.

"That's it? That's all you're going to say about it?"

"What do you want me to say, Agatha?" She's met with silence. "Where are you taking me anyway?"

Agatha hesitates. "Home. There's a spare room, you know. I'd be pretty fucking awful if I let you come back to Earth and made you sleep at a Best Western."

Oh. So Rio gets to sleep across the hall from her wife in their bed with another woman. Great. 

"That's not necessary." Rio whispers, because if she speaks she'll break down.

"Don't be ridiculous. Nicky will want to meet you."

Rio feels a flash of hot rage. "He's met me, Agatha. I put him to bed seven nights ago-"

"Will you stop fucking saying that!? It hasn't been a week Rio! I don't know how the fuck to get that into your head right now but I need you to stop saying it's been a week because-"

"-Fine!" Rio shouts. Her voice cracks.

The car falls back into silence and eventually Rio calms enough to look back over at Agatha. Her fingers grip the steering wheel.

"You're really engaged to her?"

Agatha jumps a little when she hears her voice. "Yes. Why?"

Through all of her pain, through her tears, Rio sees a glimmer of hope. She smirks.

"Then why are you still wearing your wedding ring?"

Agatha doesn't answer her. They drive all the way home in silence. 

Rio knows one thing: she won't go down without a fight.

She will get her wife back.

 

Chapter 6: Home

Agatha really needs time to process everything. Fifteen years of agony have materialised in the form of her long-dead, now-resurrected wife, and she has to focus all of her attention on the road because she can't confront the way the shards of her shattered heart ache every time she looks at her. She hadn't known what she was going to do when she'd arrived at that space centre - likely because she hadn't fully believed Rio would be there at all. When she'd finally locked eyes with her as she stumbled across the tarmac, she was overcome with every single emotion she'd felt since she left. Like her body - in finally having confirmation that Rio was okay - allowed itself to fully feel it all. Unfortunately for Rio, the dominating emotion seemed to be anger, because now she had someone tangible to direct it towards. 

She pulls up to the driveway and now the sun has finally risen. It's around six in the morning, the sky around them glowing pink, the sunlight shimmering through the woods surrounding the house. Rio had chosen the place right after they'd gotten married and started planning for Nicky. It could be considered a small mansion, really. A large porch wraps around it, a bench and two rocking chairs, where Agatha likes to sit out listening to the rain during storms. There's a large dome in the centre of it, right at the top: Rio's observatory. For the first couple of years, Agatha had approached the house when she'd return home and refused to ever look up. She didn't want to see it and be reminded of what was missing. It was hard not to though because without Rio the place felt so much larger and so much quieter. It was harder to ignore the giant hole in her life. It felt fucking lonely. 

Wanda must have heard the car pull up, because already Agatha sees the porch light turn on and the front door swing open. Wanda stands there, looking confused, out into the driveway, squinting at the car, trying to work out why Agatha had driven off in the middle of the night. 

"Can you stay here for a sec? I need to talk to her." She finally breaks the silence as the engine shuts off. 

"I thought she had nothing to worry about." Rio mutters bitterly. Agatha feels a confusing mix of anger and guilt. She knows this must be fucking killing Rio. She knows, but god, after all that Agatha has had to endure, Rio moping around her new girlfriend is nothing. 

"She doesn't." Agatha forces herself to say. "But this will be a shock for her too. I don't want her to get the wrong idea."

"What wrong idea would that be?" Agatha can hear the agony in her voice. 

It's not fair. It really isn't fucking fair. Agatha's heart lurches into her throat and she wants to scream. Rio's hurt and she gets that, but she finally had started to feel some kind of normal again and now she's shown up just to make her feel guilty for it. So she has no problem responding to questions Rio really doesn't want to hear the answer to. 

"That I'm still in love with you."

She'd been sure it would hurt her to say it as much as it would hurt Rio to hear it, but she hears the little harsh intake of breath and watches her turn to look out the passenger window the way she's done the whole ride here when she didn't want Agatha to know how much pain she's in. Agatha knows, because fifteen years have passed but she still knows Rio like the back of her hand. She feels another wave of guilt and regrets saying it so harshly. This is exactly why she needs time to figure out how she feels. 

"I'm sorry." Agatha says. "I shouldn't have said-" She stops herself. Because shouldn't she have? Doesn't she have every right? Rio's not moving.  "Just give me five minutes, okay?"

Rio doesn't answer her. She just stares out the window at the miles and miles of forest around their home. Agatha wants to do something, but nothing she can do would send the right message. If she reaches out for her it would give her false hope. She also just can't trust herself to touch Rio right now. 

 

 

The house is already awake. Nicky's in the kitchen, preparing for his morning run. Much like his Mama, he needed to keep moving at all times, body and mind. They have a home-gym on the first floor that Rio had used to readjust to Earth's gravity after each mission.

 

"This has to be my favourite part of when you come home." Agatha had stood leering at Rio. She was in a daze, leaning against the door frame, her eyes drinking in the sight of her wife on the cable machine, the muscles in her arms and back flexed.

Rio caught her in the mirror and smirked, slowly pulling the bar down below her chin with a grunt.

"My favourite part is that you follow me around the house like a needy puppy." She groaned. Agatha felt herself growing hotter by the second. 

"I missed you. If you'd just stay in bed with me like I asked then I wouldn't follow you around so much."

Rio laughed, releasing the bar slowly upwards then swigs from the bottle of water at her foot. "You know I have to exercise when I get back. It's necessary. Trust me, baby there's no where I'd rather be than back in bed with you right now."

"I'm sure there are far more efficient ways to exercise." Her voice dropped suggestively. "Some that absolutely require you to come back to bed."

Rio had made it part of her routine after that to spend an extra hour in bed with her wife in the morning after that. For readjustment purposes. 

 

Agatha had replaced all the equipment and modernised it for Nicky, her overachieving son with the mentality of a hamster like his Mama. He still loves to run a route through the forest at the crack of dawn, despite the fact he sits in Rio's observatory studying until the early hours of the morning. 

"Who's that in the car? Where the hell did you go this morning?"

She'd brushes past Wanda and closes the front door quickly. She can still hear Nicky shuffling around in the kitchen and she drags Wanda into the living room and shuts them both inside.

"I need you to sit down."

Wanda frowns but slowly does as she's told. "You're scaring me."

Agatha takes a deep breath. "So please don't freak out about this. It doesn't mean anything for us, but it's about to mean a whole lot for Nicky." She begins. "I got a phone call this morning from Houston Space Centre."

Agatha doesn't say anything else because recounting the whole story might make her cry and reinforce the exact wrong message to her fiancee. In the silence, Wanda's face morphs from confusion, to slow realisation, to disbelief.

"Rio?" She gasps loudly.

Agatha shushes her quickly. "Yes. And I need to somehow tell Nicky that his Mama is in the driveway and-"

"She's here!? How the hell is that possible!? She's been gone for fifteen years!"

"Wanda!" Agatha whisper yells because she's not being quiet at all and she still has to break it to Nicky.

"I'm sorry!" Wanda lowers her volume a little. "But I don't understand how she could've survived so long-"

"Gravity. Time Dilation. Fuck! I don't understand it but for some reason she thinks it's only been a week and she hasn't aged a day." Agatha explains in a rush, pacing the floor. "Time moved differently or something."

Wanda just sits there in shock, her eyebrows knit together like she's piecing it together. 

"And I'm assuming, by the fact you've brought her here, that your plan is to have her stay here?"

Wanda is not taking this the way she'd hoped. She already sounds insecure as hell. 

"Well it is her home, Wanda. What am I supposed to do? Tell Nicky I put her in a motel?"

"So let me get this straight, and stop me if I'm misunderstanding anything," Wanda starts. "Your wife, whom you've been mourning for fifteen years, has come back from the dead. She's got the face and body of a thirty year old, she still feels as though you were together a week ago and...you've offered her to live with us?"

When Agatha hears it all laid out like that...yeah it sounds pretty bad. 

"Yeah." Agatha says.

"And you expect me to believe you don't have feelings for her?" Wanda scoffs.

"I don't." Agatha says quickly. "I mean I do. I have so many feelings but not-" she sighs. "not like that."

It's a lie, obviously. We're talking about the love of her fucking life here, but it's totally out of the question. She had Wanda now. She was finally returning to some version of...well maybe not happiness, but contentedness. Rio doesn't just get to return back to her after, what to her, was a weeks vacation across the galaxy and have everything waiting for her. She had the nerve to come back here and expect to return to her wife and child like nothing has happened at all?  

"And you think she'll just...accept that you've moved on? She won't try to get you back?"

Agatha pauses. She's not so sure. In the car, she'd expected Rio to put up more of a fight than she had. She'd just seemed betrayed. Agatha hadn't betrayed anyone by trying to move on and she refuses to be made to feel guilty for trying to heal.

"She wouldn't succeed even if she did. She left us. I love you." Agatha crouches in front of her now and takes her face in her hand. "I'm marrying you."

That seems to relax Wanda just a little and she smiles. The sound of the car door slamming shut from the driveway pulls her attention in a split-second. She rushes to the window to see Nicky, standing still near the front door. Fuck.

She shoots Wanda a panicked look then rushes out on to the porch. 

Rio has climbed out of the car. Agatha hadn't noticed how tired she'd looked back at the space station. She was more focussed on who the hell she was actually looking at, rather than how she looked. 

"Nicky?" 

Her voice can hardly be heard, but it's so early in the morning and even the birds have barely woken up yet, so it carries.

Agatha feels her heart break all over again. She's conscious of the fact that Wanda might mistake any big display of emotion for something that it isn't. 

She wonders for a second if Nicky will even recognise her, standing there in the driveway, dark circles shadowing her reddened eyes. Her face is wet with tears, likely from the conversation she'd had with Agatha on the way here. So what if Rio had cried for an hours drive, maybe even for a few days in space when she realised how long she'd been gone? Agatha had cried for Rio for years, so they're nowhere near even. 

"Mama?" Nicky drops his water bottle on the gravel and it rolls a few feet away.

Agatha feels Wanda's arm wrap around her waist supportively and she's hit with a nauseating wave of guilt. Rio isn't looking at her right now anyway.

Rio tries to hobble forward, clinging to the opened passenger door for support. Nicky jumps into action, sprinting across the drive and wrapping his Mama up in a bearhug.

Agatha ignores the way her mind drifts to how she'd felt clinging to Rio on that tarmac, how she'd smelled like their home from fifteen years ago - literally pulled through time. How she hadn't wanted to ever let go. How she'd somehow managed to fight the urge to kiss her. 

All of these feelings would be enough to confuse anyone. 

She hears Rio break down into Nicky's shoulder. He was taller than Rio by a few inches. Agatha watches on and feels that anger creeping back again. How is it so easy for Nicky to forgive the woman that had abandoned them? She can still hear him on his bedroom floor, screaming his throat raw for his Mama while Agatha begged him to calm down. It felt like he blamed her. 

After a minute, Rio pulled back to grab Nicky's face in her hands and study it carefully as he laughed. 

"Jesus, dude when's the last time you slept?" She jokes, her voice still trembling.

"I could say the same thing to you!" 

The two of them get along as though they knew each other. As though Rio had been there all these years and Agatha's infuriated. Rio's attention finally turns to Agatha again, standing there on the porch with Wanda's arm around her. Agatha watches as her jaw clenches and her shoulders stiffen. Nicky helps her approach the porch steps.

"What does it feel like? The post-spaceflight adaptation? Do your muscles just feel weak or do you feel sore?" Nicky interrogates excitedly.

"Heavy. I just feel heavy." She tells him. Her voice sounds so, so fucking sad. Well, Agatha had Nicky's whole lifetime of sadness. 

"How long has it been for you? Did you get my videos? What does a black hole look like?"

"Buddy, Rio needs some sleep, okay? Maybe ease up on the questions a little." Wanda suggests. 

If looks could kill, Wanda would've been gone the second Rio laid eyes on her. Jesus. She finally makes it up onto the deck and gives her the most foul glare Agatha has ever seen. 

"I'm not tired. I haven't seen my son in fifteen years. We have a lot of catching up to do." 

Nicky grins at Rio like she hung the fucking moon and helps her inside the house. Agatha hates the way she wants to join them. She hates how her stomach lurches when Rio's out of sight, like she's leaving all over again.

All this time she's wanted Rio home and now she's here, her chest aches like she's still billions of miles away. 

"You okay?" Wanda asks softly when the door closes behind them. 

"Yeah." Agatha lies. "Better leave them to it, right?" 

Rio's home, and somehow Agatha has never felt more homesick. 

Chapter 7: Earth Angel

April 2006

Agatha, with her head resting on Rio's chest from where she's lying flat on top of her on the couch, quickly puts her hand over her mouth.

"Shh!"

'Earth Angel' is being performed by Marvin Berry and The StarLighters on stage at the Enchantment Under The Sea dance on screen. Marty McFly has just started to play the guitar, running out of time to get his parents back together. George and Lorraine sway to the music. 
 
'George, aren't you gonna kiss me?'

'I-I don't know.'

An obnoxious ginger kid butts in and sends George stumbling back into the crowd. 

'Scram McFly, I'm cutting in.'

Marty collapses back against the side of the stage, his picture starting to disappear from the photograph and his hand becoming translucent as his existence fades.

Agatha gasps, fully engrossed in the movie. Rio chuckles and watches her, not the movie. She's seen it a million times; it's her favourite, so she doesn't have to look at the screen anyway. If there was some paradox and you had to go back in time and try to win me over again somehow, you'd have no problem getting me to fall in love with you again."

Rio's heart nearly bursts out of her chest.

"Say that again?"

Agatha obviously realises what she just said because her eyes widen as she tears them from the screen to look at Rio. They're swimming with panic. Rio's about to tell her not to worry about it but Agatha shushes her, her eyebrows pinching in concentration like she's desperate to finally say it.

'Excuse me' George McFly shoves past the ginger to take Lorraine in his arms again as the music begins to build.

Rio's unable to contain her joy. Her eyes well up and she pulls Agatha's face to hers and kisses her hard, still smiling as she does.

On screen, George finally kisses Lorraine. Marty's hands return to normal and the strum of his electric guitar sounds out as the music crescendos.

 

Present

 

Rio spends the whole day with Nicky. Partially because she thinks every second with him from now on is like gold dust, she just wants to know everything there is to know about him. Partially because she's avoiding Agatha. Seeing her standing there on the porch of their house with another woman's arm around her was a special kind of agony. She knows Agatha isn't doing any of this to hurt her, but that doesn't make it sting any less. He tells her all about how he got the news about his scholarship for Stanford, shows her all of the research and studies he'd done in pursuit of the truth of what had happened to his Mama. Rio recommends some of her old favourite books and tells him all about what it was like in space. They're sitting up in Nicky's room when Agatha calls them for dinner. "Well yeah, given that I've slept in it more nights than you have in the last fifteen years." 

Wanda approaches her now, getting right in her face.

Rio doesn't back down. "You think that makes it yours? When my identity is reinstated it comes back under my name, alongside Agatha's"

"Who I've also slept with more than you have in the last fifteen years." Wanda sneers cruelly.

Agatha watches Rio's knuckles whiten, her breathing quickens and she stiffens suddenly. She realises that Rio might actually hurt Wanda.

"Wanda." Agatha snaps. "Stop acting like a petulant child. She gets it."

The two of them still don't move, like they're having a staring competition. Wanda is typically pretty docile so Rio must've really got on her nerves. 

"Are you going to ask her?"

Agatha's heart aches in anticipation of this conversation. She wouldn't even have to participate in it if she hadn't answered that phone call. She could've nullified their marriage and that would've been the end of it. 

"Ask me what?" Rio asks. Agatha tries to remember what her face looks like before she has to ask what Wanda wants her to. Her chest aches again and she just wants to walk away from the conversation altogether, but she knows that if there's any chance at making things right with her fiancee, she can't hold this off. 

"For a divorce."

Rio's whole expression shifts. Her eyelids flicker, her jaw goes slightly slack and she looks at Agatha, totally puzzled. Then Agatha catches her look down at her wrist and-

Rio's silver watch isn't on anymore. Agatha's gold one is in its place, the crown still pulled up. Agatha knows what she's wondering. Why would you send me this message then ask for a divorce? 

"Oh. Right." Rio swallows, then steels her expression again. "It will have to wait until all of my information is restored. I'll call them up later-"

"-No!" Agatha says quickly, panicked. Both Rio and Wanda look taken aback by her outburst. "I mean, don't do that today. I'm sure they'll send your stuff over. There's no real rush. We don't have a date anyway-"

"-Agatha, can I speak with you? Upstairs?" Wanda says through grit teeth. Agatha feels a surge of dread and annoyance because she just knows Wanda's going to be pissed. She has a right to be, of course, but Agatha can't bring herself to feel responsible for someones pain again. 

"Fine." She sighs. She shoots Rio a look that she hopes conveys some kind of apology for the heartbreak she knows she just inflicted but Rio's face is so stoic she doesn't know if she receives it. 

 

 

It starts the second the bedroom door closes. 

"I'm about a second away from packing my shit and just leaving right now, Agatha." 

It's all just too quick. She can't think right now. She'd been happy with Wanda, she really, really had, but everything's so confusing.

"Why are you in such a rush? Why is everyone in such a rush?" Agatha groans, rubbing her face with her hands and pacing the floor.

"Why are you trying to delay it!?"

"Delay what!? We don't have a fucking date!" Agatha explodes. "Not only that, but my wife just came back from the fucking dead and no one seems to care that I'm a wreck! That I'm grieving the lost time; that I'm celebrating my son having his other mother back; that I'm devastated every time I see how much she's hurting, but that I keep causing her more pain anyway! I don't need this on top of it all!" Wanda blinks at her, seemingly taking in her words.

"None of this is fair, Wanda! I know it's not fair on you and I'm trying my hardest to make sure you feel supported but no one's doing that for me! All you're doing is pushing me to reject the woman I once chose to build a life with! I told you I chose you but that wasn't enough, you wanted me to break her heart and, god knows she deserves it, but it hurts!"

Then her eyes land on Agatha's left hand that's carding through her hair. 

"You put it back on." She says dejectedly. 

"What?"

"Your ring. It's back on."

Oh. Shit.

"It-it is. It's complicated-"

Wanda sighs and sits on the edge of the bed, all the fight leaving her voice. "Why did you really stop Rio from calling up to check on her identity?"

Agatha considers lying for a second. She does. But then she thinks about how it felt to touch Rio again in the kitchen. How she'd felt like she was standing on a livewire for the first time in so many years. How she hasn't felt anything close with Wanda.

But really, she knows already how this is going to end. No matter what her feelings are for Rio, she can't keep doing this with Wanda; arguing about the nature of her relationship with her wife. It's not fair. She knows it in her heart that she loves Rio because they're a family - her, Rio and Nicky. Of course she loves her, even if it's not like that.

Wanda will never be able to accept that.

"They already called. They called my phone because it was the only number on file."

Wanda looks like she already knows where Agatha's going with this.

"And?"

She sighs. "They asked if I - if Rio - wanted to keep the marriage or nullify it." Wanda's face slackens with pain, waiting for Agatha to put the final nail in the coffin that was their relationship.

"I asked to keep it."

She knows it was a shitty thing to do. She knows she's been firing mixed signals at everyone but she can't come to terms with her own feelings right now and so her decision making has been poor to say the least. At least now she's cut Wanda free its one less person to worry about hurting.

"I can't compete, Agatha. I don't think I ever could, but I had a chance with her up there." Wanda points upwards. "I don't stand a chance with her on Earth and you've known it from the second you got the call."

Agatha wants to fight back, to tell her that she's wrong. That Rio's done too much damage to have a chance now. It really doesn't matter, because she knows she can't be with Wanda right now anyway. She can't be with either of them.

"I'm sorry I hurt you. I'm sorry I lied about how we met." She forces herself to say. "I'm sorry it had to end this way."

Wanda swipes at her cheek. They stay in silence for a few minutes as though internally coming to terms with the end of it all. Wanda finally speaks.

"So that's it? You're going back to her? After everything-"

"I didn't say that." Agatha says harshly. "I don't forgive her. I'm not taking her back. But she will always be part of my life and I know we can't be together if she is."

Wanda doesn't argue. She nods slowly. 

"I guess I'll just...send for the rest of my stuff later in the week then." She says almost in a whisper. Agatha can see the tears glistening on her face as she stands to leave. 

'I really am sorry, Wanda." Agatha says sincerely. 

"Yeah. Me too."

 

 

It was already late in the morning when she'd woken up earlier and she locks herself in the bathroom for a long time after Wanda leaves, unable to face a heartbroken Rio straight after. Of course, the divorce is no longer imminently necessary, but it might be eventually. Agatha honestly doesn't know. She doesn't see herself being with anyone else now, so really what's the point?

She bathes in there, then stares at her own reflection in the mirror, observing the forty-five-year-old face looking back at her. She doesn't know exactly what changes have appeared in the time Rio was gone, and she wonders if Rio can spot them all clear as day. She knows the grief and mourning must've taken a toll on her in some way. 

She emerges a little before dinner to find that Rio has already sorted it all. She's cleaned up the mess from earlier and seemingly figured out how to work the stove. The table is set already and she's smiling at Agatha like nothing even happened earlier. Agatha feels her heart swell. She'd really went to the effort of taking care of her knowing she'd feel guilty for asking for the divorce. She didn't push her on it, she just made sure she was here for her and Nicky. 

Dinner was just like last night - the three of them all talking about Nicky's day at school. Agatha shared some stories of things Nicky got up to as a kid and found that it didn't hurt to talk about it anymore. That she didn't focus on the absence of Rio, just the memories with her son: the time he played John Proctor in The Crucible and tried to make the character as cartoonishly villainous as possible, much to the chagrin of his drama teacher; the time he ran track and would've come first but he stopped to help a fallen kid; the time he won the science fair with a replica model of the shuttle his Mama flew to the black hole. 

Rio didn't seem upset at all. She grinned through every story, reaching over to ruffle her sons hair with pride every so often. 

"Moms, I'm having so much fun but I promised Skye I'd call her tonight."

Agatha's eyebrows raise. 

"Skye? You guys are talking again?" She tries to sound as casual as possible, like she's not prying, but her son rolls his eyes anyway. 

"Yes, Mom. We're...seeing where things go."

"This the girl you broke up with a few months ago?" Rio asks bluntly, nearly making Agatha laugh.

"She broke up with me." He mutters. "But I'm trying to win her back." 

Agatha gasps, horrified. "Women can't be won."

Rio and Nicky both laugh.

"You know that's not what I meant." He says, standing and taking his plate with him. "I love you guys."

He stops by, planting a kiss on Agatha's cheek before heading for the door. 

Agatha calls back after him.  "Hey, where's your Mama's kiss?" 

"Maybe you can give her one for me?" He calls in return, his back still turned. Agatha can hear the smirk in his voice as he walks away. 

Agatha's face flames at that, the heat spreading to the tops of her ears. Little, conniving shit. Rio just laughs. 

"He's mad at me." 

Agatha frowns a little. "Nicky? What makes you say that?"

"He's been less affectionate since he found out the truth. I think he's worried to tell me how he feels in case I leave again."

Agatha's heart breaks at that. Rio's right - she had noticed Nicky pulling away from Rio a little and, though it was nothing like the treatment she'd received, it must still hurt. 

"I'm sorry. He'll come around."

"He doesn't have to." Rio shrugs. "It's my thing to fix, not his."

Agatha knows Rio's not just talking about Nicky. She feels her stomach flutter a little.

"I'm gonna just clean up and sit out on the porch for a bit. Thank you, by the way. Dinner was really good. I'm surprised you still know how to cook." She smirks. 

"After a week?"

Agatha could correct her and say fifteen years, but she doesn't feel the need. 

"No, because you nearly burned our house down this morning." Rio's face softens and she smiles like a goofy idiot. "What?"

Rio shrugs, still smiling as she collects her own plate and reaches for Agatha. 

"Our house."

Agatha swallows. "It is our house. Legally."

"Technically not. I'm dead, remember? I just haunt it now."

She says it jokingly but something about it stings Agatha in her gut. 

"You have the whole time." 

Rio's smile falters. She looks like she's about to apologise for saying that, but Agatha doesn't want to have that kind of conversation right now. Not after today.

"You want to come sit with me?"

 

 

It's a clear night. They're not both on the bench this time: Agatha sits on the bench while Rio takes the armchair to the left of her. Agatha stares out at the thousands of stars twinkling above the forest. She tries not to think about how many nights she spent out here imagining Rio billions of miles away, and that now she's right there. 

She breaks the silence after a few moments.

"You used to love sitting out here and looking up when we first moved in. You could sit here for hours with your telescope some nights." 

She turns to look at Rio, expecting to see her looking up, utterly encapsulated by the cosmos as she always was. But she's not.

She's looking right at her with the same expression she always reserved for the stars. Agatha loses her breath for a moment. 

"I'm sick of looking at them now."

She can feel her heartbeat in her throat.

"I guess you've had fifteen years of that, right?"

"No, I've had a w-" Rio stops herself and starts again, with a sigh. "I guess I have."

Agatha smiles at that and looks over at her. Her eyes involuntarily land on the watch and she scolds herself the second she feels Rio catch her do it. 

"You don't mind me wearing it, right?" Rio asks softly.

Agatha clears her throat awkwardly. She knew this conversation was coming, but she's inadvertently spurred it on. 

"Where's yours?" She says defectively. 

Rio smirks. "Nightstand. I liked this one better."

Agatha clenches her jaw and reminds herself that she's still really mad at Rio so that fluttering in her stomach means nothing.

Agatha clears her throat. "Did you fix yours to say the right time yet?"

"It says the same thing this one does."

Agatha wills her breathing to slow down and tries to stifle the butterflies cascading around in her gut.

"So it's wrong."

"Is yours wrong?" Rio says gesturing to the gold watch on her wrist. 

Agatha's lip trembles. "I don't know. Maybe."

"That's okay." Rio whispers, watching her carefully. Agatha wants to pull her eyes away because the way Rio's looking at her is nothing short of dangerous.  "But mine isn't. It'll stay frozen at the same time forever, and it will still be right."

Agatha can't let it be that easy. She hasn't forgiven Rio. She doesn't know if she ever will, but warmth spreads through her chest like sunlight on skin through a car window. 

Rio stands, sighing and dusting herself off. 

"I need to apologise for something in advance." She says once she's standing, her voice louder now. Deadly serious. God, what now?

"For what?"

"I'm going to have to break my promise." 

Agatha's heart does fucking somersaults but she somehow manages to keep her face still. 

"You are?"

"Yeah. And I'm afraid I'm going to make you fall in love with me again."

She says it like she's apologising for having to work late or something. Agatha can hardly breathe as she blinks up at her wife.

"I'm testing your hypothesis and I'm pretty sure all the evidence is pointing me in one direction. You were right: I’m a scientist. So I don't care if it takes forever, I don't care if I have to spend the rest of my life trying to find a way. I know I will."

Agatha can't speak. She just stares up at Rio as she heads for their front door. Right before she disappears, leaving Agatha on the porch, her eyes wet from something other than pure pain and hurt for the first time in recent history, she hears Rio speak one more time.

"I have nothing but time and I'm going to spend every second of it loving you."
