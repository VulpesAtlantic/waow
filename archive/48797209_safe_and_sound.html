Missions where the six of them aren’t all together make Gamora anxious.

It’s not that she doesn’t believe that all of them are perfectly capable of looking after themselves, because they absolutely are. More than they’ve ever been. They’ve never been stronger, both individually and as a team.

But there’s always a part of her that gets overwhelmingly nervous with no explanation at the thought of them all splitting up, and she tries to not let it consume her as the team goes off separately.

It certainly doesn’t help that their last mission ended with Peter in hospital and Gamora staying up all night as he was unconscious, worrying about him, as always.

Peter always tells her to take a deep breath and squeeze his hand, so she did just that and tried her hardest to stop her mind from going places she doesn’t want it to.

It didn’t help, without him able to calm her himself.

That’s in the past, though.

Gamora shakes herself out of her thoughts, internally reminding herself that all the panic and stress of Peter being in the hospital was over two weeks ago now, and he’s all clear to go back on missions as usual. He’s walking normally again, and this mission is simple enough, meaning she should not be worrying like she is.

He meets her gaze from across the room as everyone else is getting ready, and he smiles.

Gamora walks to him slowly, and exhales as Peter wraps his arms around her middle in response to her arms around his neck, trying to get as close to him as possible.

“Don’t tell me not to worry, because I’m going to do that anyway,” she says into his chest, her voice muffled.

Peter just laughs, and holds her tighter. “I’m gonna be okay.”

“I know. I’m going to be okay too.”

“I know.”

Gamora lifts her head up from Peter’s chest, and leans up to kiss him softly. It’s short, but sweet, and Gamora is careful to savour it and tuck the moment away safely into her heart.

“I love you,” she whispers.

“I love you too.”
