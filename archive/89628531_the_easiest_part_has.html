The Easiest Part Has Always Been You

Bonded pair, do not separate.

That’s what it should say above Agatha Harkness and Rio Vidal’s heads whenever they walk into a room.

At least, that’s what every single one of their friends thinks.

Well...

That, and also that their idiot best friends are clearly in love with each other but refuse to acknowledge it or see it.

Agatha and Rio met all the way back in middle school in Massachusetts where they grew up.

That was when Rio was the first person to truly see Agatha and love her exactly as she was, giving her a glimpse of the unconditional love she’d never felt growing up in a house with a mother who hated her own life choices, and hated what came out of them.

Aka, her.

Rio met Agatha doing the most Agatha thing possible, scamming money off the class jock by selling him fake test answers from thirty years ago, answers the idiot had no idea weren’t applicable anymore to the test happening that very day.

There was just one problem.

He was on the verge of backing out of the deal based on Agatha’s reputation.

So Agatha marched confidently right over to one Rio Vidal, the smartest person in class, and smoothly slid over and asked her in front of all of their classmates if she thinks the test she had was a fake or not.

She KNEW Agatha was lying.

She had been watching Agatha for a while now and what she did for a side hustle.

So at first, Rio felt conflicted about the lying aspect of it all.

Lying never made sense to Rio.

But...

When the beautiful girl who sat next to you in class wanted to make some cash off of a dumb boy, who was Rio to stand in the way?

Rio lied for the first time that day.

The wide, mischievous grin that spread across Agatha’s face was worth every second of the consequences that followed.

Agatha grabbed Rio by the wrist at the end of that school day, declared they were walking home together, and from that moment on, they’d been inseparable ever since.

They went to college together, they moved to apartments across from each other after graduating too.

Being in each other’s lives was just part of their identity at this point.

There was no questioning who they were to each other while they’re together because, well, they don’t remember a life where they didn’t have each other.

It’s like when you meet someone and you instantly forget what life was like before them.

And that's where they still are today.

Alice stares at her dumbfounded again.

“And you thought that was a normal thing friends felt for their friends?”

“I mean a bit yea.. I just thought it’s a bodily response to being touched by beautiful women..”

Alice shoots back at her without a second of silence, “You feel turned on when i hug you?”

“What? Ew no.”

“I mean Ew is a little harsh but.. you get my point now i hope.”

Alice sits back and continues,

"I'm saying this with so much love, Rio. You're one of the smartest people I know. You notice details no one else does, you remember everything, you're an incredible doctor already..." She smiles softly.

"But when it comes to relationships, you've always had a blind spot.”

Rio looks down, a little embarrassed now for taking so long to get here. “…So what now?”

“Just take some time to sus it out. The next time you're with Agatha, don't shove those feelings into the friendship box.” She takes another bite of her cinnamon roll and continues with food in her mouth, “Recognize the feelings next time you’re hanging out with Agatha and see how it feels to feel them around her while knowing they exist.”

How does one sus out romantic feelings?

I guess Rio will have to figure it out.

 

The problem is, Agatha has been busier than ever this past week.

She's been working late on some sort of new project, and every time Rio asks if she can come over after work, Agatha tells her she's not home.

Which is strange.

Twice now, Rio swears she’s smelling the scent of Agatha's perfume lingering in the hallway between their apartments.

She stands there for a second each time, wondering if she imagined it.

Maybe Agatha had only stopped by to grab something before leaving again.

Maybe someone else in the building happened to wear the same perfume.

Or maybe since Alice's conversation she’s been thinking so much about Agatha that now she was imagining things that weren't even there.

 

Rio's just making herself a cup of tea in her apartment a few days later when she hears a loud crash coming from Agatha’s along with an equally loud yelp.

She doesn't even think.

Her mug lands back on the kitchen counter with a loud clink as she runs out the door.

"Agatha! I'm coming in!" she calls, already opening the door.

She doesn't give her a chance to answer.

She finds her best friend on the floor with several large wooden planks collapsed on top of her.

"What happened?" Rio rushes over and lifts the planks off Agatha, who does little more than pout through the pain.

"I was just putting a bookshelf together and I slippe—"

Before Agatha can finish explaining, Rio's eyes catch the deep gash running down her forearm.

Blood.

"You're hurt." Rio's entire demeanor changes in an instant. "Where's your first aid kit?"

"It's fine. It doesn't hurt tha—"

"Agatha." Rio cuts her off sharply. "Where is it?"

The tone makes Agatha pause.

She lets out a long, defeated sigh before tilting her head toward the bathroom.

"First drawer under the sink."

Rio is gone before she's even finished the sentence.

By the time she comes back, Agatha has managed to scoot herself against the sofa, still sitting on the floor with one hand clamped over her arm.

Rio knows she's hurt.

Her best friend has simply always been far too stubborn to admit pain. Or defeat.

She kneels beside her, opens the first aid kit, and gets to work.

Agatha hisses quietly as the alcohol wipe passes over the cut.

"I know," Rio murmurs. “It won’t take long i promise.”

She gently holds Agatha's forearm with one hand while applying the ointment with the other before carefully starting to wrap the bandage around the wound.

It feels strangely intimate.

"You should've just called me," Rio says softly, smoothing the end of the bandage down with her thumb. "I always help you with this sort of thing."

Agatha doesn't answer.

Not for several long seconds.

She looks embarrassed.

Vulnerable.

"I already take up too much of your time," she says quietly.

She doesn’t even look Rio in the eye.

Rio's hands stop moving, but she keeps her hand on Agatha’s wrist.

"I've never complained about that."

Agatha just shrugs her shoulders.

Another stubborn pout.

Like she doesn't know how to explain herself without saying far more than she's said.

Rio stands up and announces, "Well, I'm building it for you."

Agatha opens her mouth to protest.

Then closes it again.

“...Yea okay."

Rio smiles to herself and begins gathering the scattered pieces of the bookshelf.

Agatha stays where she is on the floor, back resting against the couch, quietly watching her work.

An awkward silence settles over the apartment.

Agatha's acting... off.

Rio frowns as she slots two wooden panels together.

Usually, she's the best person to come to if you need help reading Agatha Harkness.

This past week, though?

She's coming up empty.

So she decides to do the one thing they've always been good at.

Cheering her up.

"Wanna know what Alice told me Monday?" Rio asks, dragging the sentence out like she's about to share some fun gossip.

Which isn't usually something that interests Rio.

But...

Agatha likes gossip.

So every now and then, Rio indulges her.

Agatha glances up eyebrows lifting in quiet interest.

"Apparently..." Rio begins, "Jen fired their fourth wedding planner."

Agatha's eyebrows climb a little higher.

"Oh?"

"Said they had a... 'millennial beige personality.'" Rio pauses, trying to remember Alice's exact wording. "Because they suggested putting shirts with Jen and Alice's faces on them in the wedding gift bags."

Agatha lets out an undignified snort before she can stop herself.

Rio smiles.

There it is.

The first genuine laugh she'd gotten out of Agatha all week.

"Too bad." Agatha shakes her head, still smiling. "I would've loved wearing that shirt to work every day just to annoy Jennifer."

They both laugh for another minute.

"I can design one for you," Rio offers. "You can print two and give one to her as a gag gift."

Agatha chuckles. "...Yeah."

The apartment falls quiet again.

Even with the awkwardness hanging between them, it's been nice.

Really nice.

Rio hadn't realized how much she'd missed this over the past week.

Just...

being around Agatha.

Maybe she can use some of Agatha's own tips and tricks to cozy up again.

She is supposed to inspect their connection more closely, see what Alice has been talking about.

So far, she's experienced warmth in her chest seeing Agatha smile, and panic shooting through her entire nervous system the second she saw Agatha in pain.

Maybe Alice was right.

Maybe she just needed to start paying closer attention.

Rio sets the bookshelf where Agatha wanted it before making her way over to the couch and sitting beside her.

"Wanna order some pizza and watch your trashy reality shows?"

Agatha gives her that infamous half smile Rio has always loved.
"Sure.”

 

Their food arrives half an hour later, and they spend a while eating on the couch while some reality show plays in the background.

Rio then remembers Alice's words to pay attention to what she feels around the brunette.

She subtly scoots a little closer to Agatha.

Then a little more.

Then more.. until their shoulders are almost touching.

She can tell Agatha notices immediately which makes her nervous.

She feels even warmer now. Agatha smells nice and she looks really beautiful from this angle.

I mean, that was never something Rio was confused about.

She always found Agatha beautiful.

"What are you doing, Rio?" Agatha bluntly asks.

Rio lets out a nervous chuckle. "Just getting comfortable."

"Well you're acting weird. Well... weirder than you usually are. Stop it."

Rio mumbles a quiet, "Sorry."

They go back to watching the show.

A few minutes later, they both reach for their wine glasses at the same time, their hands meeting in the middle of the coffee table.

Rio lets out a small fake cough to dispel the sudden tension before picking up her own glass. Then almost instinctively, she grabs Agatha's and hands it to her.

"Here."

Agatha nods. "...Thanks."

Rio wraps both hands around her own glass.

Now that she’s putting her senses around Agatha to the test, She can't help but notice how nice Agatha's skin felt against hers.

Soft.

Rio notices her palms are starting to sweat too.

She frowns down at them.

...Has that always happened?

Or had she just spent fifteen years never paying enough attention to notice?

Has her friendship with Agatha been so instinctual and a part of who she is that she never let herself think there’s more to it?

Back on the tv screen there’s a scene where a character that looks.. awfully similar to Agatha is kissing someone.

Which is when another question enters her mind.

If Agatha met someone tomorrow...

How would I feel?

Someone who replaces everything i do for her now.

Who puts furniture together for her.

Orders take out and spends most nights after work with her.

Goes with her to parties as a pair.

Sits as close to her as i am right now.

Rio pictures it before she can stop herself.

The image hits her so hard it feels like all the air is punched straight out of her lungs.

Her stomach drops and she feels sick.

No.

No, she doesn't want that to happen.

Not even a little.

…Oh

Her eyes slowly drift over to Agatha, who's still completely invested in the ridiculous reality show playing on the television.

Nothing had changed.

Not really.

Alice hadn't given Rio those feelings or pushed her to create them this week.

She had just helped Rio recognize and notice feelings she had been carrying in her body for fifteen years without labeling them in her mind.

 

I'm in love with Agatha.

Her mind now screams loudly.

 

“Agatha.”

“Hm?”

“I’m in love with you.”

Agatha spits out her wine everywhere and coughs really loudly.

Rio immediately reaches for the napkins.

“…Sorry. I probably should’ve waited until you swallowed.”

Agatha just stares at her with the most shocked expression on her face Rio's ever seen.

“…What?”

“I love you.” Rio admits it out loud again. “Like... as more than friends.”

“Rio…” Agatha shakes her head, already feeling her chest tighten. “Don’t do this.”

“What?”

“Please.”

“I don’t understand.”

“It’ll ruin everything.”

“Ruin what?”

“Our... relationship!” Agatha’s voice is now cracking and is more desperate. “What we have!”

Rio's eyebrows knit together.

“I don’t understand how this could be a bad thing.”

“Because loving someone like me isn’t easy, Rio!” Agatha lets out a shaky laugh that sounded mocking at her own expense, and scoots away from Rio until she’s in the far end of the couch.

“I’m difficult. You know that. I make everything harder than it has to be. I’m literally as broken as it gets.”

Rio opens her mouth, but Agatha keeps going before she can interrupt.

“Everyone who’s ever tried to love me left eventually. You wanna be one of them too? For what? We’re fine as is!” Her voice cracks and eyes start turning red and teary.

Agatha wipes at her eyes, looking away.

“But that doesn’t make any sense to me.”

Agatha lets out a small, defeated laugh.

“What doesn’t make sense to you?”

Rio gently shakes her head. “Agatha, loving you.. has always been the easiest part.”

Agatha finally looks back at her, searching Rio's face for any sign that this is some kind of joke.

There isn't one.

"I don't see why this would suddenly become the hard part." Rio says softly. "Logically... it would only increase our chances of happiness."

Agatha doesn't answer.

"Do you not love me back like that?" Rio asks, her own voice beginning to crack. "Is that why you're against this?"

Agatha's head snaps up.

"I didn't say that."

Rio's heart pounds.

"So you do?" She asks with more hope in her voice. "Please... just answer me honestly. I need to kno—"

"OF COURSE I LOVE YOU!"

The words tear out of Agatha before she can stop them.

"I loved you since the day you stood up to my mother even though you knew how psychotic she was. I loved you when you took me home to meet your mother and never once acted ashamed to introduce me to her. Hell, I loved you from the day you lied to Scott the fucking meathead and told him I wasn't lying just so I could rob him of his monthly allowance."

Her hands are flying everywhere now, every emotion she'd buried for years spilling out all at once.

"I do love you back, Rio." Her voice breaks. "I don't know why it took me so long to realize it, or why I'm so terrified of letting this happen, but i just feel so scared to..."

She can’t even finish.

The words get stuck somewhere behind the tears and in her throat.

Her whole body is trembling now, her cheeks flushed a deep pink as she stares stubbornly at the floor again.

Rio stands up from her seat slowly.

She crosses the small space between them until they're only inches apart.

Without saying a word, she slips her hands around Agatha's lower back and gently pulls her against her.

Agatha doesn't resist.

She sinks into Rio's arms, burying her face in the crook of her neck.

Rio holds her there without another word, one hand slowly rubbing comforting circles into her lower back.

Little by little, she feels the tension leave Agatha's body.

After a few quiet minutes, when Agatha has finally stopped shaking, Rio speaks again.

"...Your mother was a cunt."

Agatha lets out a watery laugh against Rio's neck.

Rio smiles to herself.

Confirmed, Her talent has always been and always will be, cheering up Agatha Harkness.

And somehow...

It feels better than any success she's ever had in her career and life.

"I know this is a risk, Mi Amor."

Rio gently brushes a strand of hair behind Agatha's ear before continuing.

"But I think we can handle it."

She smiles softly into Agatha’s hair.

"I've loved loving you all these years we've been friends, Agatha. Realizing that it wasn't just friendly feelings i have doesn't make me scared. It makes me happy."

She pulls away just a little to see Agatha, she then cups Agatha's face to insure she’s looking her in the eye when she says this.

"I know you. You know me. You know I'd never hurt you.” Rio feels herself getting emotional too.

"And you know I'd never leave you. Hell i felt off this entire week because I didn't get to see you for a few days."

Agatha lets out a tiny, guilty laugh.

"...Yeah. Sorry about that." She wipes at her face. "I kind of had my own realization... I didn't handle it very well."

"I understand."

Rio pulls her back into her chest again as they regulate through all their emotions together.

A quiet minute passes.

"Call me that again."

"What? Mi Amor?"

Agatha nods against Rio's chest.

Rio can't help but let out a soft chuckle.

"Te quiero, Mi Amor."

“…I love you too" Agatha mumbles against Rio's chest.

They slowly pull away just enough to look at each other.

Neither of them says another word.

They don't need to.

They both lean in at the same time.

It isn't clear who closes the final inch between them, only that the moment their lips meet, something inside both of them finally settles.

Like this had always been where they were meant to end up.

The kiss is slow but as passionate and intense as their love.

And as natural as every hug they had over the years, every holiday they spent together because they’re all they had, every phone call after getting good or bad news, every ordinary moment that had quietly been leading them here all along.

Agatha smiles into the kiss, a few tears escaping despite herself.

Rio reaches up one hand and gently cups her cheek, her thumb caressing her soft cheekbones, while her other hand stayed behind Agatha’s back holding her steady like she knew what Agatha needed.

And after years of floating around each other’s orbits, Maybe out of fear, Maybe it was just preparing to accept something they weren’t ready for, But tonight they finally let gravity pull them where they'd always belonged.

Down into each other's arms.
