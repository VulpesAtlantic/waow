A collection of one-shots of Agatha proving how much she loves Rio Vidal without ever saying it.

Chapter 1: The Best Friend

Rio doesn’t look up from her book when Agatha hurls her phone across the room and curses Billy Maximoff in a slew of Latin. Not even the crash of the lampshade the phone flies into causes Death to blink.

“What did your dear pet do this time?” The green witch mutters in a mixture of amusement and annoyance as she casually turns the page of the botany book she was reading.

“He got sick with the flu,” Agatha mutters in disgust.

Rio finally looks up from her book with a raised eyebrow. “How tragic.”

Death hardly cared much for when humans get sick. Especially overly enthusiastic little witch boys who disrupted the sacred balance and pretended to be an Avenger. To be honest she’s surprised her wife cared either, Agatha spent most of her time grumbling about the kid, albeit fondly.

“But he still invited the coven over for tonight!”

“Ah.” Rio nods slowly, suddenly understanding her wife’s outburst better. Agatha did love to pretend to hate the people she cared for the most. Rio would know.

The green witch closes her book and leans forward, biting her lip as if she’s about to drop a bombshell of an idea on her wife. “You could always…cancel?”

Agatha scoffs, “I can’t give Jen the satisfaction.”

Death sours at that. She had never fully gotten over the obvious shared history Jen and her wife had. Agatha said it was nothing, but Rio didn’t like it either way.

“Right, wouldn’t want that,” Rio mutters.

The purple witch softens slightly at the tone, “Rio. We’ve been over this. Nothing happened-

Agatha sighs and crawls across the couch to settle on her wife’s lap. She tilts Rio’s chin up to look into brown eyes for a moment before kissing her deeply, trying to communicate all the things she’d never been good at saying out loud.

Rio accepts the message, tangling her hands in her wife’s hair. When they finally pull apart the look of brooding is gone on Death’s features, replaced with her usual look of mischief.

“Sounds like we have a party to plan.”

Agatha groans but pulls herself away from her wife to stand up. “I’m killing that teen in the morning.”

Death chuckles, “Just tell me when so I can have my dagger sharpened.”

The spirit witch shakes her head but her lips twitch upward in amusement before she can stop it. Rio of course catches this and grins wider at having drawn a tiny smile from her wife. The look of proud accomplishment on the green witch’s face forces Agatha to turn away completely to hide the now matching grin of her own. She knows Rio still sees it, but the green witch knew better than to comment on it. That only makes Agatha smile even more.

Rio glances at the lampshade the purple witch had toppled earlier and repairs it with a snap of her fingers as if broken objects weren’t unusual in their household, because well, they weren’t. She then moves to tidy up the rest of the house and tend to her plants.

Alice was always nice enough to ask about her plants when she came by, and Rio was excited to show her how her Variegated Monstera was doing. Rio had nearly swooned when Agatha had brought it home one day. Sweeping in casually and setting the extremely rare plant down on their dining room table haphazardly in front of the green witch like it was nothing. Apparently the spirit witch had seen the plant in a window on one of her walks and immediately broken in, killed the owner, and stolen the plant. Agatha Harkness was truly Death’s dream girl.

Meanwhile the spirit witch clamors around the kitchen. She never thought she’d like something so domesticated as cooking, she only learned it to blend into Wanda’s hex anyway. Yet it had become something close to a hobby over the years, not that she’d ever admit that, especially to Billy. The happiness on Rio’s face whenever she cooked made it worth it though. Rio made a lot of things worth it.

She’s just pulling the lasagna out of the oven and considering lacing it with poison just to spite Billy for getting sick when the doorbell rings. Guess the poison would have to wait for next time.

“Welcome to your doom!” Rio opens the door with one of her signature maniacal grins.

Jen, Alice, and Lilia give Death an unimpressed look as they stroll into the house, by now used to the green witch’s psychotic exterior.

“Where’s Teen?” Jen asks immediately.

“Dead.” Agatha declares as she waltzes into the room.

“What??” Alice screeches.

The serial killer laughs heartily, “Are you ever not going to fall for that?”

“Ha ha.” Alice mumbles.

“He just texted in the group chat he has the flu,” Jen exclaims, frowning at her phone. “The little shit organized this whole coven get together and waited for us to get here before telling us.”

“Mmm we’re actually plotting his murder tonight,” Agatha smirks.

“We are?” Alice exclaims again.

“Alice, dear, is your last name Wu-Gulliver or Wu-Gullible?” Lilia asks.

The protection witch huffs, “Sorry I’m the only one with genuine morals in this coven!”

“Please, Rio is just as much a rule follower as you.” Agatha waves her hand casually at her wife.

“You take that back, Harkness!” Death pulls out her dagger and tucks it under Agatha’s chin. The purple witch’s eyes sparkle, “Make me, Vidal.”

“Okay! Rio you’re just as much of a freak as your wife, let’s move along shall we?” Jen interjects, eager to avoid the makeout session and/or fight they were two seconds away from witnessing between Death and the serial killer.

“Damn right I am.” The green witch winks at the spirit witch before skipping happily over to show Alice her plants.

Agatha rolls her eyes and heads toward the dining room. Jen and Lilia follow, taking in the meal laid out on the table with raised eyebrows.

“Why did the teenager even want us to meet up today?” Lilia asks.

The purple witch shrugs in annoyance, “Something about coven bonding. Whatever that means. Youth these days.”

Jen laughs, “That boy has made you soft, Harkness.”

“I swear I’ll burn down your shop, Kale.” Agatha growls.

“Promises promises.”

The purple witch huffs as Rio and Alice join them and then sit around the table. It starts off slightly awkward without Billy there to fill in the space, but eventually Rio starts talking about her job and soon they’re all standing around the table arguing about the best way to hide a body.

“Come on, this answer is easy, just burn it! No one would ever find it, unlike if you buried it in the ocean.” Alice looks pointedly at Jen, her previous self-proclaimed morals out the window as she gets roped into the debate.

“Who is finding it in the ocean?” Jen says incredulously.

“Why don’t you just bury it like normal people?” Lilia asks.

“Or you can just leave it lying there to taunt people,” Agatha shrugs.

Death grins, “That’s what I’m talking about sweetheart.”

“Well,” Jen says as she sits down on a recliner as if she owns the place, “What should we do now?”

“Oh let’s play a game!” Alice suggests. “Like…the best friend game!”

“What’s that?” Lilia asks.

“We ask questions and try to guess how each other will answer.”

“Hard pass.” Agatha scoffs.

Rio smiles and flops on the couch beside her wife, “Come on babe, it will be fun.”

The purple witch frowns, “No.”

“Fine we’ll play without you,” Lilia says.

“Whatever.”

Jen goes to get some paper for the game while Alice looks up some questions on her phone. They sit in a circle on the floor while Agatha lies out on the couch, scrolling on her phone and acting disinterested in the game.

“Alright,” Alice says, “I’ll ask a specific person a question about themselves. They write down their answer and everyone else writes down what they think the answer is. Whoever gets it right gets a point.”

“I say Rio goes first,” Jen declares, earning a glare from Agatha on the couch.

The green witch raises her eyebrow, “Sure.”

Alice looks at the first question on her phone, “Oh this starts off too easy. Rio, what’s your favorite color?”

Everyone quickly writes down their answer without hesitation, Rio included. None of the coven notices Agatha smirking on the couch as she continues to scroll through her phone, already knowing the outcome of the round.

“Alright everyone reveal their answers.”

Alice, Jen, and Lilia all hold up their cards with the word green written out.

Rio holds hers up.

“Purple!??” Jen exclaims.

“But you’re the green witch!” Alice insists.

“Agatha’s magic is purple,” Rio shrugs nonchalantly, as if the reason is obvious.

“Oh my god,” Jen rolls her eyes.

Meanwhile Jen is still glaring but Alice pulls the group back into the game before the argument can escalate any further. Rio’s eyes light up, “It’s a dinosaur I knew of back in the day. They never got enough attention.”

“She had one as a pet,” Agatha chimes in from her spot on the couch.

The coven gives the purple witch strange looks.

“What?” The spirit witch says defensively.

“You knew that?” Lilia asks.

Agatha frowns, “Well yeah, Rio rambles about it all the time.”

“I’ve mentioned it like twice,” The green witch comments, secretly pleased her wife had remembered that anecdote from decades prior.

“Same thing.”

“Sure, my love.”

The game continues with Agatha getting more and more involved each round as the coven continues to guess her wife’s answers incorrectly.

“Why would she want to vacation in hell, a cemetery, or a mass burial site?” Agatha scoffs at her coven’s responses to what Rio’s dream vacation would be.

“Uh…because she’s Death?” Alice asks timidly.

Agatha rolls her eyes, “That’s her job. No one wants to vacation at their job.”

Death laughs, “I like my job when Agatha is the one doing the killing. The rest isn’t all that enjoyable.”

“Alright then, where do you think Rio’s dream vacation would be?” Lilia asks.

“Puerto Rico.” The purple witch says without hesitation. “See the gardens. Hike in the forest. Talk to who knows what wildlife. Scare the locals.”

The rest of the coven glance at Death in disbelief, “Surely that can’t be right.”

Rio holds up her answer. Puerto Rico. (Gardens, talk to animals, mess with humans)

“Okay there’s no way Agatha knew that exactly. You two must be cheating!” Jen exclaims.

“I’m not even playing,” The spirit witch rolls her eyes. “You all are just bad guessers.”

“So, it has her name in it. Seems like Rio would appreciate that.”

“A venus flytrap?” Lilia guesses.

“What? That’s not a flower either.” The spirit witch scoffs again.

“I was trying to think outside the box.”

“Those are pretty cool Lilia, but no.” Death shakes her head and goes to reveal her answer but Agatha interjects.

“Wait.” The purple witch begrudgingly drags herself off the couch and picks up a paper. She glances at her wife who tilts her head with amusement and curiosity. The look still makes her stomach flip after all these centuries.

Agatha grabs a pen and scribbles down her answer. She had resisted joining the game at first just to prove her disinterest, but she couldn’t live with everyone blatantly getting Rio’s favorite flower wrong. It was too special to Rio. After all, flowers were a love language practically invented by the original green witch.

She holds up the paper. Lotus.

“Uh, really?” Jen asks.

Rio holds up her paper with a grin, looking adoringly at her wife.

Lotus.

“Oh come on,” Jen huffs.

“Why is your favorite flower a lotus?” Lilia asks.

“I remember the first one I ever created. It felt special to me. And now humans view it as a sign of rebirth which I appreciate. Full circle of life and all that.”

Agatha glances at her wife and remembers the day three centuries ago she had asked Rio this very question. Death had said she liked that the lotus represented rebirth because it meant that maybe she wasn’t the end of everything. That life could persist beyond her. It gave Death hope that maybe one day not everyone would hate her. Turns out, that day had already come. Agatha was already head over heels for Death at that point.

Jen frowns at the deadly couple and conjures up some bottles of wine. “Alright. Now that Agatha’s playing, let’s ask some actually challenging questions. If you guys don’t answer, you drink. If you get it wrong, you drink.”

Agatha rolls her eyes and waves her hand, “Fine. And if we get them right, you all drink.”

“Deal.” Lilia says, grabbing a bottle of wine.

Rio glances at her wife with a smirk, “Feeling confident there, Harkness?”

Agatha rolls her eyes, “Watch me get more answers right than you, Vidal.”

The green witch narrows her eyes at that. “Game on.”

“Wait, Agatha, sit on the other side of the circle across from Rio. Can’t have you guys cheating.” Jen says sharply.

The purple witch gives the potions witch an unimpressed look but begrudgingly moves her seat.

“Alright I have a new list of questions,” Alice says. “Agatha you’re first. Have you ever gotten back together with an ex?”

Jen frowns, “That seems really easy.”

“Let’s just see,” Lilia mutters.

“Alright both of you flip around your cards.”

Rio: No

Agatha: No

The spirit witch rolls her eyes, “Technically, we never explicitly said that to each other.”

Jen huffs as she sips her wine, “Alice, ask a harder question.

“Okay, Rio, who proposed to who and how?”

Rio: Agatha had the ring. But she’ll claim I proposed first even though she was definitely planning it first

Agatha: Rio proposed first but she’ll claim I had the ring first

The coven leader shrugs, “Guess we’re just that good.”

Jen shrugs, “Well they’ve had so many experiences together I figured they’d write down something different.”

“Hmm fair.”

Rio: Beijing, Queen Victoria, Judy Garland,

Agatha: The Beijing incident, Queen Victoria, Judy Garland

“You ruined my potential career as a famous actress.”

You have no idea, Rio thinks as she stares back into blue eyes across the room.

Death brought her flowers.

Death was the most powerful being in the universe.

Death was hot as hell.

Rio had been hesitant to perform the ritual all those years ago, concerned that it would be too much for a mortal to share with Death. Agatha: We won’t make it tonight

Green eyes blaze back at her, and she’s sure any other being would have run and buried themselves by now, but even in this state, she knows Rio won’t hurt her.

The green witch is reluctant to let go, but now that her relief has sunk in, she remembers who she is. Who they are. They weren’t friends, after all. They weren’t…anything.

Except that Agatha was still Rio’s entire world. Her entire world that she thought she had lost today.

“Fucking Thanos,” Rio mutters, shaking her head. “Fucking Peter Quill. They couldn’t stop him.”

“Thanos,” Agatha mutters in disdain. “Why did he even do this?”

“He claimed it was to restore balance from overpopulation. But I think he just wanted to get my attention. The asshole has been harrassing me for years.”

That makes Agatha’s fists clench. How dare that titan try to mess with her wife? Sure, she could fight/flirt with Rio and go on murder sprees for attention all she wants, but that didn’t mean anyone else could. She makes a mental note to kill the purple asshole next time she sees him.

Nicky.

Nicky will be okay because of you.
