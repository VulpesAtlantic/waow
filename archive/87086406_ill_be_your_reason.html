“I’ve never been a Godmother before,” she whispers, as if she’s talking solely to Lily and cannot be heard by her mothers. “But you can bet I’m going to spoil the hell out of you.”

“We can hear you,” Agatha deadpans even though she’s smiling.

“I don’t care,” Wanda teases, not lifting her eyes from the baby in her hold. “You don’t know just how lucky you are. You got the only two people who could ever love you as much as they’re going to. I mean that.”

And, from pure emotion over physical pain, Rio begins to cry.

Their hive, their coven, their family.

Made bigger by one new addition.

“Maybe it’s time we go so everyone here can get some rest. Jen wanted me to check and make sure you had everything you needed. She said she left latching instructions and to call for anything you need before she left.” Wanda steps up to Agatha and makes the transfer back. The moment Lily is safely in her arms again, she places herself by Rio’s hip on the bed and looks to her friend.

“Thank you, Wanda. Everyone’s been amazing, but you…” Agatha glances down at Lily and back up again, a kind smile on her lips.

“You’ve been our lifevest,” Rio finishes, her hand landing on Agatha’s thigh to give it a soft squeeze.

“Yeah, that thing. Keeping us afloat or whatever,” Agatha tacks on, her smile widening to a grin.

“Believe me when I say it’s always my pleasure,” Wanda assures with a nod of her head. “Goodnight, Harknesses Three.”

Rio and Agatha both chuckle softly, careful not to rouse the sleeping baby.

“Goodnight, Wanda,” they voice in sync.

She offers one last wave and disappears to collect Billy and head home for the night. Almost as if she knows, Agatha turns and lays Lily in Rio’s arms so she can climb into her side of the bed. The moment she does, she fits herself to Rio’s side and looks down at their little girl.

“We did that,” she marvels, her voice a disbelieving whisper.

Rio huffs a light chuckle through her nose.

“That we did, my love.”

Agatha’s attention hesitates on Lily a moment longer before she speaks again, still quiet and taken. “I love her already.”

“So do I,” Rio agrees, turning her head to kiss Agatha’s temple. “And I love you, Agatha.”

Agatha’s own head turns and their lips brush in the most tender of kisses.

“I love you too, Rio. Both of you. Forever.”

“Forever.”
