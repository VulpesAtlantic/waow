"Principle Calderu." Alice lies quickly. "Better to just take her input on the curriculum. Rio here has an observed lesson soon."

Ah. This must be Agatha. 

Agatha's focus shifts from Alice to Rio then flicker for a second, almost as if taken aback by something. Her mouth falls open slightly. 

Oh, that's right: Rio's in her chair. 

"Is there a problem?" Rio asks, raising an eyebrow. 

Agatha hesitates, like she's going to say something then changes her mind. Then a little smirk tugs at her lips. 

"Isn't it a little early for observed lessons? Did you come with a bad reputation or something?" 

"Why? Have you heard of me?" Rio fires back.

Agatha's little smirk grows, like she's enjoying messing with her. "Just an assumption. Given that I'm sure Alice already mentioned that's my seat and you're still sitting in it." 

Rio doesn't back down. "This isn't high school. You can't claim ownership of something."

"If you'll look around I think you'll find it actually is high school, and if you ask anyone around here, I certainly can." She says pointedly, her eyes glimmering with amusement. "Find another seat. There's plenty of them-"

"No." Rio says firmly, knowing it's probably the first time the woman's heard the word. Her gaze is fixed on Agatha, daring her to double down. For a second, she sees a flash of something cross her face, but it's gone so quickly she doesn't catch what it was.

Agatha's eyebrows shoot upwards at that, not necessarily in anger, more like surprise. 

"See? That's a bad attitude. I'm beginning to understand why Lilia wants to keep an eye on you." Rio feels her skin prickle under Agatha's stare. She can handle a bully, even an insanely hot one. Her voice drops lower, her eyes narrowing as she sizes her up one more time.  "Maybe I will too."

Agatha doesn't stick around anyway. Clearly accepting defeat, she takes her coffee through to her own classroom, leaving her and Alice alone in the teacher's lounge again.

"Well that was extremely uncomfortable." Alice mutters. 

 


It doesn’t stop being uncomfortable. No matter what she did, Agatha would not stop watching her. In any room she entered, she could feel her gaze on her. On Wednesday, she dared to use the copier over break. Agatha arrived with a stack to copy and stood, leaning on the door frame of the teacher's lounge, staring at her until she was finished. It was a clear attempt to make her uncomfortable enough to cancel the job.

Rio would be lying if she said she wasn't getting a kick out of getting her attention. Every time she felt Agatha enter the room she felt giddy; just knowing that, even if it was negative, even if she was clearly pissed, Agatha's focus was always on her. She even looked up when Agatha's planning periods were just to go into the lounge and hold up the copier. 

By the time Friday rolls around, the place is deserted. She'd learned the day before that a number of kids were out on a field trip to see the local production of A Streetcar Named Desire and all of the Science and English teachers were supervising. It works for her because she has a lot of work to do, given that it's the end of her first week in the place, so it means she doesn't have to try to be sociable. She stays in her classroom for most of the day, assuming she's the only one on the whole floor. She fully prepares to have the lounge all to herself at lunch when she wanders in to make coffee.

"You've been quite the hermit today, Miss Vidal."

Rio jumps a little when she hears a familiar low, sultry voice break through her own internal dialogue, going over all that she has left to do. Agatha sits in her usual seat, thumbing through papers she's grading, not even bothering to look up at her. She sips from a little mug that reads 'English Teachers Get Lit' over a dorky emoji with sunglasses on it.

"I didn't know you were here." 

"Oh, so you would've come in here earlier if you'd known?" That little smirk returns to Agatha's face, her attention remaining on the papers in front of her. "I couldn't help but notice that on the one day you think I'm not here, suddenly you have no copying to do and my seat in the lounge is finally available again."

Rio scoffs. "Did you consider that maybe I haven't been spending my first week here worrying about what might or might not piss you off?"

She absolutely has been doing that. Her entire week had revolved around petulantly working for Agatha's attention, but that's besides the point. So what if she'd been going out of her way to copy shit she didn't really need? It would do this woman some good to see that the world doesn't revolve around her. 

Agatha's brow creases a little and she looks up at her finally. "What? What are you talking about?"

"The fact you terrorise everyone around here but you're not about to do it to me. That you think you can get away with whatever you want because people just roll over for you."

Agatha remains confused. She doesn't say anything for a moment, just blinking at her like she's been completely thrown off by Rio's assessment. Then, Rio watches as her jaw clenches and her face shifts from teasing to actual anger. 

"You've been here for a week and you've got me all figured out, is that it?"

"Well you've been glaring at me all week trying to intimidate me. I'm sure that shit usually works, but I won't let a grown woman bully me-"

"-Bully you?" Agatha laughs incredulously. "Miss Vidal, I don't have to do that when your students will do it for me if you continue to dress like one of them. I'm confident I've seen Noah in the exact same shirt you're wearing now."

Rio can't believe this woman has the fucking audacity. Sure, she's dressed a little casual right now because her clothes haven't arrived yet, but Alice says the dress code isn't really an issue here. Agatha's dressed in expensive-ass, conservative dresses and pantsuits all week and now she's looking down on her for what she's chosen to wear. This uppity, entitled bitch. Rio seethes.

"It's none of your concern how I choose to dress."

"It's just a little professional advice, Hon." Agatha smiles falsely, standing from her seat. She approaches her as she stands at the entryway to the lounge. "Teenagers just tend to listen to people who dress like adults."

She shoves past Rio, bumping her shoulder as she does, and returns to her own classroom. 

 

The rest of Rio's things arrive the next day including all of her work-clothes, but she can't wear any of them now. If she does, it'll look like she's following Agatha's advice, which is the last message she wants to send. So, from here on out, Rio decides to continue to dress in oversized graphic shirts and shorts or jeans. When she gets back in the building on Monday, after a week of stewing over her run-in with Agatha, she quickly grabs Agatha's "English Teachers Get Lit" mug and takes it to her classroom. She alters her lesson plan to include a spit enzyme experiment, returns it to the cabinet, then waits for Agatha's lunchtime coffee to mention it. 

Agatha retaliates by unplugging the refrigerator in Rio's room over the weekend that's holding pig hearts for dissection. There's a heatwave and when Rio returns, her classroom smells so bad she has to teach from the library while the place gets fumigated.

And that's how it starts. Each of them constantly try to one-up the other using methods ranging from menial to extreme. The other teachers learn to try to vacate the lounge if they ever find themselves in the room with them at the same time. 

 

 Present

 

"Were you ever going to mention that you've been into me since we first met?"

The signs were all there. Agatha had purposefully given up her space on that trip to get her alone. Why hadn't she noticed it before? What she can't understand is why, if she was into her, would she destroy her chances by behaving like a petty, entitled bitch?

Agatha sits across from her, face a beautiful shade of red in the light of the sunset. "I haven't. Only briefly." She snaps defensively.

"Yeah? So you arranged to get me alone because-"

"-because I thought maybe you were into me too!" Agatha bursts out. "I misread it, obviously. You were always in the lounge at the same time I was, you were just copying blank sheets of paper at one point,-"

"Yeah! Because Alice told me you pretty much terrorised the staff and wouldn't ever let anyone use the copier-"

"-What?" Agatha scoffs, shaking her head in confusion. "Yeah, I had a class with really high support needs that year and they all needed the work printed in different sizes, fonts and colours. You were in the teachers lounge whenever I was, even during my off-periods, because you thought it was pissing me off?"

Sure, maybe there had been a small part of Rio that had also enjoyed knowing she had Agatha's attention. She's sure she noticed that Agatha was attractive when they first met but that was short lived once they started their war. 

"It wasn't?"

"That's why you accused me of bullying you?" Agatha laughs. 

"Well you were always glaring at me!"

"Rio, let's think for a second, shall we? Do I seem like the type of person to just stand and glare at someone who's in the way of something I want? You've known me for years now. If I was pissed at you, you'd know."

It finally clicks.

"So you weren't glaring at me, you were-"

"-Yes. We've established this now; I was into you." Agatha groans, still seemingly mortified. "I was hoping to make a move when everyone was gone, but you got all..." she gestures wildly with her hands, "...weird. You made all these accusations about me so I just fought back."

Rio laughs loudly in total disbelief. Agatha had been checking her out the whole fucking time.

"You really offered to cover Sharon's class for three months just so you could talk your way into my pants on our lunch break?"

Agatha frowns a little. "What?"

"Here you are!" Sharon interrupts their conversation to quickly place a pitcher of water on the table between them, then heads back into the cabin. The tension dissolves a little and Rio starts to feel guilty for the way she'd reacted that day.

"Well, I'm sorry I jumped to conclusions. In my defence, our entire department was terrified of you. And some kids."

"A little fear is healthy." Agatha winks, her face finally starting to return to a colour resembling her usual.

Rio waits a second, allowing a comfortable silence to fall over the table before she speaks carefully.

"You weren't...totally misreading things, by the way."

Agatha pauses with her drink in her hand. "I wasn't?"

Well Agatha deserved a little honesty, did she not? After all, it's Rio's fault that they've been at each other's throats the whole time.

"I liked knowing I had your attention just a little too much. I actually remember thinking you were the hottest women I'd ever laid eyes on." She flirts, blushing slightly at the admission. The colour that had previously faded, starts to return to Agatha's cheeks again and Rio can feel knots tighten in her stomach as she watches it spread. Agatha looks dazed for a few seconds then clears her throat.

"So...is this it? Is this the end?"

Rio's face twists in confusion. "The end of what?"

"The games? The fighting? All of it?"

Rio thinks for a second. She really doesn't hate Agatha anymore. Truthfully, she's really starting to enjoy her company. 

"Nah. I think maybe I wont be so harsh about it, but I don't think I could stop tormenting you if I tried." Rio smirks so that Agatha knows she's not serious. "At least now you'll know how to take your anger out on me."

Agatha doesn't laugh, she just watches her curiously. "You want to continue this thing? When we get home?"

Shit. That's what Rio just implied, wasn't it?

"Maybe." She shrugs, trying to act casual about it. "Would...you want to?"

Agatha smirks. "Yeah. I would."

Rio feels the knots in her stomach intensify and it worries her that it's not necessarily arousal she's feeling

"It won't complicate things at work?" 

Agatha laughs, looking at her like she's said something insane.

"Complicate things? Things already were complicated. If anything I think it would improve things at work."

She's right. Since they started this whole thing, the need to piss Agatha off has dramatically decreased. As much as she hates to admit it, their therapist had been oddly correct in assigning them that 'homework'. The tension they'd once mistaken for abject hatred may have actually been something else entirely to begin with, and now they've finally addressed it, they can actually tolerate one another for once.

She raises her glass again in a toast, smirking at Agatha across the table. 

"Well, then here's to cross-departmental relationship improvement."

Agatha laughs which makes Rio's stomach does that traitorous, weird thing again and she ignores it. Their glasses meet.

"For the good of WestView High."
