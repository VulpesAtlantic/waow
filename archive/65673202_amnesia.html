Little did she know, Rio being unable to remember who she was would break her more than anything else ever could…

“..Agatha, Rio’s been in an accident..”

 

“..She was dodging a tractor trailer..” 

 

“..Off the road..”

 

“..Hospital..”

 

Lilia’s words were rattling Agatha’s nerves, creating a burning fire that exploded adrenaline through her. The only thing that screamed above them all was a single word. One name that reigned high among the rest. 

 

Rio. 

 

The one woman Agatha thought about from every waking moment until she fell asleep. Not even in her slumber could she fully escape the face she fell so deeply in love with years ago. Her closest friend since they were in their early twenties. Rio was twenty, Agatha twenty three when their mutual friend- Jennifer Kale introduced them to one another. 

 

Agatha would never forget the night she met her. Rio had busted her face on the pavement right before Agatha showed up. She rode her motorcycle to Jen’s, (The bike Agatha learned quick was a staple to her personality) flying down the road with no wits or helmet. Her recklessness had resulted in a spin out, being tossed off her bike in a ditch. 

 

“How you walked away from this with a possible concussion and scrapes is pure luck, Rio” Jen had said. 

 

“When will you stop being ruthless?” Lilia asked. 

 

“Speed limits are meant to be broken” Alice shrugged while the other two smacked her for the encouraging words. 

 

While Agatha? She felt her stomach turn and not in a bad way. 

 

If anything, finding the woman leaning against the kitchen counter with a frozen bag of peas to her face was amusing. The dark leather jacket tossed aside. She was dressed in a tight white t-shirt (with a few dirt patches and streaks), tucked into black jeans, with black hair broken out into a wild frizz. Specks of gravel were tangled in, dirt and dried blood marks patched through covered her from head to toe.

 

The woman was a mess, disaster, every word in the book to describe someone after such a brutal fall. One usually would be concerned, ask about their well being. 

 

But Agatha? She could tell you word for word what her mouth chose to say. 

 

“Is this a normal look for you?” Agatha asked. 

 

The beaten woman looked up, lips puffed and busted with cuts. Dirt shadowed her face, red marks outlined like designs on her skin. 

 

Through it all, she offered a grin that showed her left tooth missing right beside her two front ones. The dripping blood from the gum clear she had just lost the tooth from her fall. 

 

Agatha found it intriguing how the woman didn’t seem to care. Maybe she didn’t realize her tooth was missing quite yet, but then again- That was something others would feel embarrassed over, but this woman? She didn’t appear to give a good god damn. 

 

“Why? Does it look like a good time?” She asked. 

 

Agatha smirked, a little tilt of her head as she rasped, “Oh, just dandy” 

 

The woman smiled, holding a hand out while the other held the frozen bag against her face. 

 

“Sup’..I’m Rio”

 

She didn’t hesitate to shake her hand, smirking a bit stronger with the little question that followed. 

 

“Not skid marks?”

 

The beaten biker chick named Rio didn’t seem phased. She gasped, smiling wider as she beamed brighter than her wounds ever could. 

 

“Givin’ me a cool nickname already? What an honor”

 

Agatha rolled her eyes, her smile never fading for a second. She stood taller, hand leaning on the kitchen island while the other was placed firmly on her hip. 

 

“Names Agatha. Agatha Harkness” She introduced. 

 

“Well, Agatha- Agatha Harkness” She echoed. “Lovely to meet you”

 

“Ditto, skid marks” Agatha smirked.

 

Agatha smiled softly, trying to resist the way her cheeks burned. However, it felt impossible to do so. Not when the woman before her was intriguing, beautiful- The biker looked sexier unlike anything Agatha had ever seen before.

 

‘Who knew I had a thing for bikers…huh, the more you know' Agatha thought to herself. 

 

Rio laughed, shaking her head as she sighed. “You’re going to give me a run for my money. Huh?” 

 

“Maybe so..” Agatha shrugged. “See how much you’ll be able to take it”

 

Rio scoffed. “Bring it on, Harkness”

 

Back then, Agatha would have never guessed ‘Biker Rio’ would go from something she adored and lusted over, to one she truly hated. 

 

Agatha's fears exploded completely when they moved in together to save on rent (So they said, but all their friends knew the two had become inseparable). She had been home Friday night. Pizza box, sodas and beers waiting for Rio to join. It was a ritual they had. Friday's were designated for movie or reality TV show nights. 

 

But when Rio never showed? Agatha knew something wasn't right. 

 

She remembered the second her world stopped, turned upside down and threatened to kill her on the spot. The only other time that mirrored the same moment she found herself in. 

 

“Rio’s motorcycle is totaled. A car smacked from behind her and-”

 

Those were the only words Agatha could remember. She dropped everything, ditched the cab when it took too long to get to the hospital. Most likely overpaid him as well for the short trip. 

 

On foot, she ran through the sidewalks and parking lots- Nearly passed out from the summer heat when she stumbled through the doors, slumping onto the receptionist’s desk. 

 

Pure panic, adrenaline, the fear of what she’d find and if Rio was taken from her took control. Agatha never knew she could feel such terror in her life. 

 

She remembered laying eyes on her, Rio’s leg wrapped in a cast with IV’s and severe road rashes and cuts along her body. 

 

“You fucking, idiot” Agatha whispered, soothing a hand over her forehead. “I..Rio, you gotta’ stop riding”

 

Rio weakly laughed, shaking her head- wincing the second she did so. 

 

“That's skid marks to you” She choked out.

 

She wore a lazy grin, Agatha not giving her a glimpse of amusement. Her smile faded after noticing Agatha’s smile wouldn’t even grow, feeling guilt swallow her whole.

 

Rio sighed, whispering a small, “Ag-”

 

“Don’t Agatha me, not now” Agatha said. “Your bike is totaled, Rio..don’t you think this is a sign to let it go?”

 

Agatha didn’t have it in her to lecture Rio till she was home the next day. Rio had become an emotional wreck over the news of her bike being totaled. 

 

However, she didn’t escape the lecture the next day or the years to follow. 

 

When Rio brought her new bike home, Agatha didn’t speak to her for an entire weekend. 


When Rio went out for her first weekend trip to ride across the state, Agatha ignored her for an entire week. The lecture ended up being nearly three hours long that time. 

 

Eventually, Agatha ignored when Rio’s engine would rev up outside. She avoided acknowledging her worst fear, labeling it as nothing. 

 

Of course, their friends knew otherwise. 

 

Honestly? Anyone who laid eyes on them would know otherwise. 

 

“A friend wouldn’t worry this much. Are you sure there isn’t more to-”

 

“Shut it, Jennifer” Agatha cut her off. “I don’t like Rio like that. Sorry I’m worried about my friend”

 

“Would you worry this much about me?”

 

“Wouldn’t have to. You’re too scared to even get on a damn airplane” Agatha snickered. 

 

Jen shook her head. “That’s not my point here and we both know-”

 

“Drop it” Agatha said, glaring right through her soul. “It’s not like that”

 

Thing is, it was always like that. 

 

Agatha knew deep down she fell in love with Rio. She was doomed after their first night at the apartment. 

 

Agatha hated sleeping in new places. Her mother and her moved countless times and yet, Agatha never grew accustomed to being in new environments. Of course, she had never said anything about it. 

 

But..Rio being Rio? She knew. 

 

Somehow with Agatha, she always knew. 

 

“Pizza, wings, snacks, drinks- Ice cream is in the fridge and your choice of movie- Or! A classic Law and Order SVU binge?”

 

Agatha dropped the last box, a hand running through the air while the other rested on her hip. She took heavy breaths, steadying into a regular pattern. The work out from the day of moving furniture and boxes up the stairs was busying taking its toll. 

 

She looked over the coffee table, food and drinks scattered across. The couch was behind, TV set up on the floor with the playstation connected on the other side.

 

“What is this?” She asked. 

 

“Just what I said” Rio shrugged. “Can’t just go sleep in separate empty bedrooms- Well, boxes decorate them, but still. I hate new places, don’t you?”

 

In that moment, shoulder to shoulder on the couch with Rio that night- Agatha figured out she was doomed. 

 

She knew Rio well enough to know new places didn’t phase her. The woman wouldn’t blink an eye sleeping alone. And yet here she was, somehow knowing the perfect way to distract Agatha and make her feel less alone. 

 

From then on, Agatha forced herself to lie. She did everything to shove her feelings down on the inside- desperate to never let them see the light of day. A small piece of her knew their friends saw right through it, but…denial is a river in Egypt, as they say. 

 

Besides, Rio never felt the same. She wasn’t a woman to settle down either. 

 

A string of flings, collective of friends with benefits, one night stands- Rio was known for them. The ‘Dyke chick’ Alice nicknamed her own spin on, ‘Fuck boy’ . 

 

It described Rio well and-

 

Agatha hated it. So much so, she ended up moving out three years ago. 

 

Fresh at the age of thirty, Agatha took a job offer in California. A marketing manager for a fashion company, something Agatha never saw herself doing in life, but the pay rate was decent and also-

 

She lived far, far, away from Rio. Across the country, to be exact. 

 

The night she left, Rio didn’t show up at her goodbye dinner. She chose to go on a date with some chick off Tinder- Agatha had found out later that year. 

 

Agatha's new life, job, the distance between them put a strain on their friendship. At first, Agatha was pretty broken about it. But..when she saw on Facebook, Rio's status went from single to in a relationship …

 

Agatha knew she dodged the bullet. 

 

The idea of having to move out for Rio’s girlfriend to move in or just living in the same area as them both. She shivered, cringed, grew sick over the thought. 

 

Plus, Rio being with someone else? Agatha tried to not think about that part of it.

 

Fast forward three years, Agatha barely traveled home. She kept Lilia's old lake house to use as a vacation spot. A house that wasn't even thirty minutes from her old apartment with Rio. They used to spend endless days there, both would house sit for Lilia when needed. So as much of a relief it was to be away from Rio- 

 

Agatha still felt her home was where her friends were. Not that she'd ever say that outloud. 

 

She didn’t have family, people she could tolerate. But when it came to Alice, Jen and Lilia..Agatha knew she’d always feel safe with them. 

 

Her and Rio occasionally spoke. Mostly Instagram reels sent between one another at random times. Otherwise, they only spoke during the holidays or close to birthdays to plan what to get- Inquire information for certain parties. 

 

And, to wish each other a happy birthday…well, normally. 

 

Just that year, Agatha had broken their tradition. 

 

There was so much resentment and frustration for how she still loved Rio. Any woman she kissed morphed into Rio, any date she went on- Agatha managed to bring Rio up more than once. All of it felt like a curse, pure hell brought on by Satan herself. 


In an attack of retaliation, Rio took the hit and Agatha never wished her a happy birthday. 

 

About two weeks later, Agatha had called Rio to discuss Alice’s birthday- completely ignoring the other woman's. She never bothered to ask how her day went or apologize for not saying a word. 

 

And that put the nail in the coffin, burying their broken friendship for good. 

 

“You could have said something, anything” Rio said. “You didn’t say shit, Agatha..”

 

“I didn’t think you cared about your birthday that much,” 

 

Agatha lied, a little laugh to disguise what she truly knew. She had to hide the truth. Rio needed to feel as if Agatha didn’t care or think about her like that anymore. 

 

“Anyway. When is Alice’s-”

 

“Do you even hear yourself?” Rio asked.

 

Agatha froze in the middle of the chip aisle, fidgeting with the bag in her hand- listening to the wrapper crinkling. It wasn’t enough to drown out the knowing tone in Rio’s voice. She hated hearing the pain, confusion, without a doubt wondering what she had done wrong to have Agatha treat her this way.

 

But, Agatha was saving Rio and herself from the inevitable pain of being irrevocably in love with your best friend. Especially when it wasn’t the same on the other end.

 

Agatha swallowed the lump in her throat, eyes closed as she searched for some witty response- A defense mechanism to hide herself.  

 

“Uh, well- My ears do work when I want them to. But, maybe the store’s service is shitty or your end is-”

 

“Fuck you, Agatha” Rio scoffed. “Really, just…if you didn’t even want to be friends anymore- You should have said something”

 

She could see it. Agatha could envision Rio clear as day. Her fists tightening, jaw clenched as her pupils dilated into puppy eyes anytime she felt on the verge of tears. 

 

Agatha knew this would be the last time they’d ever talk one on one. No more texts, calls, birthday wishes or small talk during holiday parties. They’d be cordial, forced smiles, appear to others as strangers who never once lived under the same roof. 

 

“Goodbye, Agatha” 

 

That small fight was the last time they ever talked. Two weeks ago, fourteen days on the dot- Agatha never knew that would be worse than slowly losing Rio day by day. 

 

She never knew either the next phone call about Rio wouldn't be from her…and it wouldn't be to curse her out for being an asshole even more. 

 

The second Lilia told her the hospital Rio was being brought to- She ran out of her office, barely telling her assistant where she was heading. The young man knew Agatha well enough to not argue and figure out what to do on his own. 

 

Somehow in her frantic state of mind, Agatha had managed to book a flight- Not bothering to head home and pack a bag. She went straight to the airport with the clothes on her back and the purse on her arm. 

 

Nothing else mattered to her. 

 

Agatha felt like she spent an eternity traveling, panicking, trying to not combust into a million pieces of raw emotion. Though finally, she was able to walk through the ER’s automatic doors. 

 

She looked both ways, searching through the scattered staff and people waiting for their own loved ones to find Lilia's wild hair in the crowd. 

 

Agatha didn't bother apologizing to the ones she shoved with her shoulder or nearly knocked them down on the floor. Too busy feeling her doom grow closer and closer. 

 

What if she's already gone? 

Can life support be a thing already? 

How bad is it? 

Do they know about our fight? Will they want me to leave? 

 

“Where is she?” Agatha asked. 

 

The three snapped their heads over in sync. Jen, Alice, and Lilia all dumbfounded by the sight before them. 

 

“Holy shit” Alice whispered under her breath, but Agatha didn't bother to listen. 

 

Her eyes were set on Lilia, panting for oxygen that ceased to exist. When the woman took too many seconds to speak, Agatha's voice darkened- On the verge of explosion. 

 

“Lilia..where. Is. Sh-”

 

“They just got her back in a room” Lilia started. “CT Scan is done, a few stitches, they’re just doing further evaluation right n-”

 

“Which room?” 

 

Agatha stormed for the doors, barely looking over her shoulder. She didn’t listen as Lilia said no visitors yet or how Jen muttered under her breath a low,

 

“Here we go..”

 

A staff member walked in front of Agatha with her hand up, telling her no visitors past the entry way until escorted. Luckily for her, Lilia was already wrapping arms from behind as Agatha was spitting out the words,

 

“Fuck you! Rio needs me, you piece of shi-”

 

Alice snickered. “Think it may be the reverse..” 

 

“Agatha!” 

 

Lilia pulled her back, giving an apologetic smile to the young terrified woman. When she spun them around, Lilia leaned close against her to roughly whisper,

 

“Keep it up and you’ll be kicked out before seeing her”

 

Agatha hated how Lilia was right, slithering out of her hold to cross her arms against her chest. She stomped back before spinning on her heel. 

 

The glare she sent Lilia could be described as personal. As if Lilia had caused the accident herself. 

 

Luckily, Lilia knew to not take offense. 

 

The woman simply took a breath, not pressing Agatha to sit or calm down. 

 

Deja vu. 

 

“Tractor trailer lost its load on the highway. Rio was in the fast lane, about to pass him-”

 

“Let me guess,” Agatha laughed, but it wasn’t humorous. Not in the slightest. “She was on that fucking bike”

 

Lilia took in a deep breath, looking to the others which only confirmed Agatha’s worst fear. She belted out a laugh, arms tossed in the air as she paced in a circle. 

 

“Of course!” She raised her voice, earning glares around the waiting room. “Because why would she not learn her lesson before! Sureee, Rio! Sure thing! Keep getting on that fuckin’ bike and we will keep winding up here! Such mother fuckin’, cock suckin’, shit-”

 

“Agatha, there are children here!” Jen said. 

 

“Fuck those kids and fuck Rio too!” 

 

The three mouthed apologies to the kids with wide eyes, the mother glaring harshly at Agatha. Though, Agatha paid no mind. 

 

She turned to her friends, eyes of her own beginning to gloss over with a wall of tears. Her smile was crazed, threatening. A manic woman on the verge of going off the deep end.

 

“Isn’t this grand?! I love how no one joined me to get rid of her fucking bike and-”

 

“Hey, fuck you!” Jen spat. 

 

Alice mumbled under her breath right along “So much for the children..” 

 

“No way you’re blaming us for this. Not a chan-” Jen said. 

 

“Well, you sure didn’t help me and we ended up here. Didn’t we?!”

 

“Oh, Aga-”

 

“Come on, that isn’t fair” Alice cut Jen off. “You know we love and care for Rio just as you-”

 

“No, not like I do” Agatha said, voice wavering.

 

Jen tilted her head, kicking a leg over the other as she sat back with her arms crossed. A taunting grin that sent a warning she wasn’t willing to be friendly. Even if she knew to not engage or rile up an already enraged Agatha. 

 

“Oh, so that’s why you didn’t wish her a happy birthday? Because you care oh, so much!”

 

Agatha took in a deep breath, nostrils flared as she imagined strangling Jennifer right then and there. 

 

Jen shot up to her feet, ready to fight and defend her own end. Luckily before anything could happen, Lilia put her hand up as Alice stood to make sure things didn’t escalate.

 

“Both of you, knock it off!” Lilia exclaimed.

 

Agatha laughed. “Lilia, I’m not-”

 

“She’s being ridiculous!” Jen said. 

 

Lilia clapped her hands, pointing to the three as she raised her own voice, 

 

“The three of you! Sit down and shut your mouths before I stitch them all together!”

 

Like a spell upon the trio, they dropped in sync. Alice and Jennifer on one side, Agatha across from them both. 

 

The one sunk in her seat most as she whispered, 

 

“I wasn’t even doing anything”

 

“Oh shut it, Alice” Lilia rolled her eyes before continuing. “Fighting, arguing, none of it will get us anywhere but kicked out on the curb and Rio does not need that. Understood?”

 

Alice nodded, but Jen and Agatha kept glaring at one another until Lilia cleared her throat with a stronger, 

 

“Am I understoo-”

 

“Yes!” Both groaned as if they were children who were caught in trouble, about to be grounded or sent to time out. 

 

“Thank you,” Lilia sighed. “Now..as I was saying,” She turned to Agatha. “Rio was about to pass him when the bike went into the ditch, she flew off”

 

Agatha cringed, closed her eyes as she held her breath. The image in her mind felt sickening. Rio mid air, the chaos of traffic surrounding her, how her bike tossed her off like a springboard that didn’t care if she lived or died. 

 

She shook her head, flicking her wrist as her fingers gestured to the ceiling. 

 

“I’m going in there”

 

“Not. Yet.” Lilia said, staring her down before she could push to her feet. “Agatha, I’m going first. You can’t handle if it’s bad news”

 

Agatha scoffed, looking at her as if she had five heads. A crazy woman who had no idea what she was talking about. When in reality, she spoke nothing but the truth. 

 

“I can’t handle the fucking unknown either!” She exclaimed. “Besides, she needs me-”

 

“She needs someone calm over one who takes joy in scaring the poor young woman doing her job just now” Lilia argued. 

 

Agatha slumped a bit in her chair, voice muffled behind her fingers as she mumbled, 

 

“Notmyfaultshesanidiot”

 

Jen scoffed. “She was doing her job-”

 

“By keeping me away from Rio?!” Agatha asked. “She’s an idiot-”

 

“And you are by leaving and not wishing her a happy birth-”

 

“What did I just say?!” Lilia asked. 

 

The group were about to explode amongst each other in another argument when a man’s voice cut through, ending things before they could begin. 

 

Never thought I’d be so happy to see a man.. Alice thought to herself. 

 

“I’m looking for Ms Caldreu?” 

 

Lilia whipped around, the other three jumped up on their feet behind her. 

 

“That’s me- How is she?” Lilia asked. 

 

He held his hand out for Lilia to shake, introducing who he was before quickly adding, 

 

“She’s in recovery. Rio is a very lucky woman. Few stitches on her forehead and chin. Otherwi-”

 

“Oh, thank heavens” Lilia sighed, a hand over her heart, not bothering to listen to the rest. “Can I go see-”

 

Agatha jumped to her feet, pointing at him like her hand was her own personal pistol. “If you fucking let her go before me, buddy. Those balls of yours will be shoved so hard up your-”

 

“Agatha!” The three yelled, sending hard glares her way. 

 

The oldest woman shook her head, returning her attention to the doctor who looked terrified of Agatha’s rage. 

 

“Can you please just..I’d like to see her before them. Just to assure her we are here and see if she’s up to all of us”

 

He (Unsurprisingly) quickly agreed, saying a low,

 

“That is probably for the best right now. One by one will be good for her. You can follow me, Ms Caldreu”

 

Lilia nodded, looking behind her as she started to walk away, 

 

“I’ll be back as soon as I can..” She assured.

 

Her eyes were on Alice, a silent exchange to keep the duo from killing each other. Plus, keep Agatha in the waiting room- That was more of a challenge than the other. Especially with Jen not holding a filter in front of her words. 

 

“I’m sure it’s fine” Alice said. “More than one person may overwhel-”

 

“Unless it’s something worse” Jen worried. 

 

Alice shook her head, determined to keep the energy as calm as humanly possible. 

 

“No. He would have said something here to all of us”

 

“Would he?” Jen asked with a laugh. “Ms dramatic over here just threatened his ballsack and we know where she was going to shove it”

 

Agatha scoffed. “He doesn’t have balls for caving into Lilia’s stupid idea”

 

“Do you even hear yourself? It’s no wonder Lilia is hesitant to have you around Rio right now”

 

“Bullshit,” Agatha snarled. “I am the best thing for her. I am the one who took care of her the last time we were here. I am-”

 

“The one who moved away, cut nearly all contact with her and then! Didn’t wish her a happy birthday!”

 

Jen shook her head, not letting Agatha breathe as she followed up with another part of her lecture. 

 

“Why the hell you’re sitting here demanding to be the only one seeing her when you lack knowledge on the damage you left, the person she has been without you-”

 

“Jen..” Alice whispered. 

 

A soft warning, half quiet. Alice didn’t like the look on Agatha’s face or the new energy between them all. 

 

Sure, it was never a relaxed energy when their closest friend was in the hospital, but there was something different- worse. Jen’s lecture weighed heavier than the previous ones and Alice wasn't sure why, but.. her attempt was short lived

 

To no avail, Jen continued on.

 

“You are a fucking asshole, Agatha. A selfish asshole who can not see why we are hesitant to let you in that room with her and-”

 

“Stop!” 

 

Agatha nearly yelled, not caring for the others in the room that would send yet another collective bunch of glares. 

 

Her voice was trembling, body curling into itself as she looked away from them both. Alice was the one to notice two tears streaming down her face. She placed her hand over Jen’s arm, mouthing a silent, 

 

“Stop. She’s crying”

 

Shit. 

 

Jen’s clipped demeanor changed. She loosened her tense posture, eyes that were burning with rage settled into worry. 

 

It wasn’t uncommon for Jen to lay into Agatha more than the rest. That was their dynamic. The two who were tough and able to take tough love, attitudes that others found frightening. 

 

However, it never had led them to… this. Agatha hadn’t shown such emotions in front of them. Especially ones pulled out from such a brutal confrontation. 

 

“Agatha, I didn’t mean-”

 

“Nope” Alice cut Jen off. 

 

She knew her wife meant well, but there was a gut feeling to leave Agatha alone. 

 

The two went back and forth while Agatha sank in her own mind. Truly, drowned at the rate she was sinking. She kept a hand over her mouth, knee bouncing at a rapid speed. Their words were muffled, sounds of the emergency room dull- nonexistent. 

 

Jen was right. Agatha had left Rio in the dust, tossed away so many years that felt like a lifetime together. All because she couldn’t handle her own emotions like an adult. 

 

To be fair though, how does one handle falling in love with your best friend? Was there a manual for that? And where could Agatha purchase such a thing?

 

“Goodbye, Agatha”

 

Agatha closed her eyes, hearing the two words like a broken record- a CD skipping the same part of the song. Except, it wasn’t a happy song nor a good one. A song that felt life altering in the worst of ways. 

 

In the store when the phone line went dead, Rio hung up after her goodbye- Agatha abandoned her cart of groceries in the middle of the aisle. She didn’t look back, rushing back to her apartment to crawl under bed sheets- hide away from society until she was forced to rejoin. 

 

Two weeks ago, Agatha thought that was the worst thing to happen. Rio saying goodbye and knowing their friendship would never be the same. What Agatha never thought of or planned for was Rio saying that goodbye…

 

And then could fucking die. 

 

Agatha thought, feeling sick over the idea of losing Rio completely. 

 

She could stomach not being friends.

She could stomach watching Rio find love, happiness without her in life.

She could stomach living with Rio hating her for eternity. 

 

But, Agatha couldn’t survive Rio dying. 

 

Rio’s physical being, energetic, sarcastic, annoying self not being in the same world as her? Agatha having to go on knowing birthdays, holidays, social media would be vacant of Rio?

 

“Agatha” Alice said. 

 

“What?” Agatha snapped, sending her a death glare. 

 

Alice held her phone up. “Lilia texted. She’s okay”

 

Agatha’s glare faded. She felt her muscles relax, melt into the chair just a bit. Her lungs fully inflated, exhaling slowly, releasing a heavy weight Agatha didn’t realize she’d been holding onto. 

 

Too many emotions, feelings, everything in her had kicked into overdrive to the point she became numb. Just a ball of fire burning with anxiety that felt impossible to put out. 

 

She’s okay. 

 

Those two words felt far easier than Rio’s final two to her. The doctor had said that, but Lilia saying it as she had eyes on her held an entirely different meaning.
