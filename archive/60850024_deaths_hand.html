Summary

In classic Agatha Harkness fashion, the spirit witch denies her feelings and tells Rio she wants a divorce. She never thought the green witch would go through with it though. Now Agatha has to win the love of her life back with a little help from people she refuses to call her friends.

You'd Rather Fall Apart

“Do you think Tommy is okay? What if he’s out on the streets right now!”

Agatha sighs as Billy freaks out yet again over his long lost twin who they were having an annoyingly difficult time finding. “I’m sure he’s fine.”

“You don’t know that! What if I doomed him to a miserable life?” Billy says anxiously.

The purple witch takes a deep breath and tries to look as comforting as possible. “Billy, we’ll figure it out. Hell, we found a way to bring me back!” Agatha gestures at her physical form which they had manifested again using ancient spells they found.

“That only worked because Rio made an exception for you!” Billy shoots back easily, too caught up in the book he was pouring over to notice how his mentor reacted to the words.

Agatha flinches at the mention of the green witch and absently runs her hand over the white streak in her hair, the only evidence that she had ever been a ghost. It was true, Death had made another exception for her, though it was hardly the same as with Nicky, she knew. Ghosts lived by different rules than those in the afterlife. Rio had just let her go when she came back in a physical body, saying Agatha’s soul hadn’t even technically died anyway. She could tell Rio hadn’t been happy about the whole stunt though. She hadn’t seen the woman since.

The purple witch sighs and spreads out in her chair, trying to push away thoughts about her ex. Agatha had spent centuries being mad at Rio, and now that she was finally somewhat over it, Rio was mad at her. Figures.

Billy thinks for a second then glances over at his mentor. “Do you think she’d know where he is?”

“No.” Agatha says far too quickly. It’s a lie. Billy can tell. Of course Death would know where the soul of a reincarnated boy would be.

Agatha reads the look on the boy’s face and scrambles to her feet. “Don’t even think about it.”

“Why?” Billy shoots back.

“Because she’ll want to steal his soul just like you!” Agatha is pleased she actually has a good excuse not to ask Rio for help.

“Well maybe you can sacrifice yourself for him again. Seemed like you enjoyed yourself the last time.”

The mentor narrows her eyes as her mentee but the teen doesn’t budge. She can tell the boy is desperate enough to try anything to find his twin at this point, and nothing she said was going to stop him.

“Damn Maximoffs.” Agatha curses under her breath. Billy can’t help but grin, knowing that was as close as he was going to get to permission from his mentor. Agatha points a finger at him. “But don’t think for a second I’m going to sacrifice myself for him.”

“Sure Agatha. That kiss of Death seemed pretty awful.” Billy grins.

The purple witch’s eyes flash as she fights back a blush on her cheeks. She allows purple magic to swirl around her. “Careful. Pet.”

Teen rolls his eyes at the threat but decides not to push his luck further and picks up the book he needs to summon Death. A few minutes later there’s a knock on the door and Agatha resists the urge to groan. She smooths out her clothes and hair, trying to prepare herself to see the green witch again, but when she opens the door she’s surprised to find Jen, Lilia, Alice, and Sharon stroll in.

“Uh, can I help you?” Agatha asks snarkily as her coven takes over her house unannounced.

Sharon is the only one to respond. “Oh Billy said you needed help! And I figured, why not? It’s not like anything went wrong the last time!”

Agatha pauses at the clear bite in the mostly docile woman’s words. “Look, Mrs. Hart-”

“Mrs. Davis.”

“Sure. Look, I know we’ve had a rough patch recently, but come on, we’ve been neighbors for years!”

“Mostly you traumatizing us.” Sharon mumbles.

“Besides. I wasn’t trying to kill you.” Agatha goes on, not listening to Sharon’s words. “I was trying to kill the rest of the group! They just insisted I bring you along!”

Alice and Lilia both just roll their eyes, used to Agatha’s dramatics at this point. It’s only Jen that continues the fight. “Uh, excuse me. You were conning us the whole time! I never would’ve said we needed a green witch if I had known the Road wasn’t even real!”

“You’re the one that fell for the con.” Agatha shrugs.

“You’re the one that put it in the Ballad!’ Jen shoots back.

“Why did you put it in the Ballad?” Billy pipes up. “Earth magic. It’s so rare, I’m sure you didn’t find a lot of candidates.”

The coven leader narrows her eyes at the boy. She saw where this was going. She feels the rest of the coven looking at her too. “What? It just fit with the others. Water, fire, earth, and air.”

Alice raises an eyebrow, “Darkest hour, wake that power. Earthly and divine.”

Agatha scoffs, “I fail to see the point-”

“I hold Death’s hand in mine?” Lilia chimes in. “Where did that line come from?”

“Yes, where did that come from?” A voice comes from the shadows.

Agatha closes her eyes in annoyance and grits her teeth as the one and only green witch steps into the room, twirling her dagger through her fingers. Death looks at Agatha expectantly, and the spirit witch wants to smack the smirk off the woman’s face.

Rio’s eyes flash and she holds her dagger out to the purple witch like a microphone. “Well?”

Agatha shoves Death’s hand away and walks to the other side of the room, trying to get away from the magnetic woman. “It’s called imagery, people! You all just aren’t creative enough to understand.”

The coven all give each other knowing looks. Agatha hates it. She hates that she now had a group of people that somewhat understood her. She hates even more that the one person that understood her even more than somewhat was now staring at her dangerously. She supposes she should have been happy that perhaps Rio had gotten over her anger, but she really wasn’t in the mood to confront her feelings at the moment.

Agatha grabs Billy by the arm and shoves him forward. “Don’t you have something to ask?”

Teen glances around then waves nervously at Rio. The green witch looks at him knowingly. “Don’t ask me kid, I can’t tell you.”

Billy frowns and then glances at his mentor for support. Agatha is busy pretending a spoon on the table was suddenly far more interesting than talking with the green witch on the other side of the room. Only when she feels everyone staring at her does she finally look up and shrug. “If she says she can’t tell you then she can’t tell you.”

Billy glares, “So that’s it? I summoned everyone here for nothing?”

“Oh thank god!” Sharon breathes a sigh of relief with a hand on her chest.

The other witches roll their eyes and move to leave but Billy interjects. “No! I can’t accept that! I’ve been searching for him for months!”

Rio sighs, “Look kid, you don’t even want me to tell you. If you find him, I’m going to have to follow and take him.”

“Agatha said she’d sacrifice herself again for him.” Billy looks coolly at his mentor.

The green witch smirks, “Did she now?”

Agatha glares at her protégé, “I said no such thing. In fact, I said the exact opposite!”

“Oh come on babe, the last time wasn’t so bad, was it?” Rio’s face is full of smugness at this point.

“I’d rather kiss a tarantula.” The purple witch shoots back, desperately trying to hide the flush coming to her cheeks.

The earth witch’s eyes narrow in annoyance, “I can arrange that.”

“Wait, if I sacrifice myself I get to kiss Death? Maybe I’ll go in Tommy’s place then.” Jen says with a sneaky grin on her face, knowing exactly the reaction her comment would get.

Alice covers her laugh behind her hand. Lilia winces in preparation for what’s to come. Sharon just looks completely confused.

Rio looks over at Jen with a smirk, pretending to consider the proposition. “Yeah? Maybe I can take you instead.”

“Why are you here!” Agatha interjects, her words echoing loud in the room. You didn’t have to be a witch to see the clear annoyance and jealousy on her face, not that she’d ever admit it.

The green witch looks back at the spirit witch, pleased to see the rise she had gotten out of her. “The kid summoned me.”

“And you have no answers for him!”

“That’s not my fault! The laws of-”

“Magic I know!” Agatha intejects. “You know, for an all-powerful being, you are the single most unhelpful person I’ve ever met!”

“Then why did you marry me?!” Rio snaps back, not liking how this conversation had steered too close to her job again.

“Woah, wait, you two were married?” Alice asks.

Neither woman seemed to hear the question. Instead Agatha just fires back. “Because I was young and dumb!”

The cuts Rio. She had always had a slight insecurity that Agatha didn’t fully know what she was getting into back then and would regret it later. Now it seems she was right to feel that way. But she’s too angry to let the hurt show at the moment.

“Yeah? Then why are you still married to me?”

“Wait, you two are still married??” Jen asks in shock.

“Only because there is no way to break it!” Agatha yells back. “But trust me, if there was, then I would!”

The words hang in the air as the entire room goes silent.

Rio recoils, the hurt clearly written all over her face. It makes Agatha’s heart break. But the green witch recovers quickly before Agatha can say anything. Death puts on an air of formality and looks at Billy. “Your brother is in California, that’s all I can give you. I’ll be busy, but you better hope I don’t find you.”

The green witch turns and disappears through the ground before anyone can say anything else.

The rest of the coven stands there tensely. Billy shifts, wondering if he should say something, though he’s not really sure what. Jen bites her lip, feeling slightly guilty. She hadn’t meant for her playful comment to cause, well, that. She probably should’ve known Agatha would go to extremes to deny her feelings in front of everyone though.

“Get out of my house.”

The whole coven can hear the barely concealed heartbreak under the angry tone their leader spoke in. None of them argue though as they quickly file out.

As soon as they’re gone, the purple witch slams the door behind them and sinks to the floor and cries.

------------------------------------- Agatha looks everywhere. She really does. She travels to nearly 50 different countries she and Rio had once visited. Tries all the hideouts they once stayed at. Kills 22 witches. But nothing. Not a single trace of Death. For six months.

She had all but abandoned her mentorship of Billy in her hunt for Rio. The boy hadn’t said a thing about it, even though he was still having a hard time figuring out what to do about Tommy. He couldn’t exactly easily go to California on a regular basis to look for him. The rest of the coven had tried to help out the teen in the meantime. They continued to all hang out and invite Agatha, but even when she attended, she was hardly there.

The spirit witch feels beyond awful. Like her very soul had been ripped out. Of course she felt similar when Nicky had died, but that was out of her control. This was all her doing. She has this desperate need to see the green witch again, but it’s slowly dawning on her that she may never. Besides, what would she even say? Sorry? Would that be enough to make up for the fact that she had basically said she regretted marrying Rio? No. Would anything make up for it? Probably not. Who was she kidding. She had lost the love of her life.

Agatha is on the floor gulping wine and dazedly looking through more books on how to find Rio when there’s a knock on the door. She frowns, if one more coven member was here to give her a pep talk she thinks she may turn back into a ghost again. She yanks the door open.

“What!...” Her anger trails off as her heart stops. She takes in the woman standing at her door. If she were paying attention, she would’ve sensed the difference in the witch then and wouldn’t have been so blindsided by what was to come. But she’s so caught up in her own shock and relief and happiness that she doesn’t notice anything else.

“Rio…” Agatha breathes, suddenly tongue tied at the sight of the woman she had been chasing around the globe for six months.

The green witch doesn’t look back at her with any fire or emotion that had always been there before. Not even any hurt or pain. Just a blank expression. It scares her.

“I found it.” Death’s voice is a cool neutral tone.

“W-what?” Agatha stutters in confusion, thrown off by her disastrous mix of emotions at the moment. All she wants is to spit out the words she’d been wishing to tell Rio for the last six months. How she didn’t mean it. How she regretted a lot of things in her life but marrying Rio was never one of them. How she still loved her. She doesn’t say any of this though, too shaken by the completely neutral tone Death was giving her. She would’ve preferred screaming over this.

The green witch just sweeps into the house and drops a large book on the table that Agatha hadn’t noticed until then. Rio flips the book open to a page and points at it.

Agatha hesitates, somehow dreading what was on that page already. But Death just continues to point, so she steps forward and reads the bold latin print.

How To Unbind From the Eternal Binding Ritual

Agatha’s hands begin to shake and she suddenly feels like she’s going to vomit up the wine. She glances at Rio with wide eyes, hoping the woman would read the desperate look on her face that she didn’t want to do this.

Rio doesn’t look at her though. Instead she pulls out a piece of cloth filled with ancient runes and places it on the table. Then she pulls out another object from her robes that makes Agatha’s breath stop. Her wedding ring.

The green witch hesitates only for a split second before placing the ring on the table as well. She hopes Agatha doesn’t see the shaking of her hand. “I’ve done my part of the ritual. All that’s left is yours.”

She looks at Agatha for the first time in the whole encounter and is surprised to see tears in her eyes. Maybe the purple witch was just that happy at finally being able to be free from Death after all these centuries.

She looks away, deciding she couldn’t bear the sight of the woman she was still so in love with. “You can do it now if you want. We both have to be here to finalize it.”

She feels the purple witch’s stare on her, but she doesn’t look up from the ground. If she does, she’s sure she’d fall to her knees and beg Agatha to let her stay. That couldn’t happen though. She had held the spirit witch captive long enough.

“I…have to do it later.” Agatha stutters out, voice trembling. “I’m busy and I…don’t know where my ring is.”

Rio barely resists the urge to look up, but her eyes narrow all the same at the tone that sounded very much like a lie. She doesn’t overanalyze it though. Soon enough she’d be free of Agatha’s lies. She tells herself that’s what she wants.

“Fine. Then I’ll come back later.” Rio sweeps out the door before she can do something stupid like tell Agatha she’s still in love with her.

The purple witch watches Rio go, feeling like she just got hit by a truck. Actually, she thinks she’d take a truck any day over this. Her hands shake as she pulls out her ring from her pocket where she always kept it. She glances at the piece of cloth where Rio had put her part of the unbinding ritual. She vaguely thinks about burning it to ash right then and there, but something stops her. She feels it would be a disservice to the green witch, and heaven knows she had already done plenty of those.

Tears fall from her eyes as she places her ring next to Rio’s. They still fit together, green and purple stones blending perfectly even after all these years. She supposes they’re the only perfect things left of their marriage. She stares at them for a long time, crying. She glances at the book, wondering if she should complete her part of the ritual. But she just can’t. Besides, Rio wasn’t there anymore anyway. So she figures there’s no harm in putting it off for a bit longer.

Agatha puts it off for more than a bit longer. Rio stops by a few times the next weeks to check and every time Agatha offers some lame excuse about how she still couldn’t find her ring. The green witch never stays around long enough to question it, but Agatha knows she can’t keep up this charade for much longer. She wishes she could just call the whole thing off and beg Rio to take her back, but she’s not even sure that’s what the green witch wanted anymore. Agatha wouldn’t blame her.

It all comes to a boil one fateful day when Rio sweeps into the house again, this time without knocking. She doesn’t give Agatha time to hide the ring so when she storms in and sees it lying on the table next to hers, she knows the truth she had been suspecting for weeks.

Agatha freezes, hoping that Rio would understand why she did it. Hoping the green witch would pick up on the fact that the last thing on earth Agatha wanted was a divorce. Unfortunately though, Rio doesn’t interpret the situation that way.

The green witch picks up the ring angrily, “Is this some sort of game to you?”

“What?” Agatha says in confusion. That wasn’t the question she was expecting.

“Playing with my human emotions?”

“I don’t…what?”

Rio scoffs, “You know, this has to be an all time low even for you. First you marry me because you’re young and dumb and just want to say you have Death in the palm of your hand. Then you string me along for centuries which is even more ridiculous considering you wanted a divorce this whole time. And then when you finally can divorce me, you won’t! So what is this? Some weird power trip for you? Because I think after three centuries you can go find someone else to torture!”

Agatha’s head is spinning at all the ridiculous words that just came out of the green witch’s mouth. Was that what she had really led Rio to think all this time? Her heart is in her throat at the thought. Sure she and Rio played their games, but she never used Rio. She loved her. She just couldn’t always stop herself from hurting her. There was a difference.

“No, that’s not-”

“Stop. Agatha.” Rio’s voice is beyond broken at this point. “Just let me go. Please.”

Agatha sees it then. The torment she had put Death through. Whether unknowingly or not. She realizes that just because she didn’t want the divorce, doesn’t mean Rio didn’t. She couldn’t keep being selfish. Rio deserved someone that wouldn’t put her through the messiest centuries-long situationship on the planet, and that clearly wasn’t Agatha.

The purple witch bites her lip to stop it from trembling as she grabs her ring off the table and writes the necessary runes frantically on a piece of paper. Then she picks up Rio’s ring and runes and all but shoves it into the green witch’s hands.

Rio realizes what’s about to happen a split second before, but she couldn’t bring herself to stop it even though she desperately wanted to. Nothing stopped Agatha Harkness when she made up her mind anyway.

The spirit witch grabs her ring and paper and then grasps Rio’s hand in the special way they had when they had bound themselves to one another. Holding part of Rio’s wrist in a binding promise that was now about to be undone.

She remembers the lyrics of the ballad. I hold Death’s hand in mine. This was what that line was about. The day she and Rio had bound themselves together for eternity. The day they had gotten married.

Now, over 300 years later, that bond breaks. They see the purple and green magic itself dissipate around their wrists as the runes and their rings also disappear.

For the first time in over three centuries, Agatha Harkness and Rio Vidal were no longer married. Neither one had ever felt more miserable.

Than Face Your Truth

Agatha feels like her world has been turned on its axis. She can’t form a complete thought and isn’t even sure if she’s aware of where she is at that moment. The binding spell had no physical presence, but now that it’s gone, she feels completely empty inside.

Death seems to recover first, pulling her hand away from Agatha’s grasp. Agatha had forgotten she was even holding it, but the action jolts her back to reality as she realizes what’s happened. Rio Vidal was no longer her wife. The very thought makes her woozy. Even in all the time she had spent hating the woman, deep down she still considered the green witch her wife. She could hardly even remember a time before Rio. 300 years was a long time. But now all of that was over in the blink of an eye.

Rio feels like she’s going to vomit, though she’s not sure she even technically could. Agatha always had a way of bringing out the most human side of her though. She risks a glance at her now official ex-wife, but she can’t get a read on her as the woman just stares blankly ahead.

The green witch moves back, trying to pull herself together a bit, even though it feels like she’s shattered in a million pieces. She glances around the house then looks at Agatha one last time who still is staring at the floor. She supposes there was nothing more to say.

“Goodbye Agatha.” She’s proud at how strong her voice came out despite her feeling like she’s dying. The woman looks at her then, and for a second they lock eyes as they had done thousands of times over the centuries. She sees a million things written on Agatha’s face, but for the first time, she doesn’t know what any of it means. It hurts though. So she does the one thing she can do and walks out the door and out of Agatha Harkness’ life.

When Death closes the door, the purple witch feels like her reason for living is gone. She sits on the couch and stares off in the distance, uncomprehending of the time that passes. She thinks she sleeps and maybe eats, but she’s not quite sure.

Billy stops by a few weeks later, rushing into the house in concern when Agatha doesn’t answer the door, the rest of the coven close behind. They had news to share, but one look at their leader told them the news would have to wait.

“What happened?” Teen says anxiously, sitting across from his mentor.

Agatha blinks as if just realizing people were in her house. She glances at the teen before shaking her head. “Nothing.”

Billy knows better than to accept that though and he gets up and looks around. Then he sees the table that had held Rio’s ring for the last few weeks. It’s gone now. He glances at the other coven members. They all knew what that meant.

Billy feels heartbroken for his mentor. He knew they had a complicated history and he knew the spirit witch could be impossible sometimes, but it was clear Agatha and Rio were meant to be together. He thought maybe one day they’d figure that out again, but now he’s not sure. He feels torn, wondering if he should just leave, but Agatha answers the question for him.

“What is it Teen?”

The rest of the coven glance at each other, all making a silent vow not to bring up a certain green witch ever again. Billy goes back and sits in front of his mentor.

“We think we found my mother.”

That seems to snap Agatha out of her daze a bit as she sits up straighter. “What? Where?”

“If I tell you, do you promise not to try to kill her?”

“No.”

Billy sighs. He supposes at least the witch was being honest. “California.”

Agatha’s eyes narrow and she tries to put the pieces together. She always had a feeling Wanda would return, but she wasn’t sure when or where. She wonders why now all of a sudden. She supposes Rio would…no, she’s not thinking about her.

“So you are all here to go on a trip to the west coast I presume?”

“I packed extra sun hats!” Sharon says excitedly, the only one not fully aware of what had gone down between Agatha and the green witch just weeks earlier.

For once, Agatha leans into Mrs. Hart’s excitement, using it as a distraction from the ever present pain in her heart. “Good! Let’s go.”

“Uh no, you need to shower first.” Jen says with a wrinkled nose.

Agatha huffs, “Jen you were alive before they even had indoor plumbing.”

“And I don’t ever plan to go back to living like that.”

The coven leader rolls her eyes but looks down at herself. She did look like a mess. She supposes divorcing your wife of 300+ years who also was the personification of Death would do that to you. Her stomach knots at the thought.

“Fine.”

Once Agatha has somewhat re-entered the land of the living, she teleports them all to California. The rest of the coven vaguely thinks it would’ve been helpful for her to do that for them these last few months, but none of them comment on it.

Billy leads them to a massive graveyard and Agatha’s stomach churns. Unlike most people, she had never been afraid of graveyards, but now it reminded her far too much of a certain green witch. The teen doesn’t seem to notice though as he follows his magical sense, leading them into an underground catacomb, deeper and deeper.

“Of course your mother wouldn’t make this easy.” Agatha mutters in annoyance as they walk and walk.

“So…what’s the Scarlet Witch like?” Alice asks nervously.

“Annoyingly raw and sometimes timid, but very powerful.” Agatha describes Wanda without really thinking much of her words.

“Do I detect a hint of admiration in your voice?” Jen smirks.

Agatha scowls. “Definitely not. Besides, I expect she’ll try to kill us. Or just me. It’s a tossup really.”

“Great. I’m such a big fan of my life always being in peril with you.” Jen mutters.

“We’re close.” Lilia interrupts in her divination voice. They all stop and turn to the eldest witch. “There’s someone else there with her.”

Agatha’s eyes narrow as she looks warily at the door ahead. Instinctively she steps in front of her coven. Whoever was able to get to Wanda first must be incredibly powerful. She pushes open the door.

There in front of them stood The Scarlet Witch looking as if she had never died. Then again, maybe she hadn’t. She’s facing the door, but her hand was on the arm of a figure with their back turned to them.

“Thank you,” Wanda smiles at the figure before looking at the coven. Her eyes become locked on her son.

Agatha doesn’t notice though because she’s too busy staring at the other person in the room who had just turned around. She looks into the eyes of her ex-wife for the first time since they had broken their vow that was supposed to last for eternity. They may be divorced now, but the feeling of looking in Rio’s eyes was still the same, if not magnified.

“Billy!” Wanda exclaims, brushing past Agatha to her son. She stops awkwardly at the last second, remembering that Billy was also William Kaplan. Billy twitches. He thought when he saw his divine mother again he’d be upset and try to fight her. He’s still conflicted, but seeing the way Wanda looked at him now made it clear the woman cared for him. He opens his arms and lets her hug him.

Agatha just continues to stare at the green witch in front of her. She has so many things she wants to ask. What was Rio doing here? Did Rio miss her? Why was Wanda back now? And why was her arm on Rio’s earlier?

The Scarlet Witch breaks the trance between the two ex wives. “Agatha! How…lovely to see you.”

The purple witch rolls her eyes, though vaguely notices Rio stiffen ever so slightly. She doesn’t have time to think much about it though as she turns to Wanda with a scowl. “You’re lucky your son is here, else I’d blast you to purple pieces.”

Wanda looks slightly guilty at that and it annoys Agatha how genuinely nice the woman could be when she wasn’t on a rampage to protect her children. “I’m sorry about the last three years.”

“Don’t remind me.” Agatha growls and holds up her magic. She can’t help but glance at Rio who she swears smiles faintly for a split second, but it’s gone in a blink.

Wanda waves off Agatha’s threat and puts her hand on her son’s shoulder, “I’m glad Billy helped get you out of the spell.”

The purple witch feels the gaze of her ex wife burning into the side of her head. “Right…that was all Billy.”

Wanda smiles proudly at her son and Agatha can’t help the pang of jealousy she feels. She misses her son. She glances at Rio out of instinct but the green witch is watching Wanda curiously. Suddenly she feels jealousy for a whole other reason.

“Well, if you’re not going to beat her up, I will.” Sharon pushes through her coven to the Scarlet Witch and takes off her sun hat to start smacking Wanda. “That’s for my husband and Westview!”

The rest of the coven look on in shock but no one moves to stop Mrs. Hart. Agatha even finds herself smiling ever so slightly. She looks at Rio and sees her smiling at the interaction as well. Her heart pangs. She missed Rio’s smile.

Wanda holds up her hands against the hat, “Mrs. Hart-”

“Oh you call me Mrs. Hart too?!” Sharon points at Agatha while still hitting Wanda. “Well aren’t you just two peas in a pod!”

Rio’s smile drops at that. Agatha doesn’t notice though. She’s too busy giving Sharon an annoyed look. As if she was anything like the Scarlet Witch.

“Uh Mrs. Davis!” Billy finally cuts in and pulls Sharon back gently from his mother. “You wouldn’t want to ruin that nice hat would you?”

Sharon huffs, “I suppose not.”

Jen pushes through and holds out her hand to Wanda. “Hi, I’m Jen.”

Agatha rolls her eyes at the obvious flirtatious tone but Wanda just smiles back politely and greets the rest of the coven. “Rio said you guys were coming.”

The purple witch’s eyes snap over to the earth witch who just looks at the ground. Agatha clenches her jaw, not liking that somehow Wanda already knew Rio’s name. She wonders how long Death had been there before they arrived. She’s not sure she wants to know.

Billy senses the tension, “Could we maybe get out of this tomb?”

“Yes.” Wanda, Agatha, and Rio all hold up their hands to teleport them away. Agatha glares at Wanda. Rio rolls her eyes at Agatha. Wanda looks sincerely at Rio.

The Scarlet Witch lowers her hand first and nods at The Green Witch. “Go ahead. I don’t know where we’d go anyway.”

The purple witch growls, “Why does she-”

Rio snaps her fingers.

Agatha huffs and glares at her ex-wife when they land at their new location. “Really? My house?”

“Would you have preferred the underworld?” Death snarks back.
