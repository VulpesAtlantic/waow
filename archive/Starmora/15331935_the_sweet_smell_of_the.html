Summary

This fanfic talks about how is the first day off of the Guardians. They went to Peter's Beach House on Earth and spent a little time in there. There are a few special moments that Peter has with Gamora, showing to her how much he loves her.

Notes

THIS FANFIC HAPPENS AFTER GUARDIANS OF THE GALAXY VOL 2. So there are many references to things that had happen in the second Guardians film. Don't read it if you didn't watch it. I don't want make you spoiled.

Everyone was surprised when Peter entered the kitchen of the Milano with some keys, making them fly loudly while whistling. He sat at the table with the other Guardians and took his dinner. Spaghetti with tomato. Drax had prepared his dinner with the help of Peter's Earth recipes.

When he was about to eat his Spaghetti, he looked at his friends and his smile dissipated when he saw that they were not paying attention to him and they were having dinner.

-Seriously? Nobody is going to ask?

He turned their attention to him and it was Rocket who answered.

- What silly thing has occurred to you already?

Peter gave him a defiant look and then threw a Nova Corps letter on the table.

-Quill, do you have another invitation from some Aaskvarian again?

Drax said without looking carefully at the letter. Gamora looked at him from the corner of her eye without losing the serious expression on her face.

-Of course not! That was a long time ago. It is a letter from Nova Prime.

-Nova Prime sent you a letter?

Drax said in bewilderment, staring at Peter. Rocket was responsible for clarifying the matter.

-You know, he will have done as when he thanked Ayesha and whoever will be interested.

Both laughed loudly as Peter blushed and denied him forcefully, as if he were a kid. Gamora, wanting to disprove Rocket's absurd theory, picked up the letter and pulled out the paper inside. When she finished reading, Gamora lowered the letter from her sight and looked at Peter without understanding.

-What? Does not seem good to you?

- Have you asked Nova Prime to give us a vacation with all the criminal activity that has occurred in recent weeks?

Everyone looked at Peter knowing that Gamora was right, as usual. Peter let out a sigh and set the cutlery aside.

-I really asked her for a day off for us, to have fun like the family we are. But she immediately insisted on giving us three vacation days at least. So I did not refuse.

He said smiling innocently as he raised his hands. All the Guardians looked at each other and Mantis quickly wondered who was going to take care of everything during those little vacations. Peter explained that Nova Prime would send more Nova Corps units to temporarily cover them. Everyone looked at each other without accepting it at all, but finally decided to take the opportunity.

-Okay Quill, a break will come in handy. You will say tomorrow where we are going to go with those keys, I am too tired right now.

Groot went to his room after taking the medicine Gamora gave him because he was sick. Then everyone else left the room to go to sleep. Peter waited thirty seconds smiling there while he was waiting for Gamora to return. After a moment she returned to the kitchen complaining about the holidays.

-Peter, a lot of people could really die, a lot of innocents and agents of the Nova Corps. And if we are not there to defend them and something happens and they call us and we are on vacation and ...

Peter gently placed a finger on Gamora's lips and moved closer to her.

-Trust me, everyone will be fine.

He said in a reassuring whisper. She looked him in the eyes and then looked away.

-I just ... I am worried Peter. I do not want more people to suffer and it will be our fault because we could have avoided it. Although I suppose you have had a good idea. My body is not one hundred percent and could have some failure during a mission. It will not be bad to rest a little.

Peter threw his head up and when he came down he sighed with a smile. That smile that he kept only for her.

-What?

Gamora whispered smiling, noticing that Peter was beginning to show the flushed face. She loved to see him like that, and even more so when she was responsible for his reaction. Peter rested his hands on her hips and pulled her closer, putting Gamora practically in his arms.

-Nothing, I just ... I remembered what I had thought for the holidays.

Gamora raised an eyebrow and pulled the Ravager jacket to bring Peter closer.

-What have you thought?

-I will only tell you that I will take you to my childhood home.

They whispered in the clear darkness of the kitchen. When Peter wanted to cut the tiny distance between them, Gamora stepped forward and wrapped her arms around his back as she whispered something.

-I wanted to hear that.

That said, Gamora slid her hands from his back to his hip and leaned down to kiss him on the cheek. Peter stood still trying to wake up from that feeling, and when he did Gamora had gone to her room to rest. He laughed silently, looked at the keys on the table and went to sleep.

Although after the events of Ego they had declared love for each other through an "spoken thing", they had not gone beyond fooling around and playing. They did it because it felt good. But now Gamora realized how much she wanted him closer and closer, but the thoughts of stration kept her away from him. Peter knew that well, and she knew that he knew it, and that made her love him more for considering the time she needed, without forcing her to anything.

That night Peter slept in his captain's chair, sitting on it with his legs dangling to the side. He liked to sleep there when he could not wait the next day. He looked at stars and distant galaxies while thinking about anything until sleep invaded his body.

One night he woke up with a blanket up to his neck and saw that Gamora was in her seat below, piloting in the wee hours of the morning. He does not remember how long he was contemplating her in silence until he stole the command from his place. Gamora turned around with a smile and climbed up beside him.

-You are supposed to be sleeping.

-I can not sleep if you take care of me that way and I have you nearby, with the faint light of the galaxies, only the two of us.

He remembered that it was the first time that Gamora hugged him from behind, passing her arms around his neck. It was easy to reach that height because Peter was still sitting in his place. She told him that his "unspoken thing" was solved.

Peter smiled even more as he remembered that night. He was going to explode with happiness. He wanted to take his friends to Earth, to the summer house on the beach. That beautiful wood house that he liked so much when his mother took him during the summer holidays. He wanted to remember some moments that happened there, although some of them were somewhat painful.

He wanted Gamora to be by his side, because he needed to remember, because there were nice and sweet memories that he would like to share with her. He just wanted tomorrow to arrive.

The noises of the training room woke Peter because the bridge was near the room. Peter smiled knowing that Gamora had gone back to training after a few days, since thoughts about her past had held her back a bit lately.

Peter got up from his seat and leaned out the door of the room. There she was. A bright green body moving fluidly between the enemy robots that Rocket built for training. What Peter did not expect is that she was also using his blasters. He smiled to himself and immediately set off for Earth. It would take a little less than five hours. They were approximately 500 clicks away.

Groot had discovered the art of video games. Rocket worked on something he had called "Bubble- blust02", no idea what it was. Mantis was assembling a puzzle box with Drax. It was very funny, none of them had seen one before and they did not know what was inside. That reason motivated them even more to open it. Gamora had just announced that Earth would be visible in two minutes.

- Are you sure you want to return now?

Although a little time has passed after the events of Ego, he still love this place.

-Although there are bad memories, I also have many good ones.

Gamora smiled and Peter passed behind her a stray rebellion that had escaped.

-I hope you know how to swim.

-Much better than you.

She said moving away from him with a little push, returning to her seat to prepare the descent. Peter told her the exact coordinates to Gamora and quickly were found descending under a blue sky with slight clouds in this.

-It is there!

Peter exclaimed, pointing to the wooden house right next to the beach. Everyone observed the beautiful aerial views of the beach from the Milano. They left the Milano just behind some trees that led to a small hill very close to the beach. They did not want anyone to see the spaceship because they could be scared. They carried all the luggage to the entrance and Peter took out the keys. Those keys that have been in the drawer of the small table in his room in the Milano, abandoned since the day he settled in it. He noticed Gamora's hand on his back and decided to open the door, not knowing what he would find. Only twenty years or more had passed... Upon entering, Peter remembered that everything was there. Except for the excess of technology that was in the house. Someone has been taking care of the house. It should be his family.

-This is the summer house where I used to come with my mother.

Everyone watched closely.

-It's cozy.

Mantis said cheerfully. Peter smiled to see the happiness in the faces of the others, especially in Gamora.

-Where do we leave our things?

Rocket said, getting impatient to unload the heavy luggage.

-There are three rooms upstairs. There are two guest rooms and there is another...

Peter blushed in a moment. Rocket left a mischievous smile and picked up his luggage again.

-All right Star-Lord. We already managed.

Peter nodded and glanced at Gamora. She was in the living room window, watching the sea. All the others went up to leave their luggage. Peter came over and put both arms around her, resting his chin on her shoulder.

-This view is beautiful.

-Always use a stool to contemplate it from this window. I really spend a lot of time looking at the sea. Although I will have to show you the terrace.

She smiled and looked back at the stool in the corner of the room. Peter kissed her cheek and took her luggage.

-I will upload it, you make yourself comfortable.

She smiled and settled on the sofa, looking at everything in the room. There were many photos of Peter as a child with his mother and some other children, who seemed to be his age. He had not made the case for the photos, she thought. Gamora knew how he was when he was a child because he had a picture with his mother in his room. She has always thought that he was adorable. As she turned her head a little more, she saw a large picture of him on the floor, leaning against the wall. There were papers on the floor. She came to read some. They were letters in which they missed Peter. Gamora supposed they were his family. She felt sorry for his family. How sad it must have been.

Suddenly she heard Drax running down the stairs, chasing Rocket, who headed for the beach quickly. He is also followed by a Mr. Star-Lord in a swimsuit.

-Gamora hurry up and put on a swimsuit. The one you like most in the closet above. Second door on the right.

Peter said smiling. Everyone went to bathe and Gamora went to her room just as Peter had said. Upon entering she saw her luggage well placed next to the bed. Gamora blushed when she saw such a big bed.

-I will sleep with Peter then.

She opened the closet and saw some bikinis and swimsuits. She opted for a bikini of simple black color. She moved quickly away and went out with the others to the beach. They are all there already, bathing while Mantis was playing in the sand with Groot, taking care of him. It was strange how everyone had got used so quickly to Peter's planet. Gamora was the first time she saw sand like that and was fascinated. They spent the morning before eating. Peter had been smart for once and had changed some of his units in Xandar for what he called "Dollar". The currency of the Earth, Gamora supposed. Peter went to buy food and dinner. That day was filled with Peter's funny stories about Earth. Everyone listened attentively until the night enters quickly. After seeing Footloose, everyone went to sleep.

-I want to show you something.

Peter said quietly to Gamora. When all of them where in their rooms, Peter led Gamora to the terrace.

-On Earth it is beautiful to observe the stars at night, but we always have a privileged view from up there always. So it is not as romantic as it should be.

Peter said bringing Gamora around the waist, leaving her to one side. Gamora laughed silently and turned to look at Peter.

-Not every day you have so much water in front with such a beautiful sound. That is added to the romantic point you wanted.

Peter looked down at her eyes and smiled tenderly at her, his gentle eyes shining through her.

-You are the most beautiful thing I have seen in my entire life.

He noticed Gamora's nervousness at once, seeing the light green blush on her cheeks with silver touches. What Gamora felt right now was too much. Peter looked at her as if the world was going to end in a few hours, and she felt like the luckiest woman in the universe.

-Peter. I love you.

She finally dared to say, with a feeling full of relief. Peter smiled even more and put both hands on her cheeks, caressing the silver marks of her face.

-Do you still wear the bikini?

-Yes. Why?

-Come.

Peter took her by the hand quietly to the night beach. The faint light of the moon illuminated everything. They both took off their flip-flops and walked along the shore of the beach holding their hands.

-Your home is adorable.

-It is my summer vacation home. I really lived in Missouri, remember?

-Yes.

There was a comfortable silence until Gamora spoke again.

-Peter. Are you really okay with all the memories of your mother?

Peter looked at her as he thought. After a moment he put his arm around Gamora's shoulder without letting go of her hand, getting closer to him.

-I really miss her a lot, like you do with your family. But with you here it's much easier. The other Guardians also make it better, because it's my current family. My mother would be cheerful to see me happy instead of seeing my whole life sad, missing her.

Gamora was relieved to hear those words and circled Peter's bare waist with her free arm. Peter felt a tingling in the skin that made Gamora's hand tighten harder, which caused her to lean on his shoulder. They walked for a long time on the beach until they returned to the place where they had left their flip-flops.

-Let's go for a swim.

Said Peter lifting Gamora. She nodded silently without saying anything and put her arms around Peter's neck, resting her forehead against his. She could feel his breathing against her chest and Peter's fresh but warm strawberry breath tickling her neck. She loved the smell of his toothpaste. They were so close when the water covered them just below the shoulders that Peter had to hold Gamora stronger because she did not reach the ground.

-Peter, I can not reach the ground.

-Don't worry, I got you.

He said sliding one hand up her back until it reached her arm. The slight touch made Gamora shudder and cling closer to him. Peter continued his expedition and cupped her cheek. Gamora slid a hand down his neck and ran it down his sideburns, caressing the slight amount of well-groomed hair that formed there. She followed the hair path to his chin and there it reached his lips. His lips so perfect that they always smiled at her so lightly. Peter left a kiss on her finger and Gamora laughed, making Peter laugh.

-Gamora, I would give anything to be with you more than one life.

Gamora smiled and ate his lips with her eyes. Peter slowly kissed her lips. When they parted for air Gamora kissed him, this time more passionately. Peter had responded in the same way to that kiss. Now he placed his lips gently on her neck, leaving a trail of kisses. Gamora could not help but tilt her head and hold him tighter. Still kissing, Peter stepped involuntarily and stopped touching the ground, so they both sank momentarily. When turning back, Peter approached Gamora around the waist while they both laughed. -I am sorry.

-It was funny.

She said returning to his lips, savoring the salt in them. The temperature continued to rise between them to the point that they felt they were going to melt in the cold night water.

-Peter.

Gamora interrupted something alarmed.

-What?

-I forgot Groot's medications.

Peter complained with a small moan as he continued to kiss Gamora's shoulder.

-I promise we are continuing this when I give it to him.

Peter smiled on her shoulder and deposited one last kiss on her lips.

-Agree.

They both came out of the water and took their clothes. They went to the house and while Peter was changing Gamora prepared the medication of Groot, who was already sleeping and had to wake up. His age could be compared to that of a six-year-old earth boy. Gamora went up to the room and changed while Peter was on the terrace with the Zune. She went out with him and Peter took her to dance. Their lips met again shortly after the song "Bring it on home to me" was playing.

-I loved that time on the balcony.

-Our "unspoken thing".

They both smiled. They stayed quiet for a while when the song ended. Gamora so buried in his bare chest as much as possible. Peter breathed the scent of her shampoo with the scent of seawater. Gamora separated from him and with her hand led him to the bed. Peter picked her up and kissed her, laying down with her. Peter's hands ran up and down Gamora's back. She took off her shirt to feel the warmth of his hands, making her body tremble. When Peter reversed the positions, leaving it up, he kissed Gamora's chest until he arrived at her neck. She sighed and rested both arms aside her head. Peter found the fastener closure.

-Gamora...

-Go ahead please.

Peter obeyed and with a deft movement unbuttoned it.

The night continued on a warm course until dawn.
