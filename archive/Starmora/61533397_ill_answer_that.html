It felt... off. She wondered aloud if they were making her the butt of some intergalactic joke.

However, the team was insistent, and eventually, she gave in. By the time she was sitting in front of a mirror under the hot lights, with stylists fussing over her hair and makeup, Gamora was still skeptical. The gown hugged her figure perfectly, and the glittering accents caught the light in a way that made her look otherworldly, but all she could see was the clash of colors.

"I look ridiculous," she muttered, fidgeting with the hem of the dress.
