She sees him die every time she closes her eyes.

She is not a stranger to nightmares, she has been plagued by them most of her life, but she has never felt this everlasting tight-gripping terror, squeezing at her heart, like she does these days. It’s been days, but she keep waking up in the middle of the night, chocking on air and barely breathing.

Where is Peter?

She was so scared in that moment. So much more than when Mantis had called her out just mere minutes before that. She is still scared.

Where is Peter?

He is dying. All the time.

At least in her sleep.

She doesn’t know how to deal with these feelings, with these emotions of desperation and longing and fear and pain and utter failure.

Because he might not be actually dying now — in real life — but she also can’t save him, can’t help him, like he has saved her, like he helped her, and he is pulling away, creating so much distance between them, and she feels useless.

“Peter, I…”

She swallowed, stumbling over the words. Her heart was bleeding — for him. In the span of his life, he lost so much, he faced the worst of possible evils — evils he didn’t deserve — and she didn’t know what to say. How to help him.

His heartbroken expression made her want to cry.

As if he doesn’t trust her.

And maybe he doesn’t.
