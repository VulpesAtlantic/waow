Nathalie attempts to greet her back but the name escapes her; all she remembers is that it starts with the letter ‘M’.

She settles for a cordial nod, and is gratified when she accepts this as enough pleasantries and starts the preparations for take-off. The next few minutes are spent in awkward silence as they both settle in the plane, avoiding each other’s eyes.

"I almost thought you'd have the beds laid out," Nathalie finally says as the plane starts to taxi.

"They can't do that before take off."

"Ah. Are we doing that then?"

A sheepish expression crosses Gabriel's face. "That might depend on you."

She raises an eyebrow at him. "Is this...something we're going to get another chance to do?"

Gabriel swallows. He’s not looking at her when he answers.

"I'm married."

"You were married last night,” she retorts. “And when we almost missed this flight."

"I know, but I—," he swallows and cuts himself off. Nathalie very carefully doesn’t look at him as the plane lifts from the ground.

"If this is your way of saying it can't happen again,” she starts, keeping her voice steady, “then we don't have to talk about it. We wanted each other, we gave in, and now we're going to be professional again. When the wheels hit the runway in Paris, this never happened . But don't try to blame this on being drunk , Gabriel. Not after this morning. Not after you made me confirm I wanted every bit of it."

He’d stiffened up when she’d cut him off, but the more she talked the more he seemed to slump in his seat. When he looks at her, his eyes are dark with the same kind of unreadable something that Nathalie had seen right before he kissed her for the first time.

"Alcohol is a great lowerer of inhibitions,” he admits, “but it's never made a man do anything he doesn't want to do already. You are...entrancing, my dear Nathalie.”

“I—”

“ Addictive .” Gabriel breathes, and as the light of the morning sun hits his jawline she all but blushes at the faint red lines where her nails have dug in last night.

His next words, however, pour a bucket of cold water on her ardour.

“But I can't pretend I'm free to indulge anymore."

Nathalie leans back against the seat with an inward sigh. "Then we forget this. When we land in Paris."

"Yes." Then his eyes widen. "When we land in Paris?"

"You wanted to take every taste you could while you were indulging, didn't you? Did you assume I was any different?”

He swallows. "I would have thought you were…ah, too sore to do anything."

"I am," she confirms. The overhead lights switch to green as the pilot gives the all clear, and Nathalie unbuckles her safety belt and moves to sit on Gabriel’s lap. Her fingers toy with his lapels as she curls up into him, tucking her head below his chin.

"I just want to do this,” she says, and closes her eyes with a pleased hum.

Gabriel looks down at where she's curled up on top of him, her warm weight a. gentle callback to the way she felt like home in his arms last night. His own arms rise up to her waist, holding her secure against him. Nathalie smiles against his neck and for a moment, Gabriel is free to pretend.
