The aftermath of RE9

Notes

Ok, I'm actually so proud of this one!

Sitting in the helicopter with Leon was one of the biggest reliefs of Grace's life. The living nightmare was over now.

Leon at her side was explaining what the next steps were going to be. They were going to be flown to a safehouse where they were going to stay for at least twenty-four hours just in case. There they can patch themselves up, shower, and rest

“Sherry will probably join us as well.”

“Sherry?” Grace asked.

Leon gestured at the com in his ear, “She's in need of the antidote too.”

Ah. Well Grace was all for getting out of here and getting clean. She was in pain and so very tired. The blood absolutely covering her wasn't helping anything either, and Leon seemed to be in the same boat as her.

Thankfully, Leon had them moving not ten minutes later. Grace was realizing quickly that Leon wasn't just anyone in the DSO. The people who saved them, Leon said they were called The Hound Wolf squad, treated Leon like their superior. Leon had explained that it was probably because Chris, their commander, was a good friend of his.

“Chris was supposed to send back up a while ago. I'm glad it got here when it did, but I wish it had come much earlier.”

Grace couldn't help but agree.

She fell asleep on the flight out and was shaken awake by Leon once they landed. She startled awake and then began apologizing. She'd fallen asleep on the other man and hadn't even realized.

Leon had just waved her off, “Don't worry about it besides It doesn't bother me.”

She was still embarrassed but didn't fight him, just let herself be led to the safe house. Leon left her in the main area as he swept the house ‘just in case’. She was sure it wasn't necessary, but it did make her feel better. Once he confirmed there was nothing there, he started digging around and found a change of clothes for them both. She took hers and found the nearest bathroom.

She wants to get clean and patched up so she can just lay down and sleep. The water ran red and she shuttered at the sight. She scrubbed her skin until it was red and did her best to avoid the stab wound on her side.

Everything hurt but at least she was clean. It took her some effort to get her new set of clothes on, but once she did she walked back into the main area. Leon was sitting at the kitchen table with his shirt off as he cleaned and bandaged his own wounds. On the table were the contents of a first aid kit laid out neatly for easy access.

She couldn't help but let out a surprised gasp at the sight of him. The number of scars littering his chest was astounding. Burns, bites, and gouges covered him, and she could tell most of them were from something definitely not quite human.

He just looked up and gave her a small smile before finishing up and put on the shirt he had laying on the table.

He stood and gestured for her to take a seat in his place. She did without fuss.

“You hungry?” He asked.

She’d completely forgot about food, and the reminder had her stomach growling. She didn't know if she'd be able to eat, but she was starving. “Y-yes.”

He just chuckled as he went about patching her up, “Yeah me too, I think they got a pizza in the freezer we can put in the oven. They usually have that in the safe houses.”

It sounded great. Honestly, anything sounded like a gourmet meal right now, and she told him as much.

Leon just chuckled, “I know exactly what you mean.” He agreed easily.

Luckily it didn't take him long to patch her up, and as he took a step back, he grabbed something on the counter and threw it at her. She fumbled it for a second but miraculously didn't drop it.

“Should help with the pain.” He said simply.

She looked down at the packet in her hand and opened it. Sure enough, a few pills were inside. Leon tossed her a water bottle a second later from where he was digging in the fridge.

She thanked him and quickly cracked it open, swallowing down half the bottle before finally taking the pills and finishing the rest. She hadn't realized how thirsty she was either.

She walked over and collapsed onto the couch with a sigh. And immediately almost cried with relief as it finally sunk in that it was well and truly over. A few moments later, Leon dropped into the chair next to her with a groan.

“I'm getting too damn old for this.” He grumbled and Grace giggled a bit hysterically.

Leon just gave her an amused smile before pulling out his phone. Fuck where even was hers? Probably lost somewhere in that God forsaken bombed out City. She'd get another.

“Sherry will be here soon.” He told her. She just hummed in acknowledgment.

She didn't know how long they sat there in silence, neither of them daring to suggest they turn on the TV. There was no question in either of their minds that Raccoon City was the only thing being covered right now, and neither of them wanted to think about it.

Eventually the stove went off and Leon got back up. “I'll bring you some.” He told her simply and she just let out a relieved breath. She wasn't sure she'd be able to get up right now if she wanted to.

A plate was handed to her as she sat up slightly. Leon collapsed back into his chair and silence rained once more as they ate. It wasn't nearly as hard to stomach the food as she thought it would be, and for that she was thankful.

Leon, the genius, had brought over the whole pan and set it down on the coffee table over and oven mitt. As they both reached for more, he'd just chuckled at her slightly awed look.

“I've done this enough times to know what to do.”

She doesn't doubt it for a second, and she'd never been more thankful.

There was a knock on the door only a few minutes after they finished eating. Leon was up and opening the door a second later. He had Requiem in hand, but he didn't seem to think he'd need it. Sure enough, after checking the peephole he quickly opened the door.

In stepped a woman who looked to be in her forties. She had blonde hair in a pixie cut and blue eyes so very similar to Leon's own.

Leon just pulled her into a hug. “Hey kiddo.”

Grace blinked in surprise. Kiddo was not the epithet she expected.

“Leon.” The woman acknowledged letting herself be pulled against him wrapping her own arms around him in return.

They pulled back from one another a few moments later, and Leon started dragging her behind him. He snatched one of the two vials of antidote on the table and quickly handed it to the woman.

Grace watched as the woman rolled her eyes but dutifully took it. Grace looked closer and saw she was wearing a glove underneath her white button down. The woman rolled up her sleeve on her gloved hand and Grace saw the black veins creeping out underneath the glove.

Leon watched on intently as she injected the vial into her arm. The black veins as it had for Leon quickly faded away. The woman just let out a deep breath and sighed before pulling off her glove and watching as the black completely faded away.

“Shit that does feel better…”

“I told you.” Leon said simply before leading her back over to where Grace was. The woman introduced herself as Sherry which Grace had put together and explained that Chris’s team was currently rescuing Emily.

Grace felt tears prick her eyes as relief flooded her. Sherry explained what was going to happen next, and Grace just nodded along. She could live with it as long as Emily would be safe and alive.

Leon too seemed relieved at the news.

They all chatted for a while longer before the sound of clicking footsteps had Grace freezing in abject terror. Sherry was the only one supposed to be coming, Leon had told her as much.

Sherry and Leon were frozen too. Sherry had a gun in her hand the next second, but Leon made no such move just tilted his head listening intently. Leon stood the next moment and on cue a figure turned the corner bathed in the shadows from the hall.

Sherry looked unsure but Leon just smiled.

“You could have told me you were coming.” He spoke into the silence amusement coloring his tone.

“Well, I was going to surprise you.” A feminine voice responded dryly.

Sherry let out a breath and went lax her eyes now annoyed instead of weary.

The figure took another step finally allowing herself to be seen. The woman was about Grace's height if not a bit taller. She was a beautiful Asian woman her short hair pitch black with strands of gray, her face youthful and nearly glowing. She wore an expensive looking red dress, black stockings, stiletto heels, and forearm length gloves.

Leon stepped forward to meet her, “How did you even get in?”

“Your window.”

“How did you know it was mine?” He asked as he reached a hand out to curl around her hip.

The action had Grace blinking in surprise as it highlighted something she had missed before.

A ring, since when was he wearing that?

“Because it's the smallest room in the safehouse.” She answered with a knowing smirk. Leon just chuckled before pulling the woman into his chest, “I wasn't expecting to see you for a few more days.”

“Yes well, someone has to make sure you don't get yourself killed.” The woman huffed, but her tone was fond.

Sherry from her place next to Grace just chuckled. “Ada.” She greeted.

The woman, Ada, pulled back and glanced over to Sherry. “Birkin, it's been a while.”

Leon didn't pay them any attention, just led Ada to the table with the hand he had firmly wrapped around her. He snatched up the other vile quickly before turning his full attention to her.

Ah, this is why he grabbed two.

He helped her take off one of the elbow length gloves, and those same black veins were there crawling up her arm. He injected her with the vile a second later and just as it had for Sherry and Leon, the veins quickly disappeared.

Ada swayed lightly, but Leon had her firmly in his grasp as he watched the black disappear with palpable relief. He took off her other glove as she just closed her eyes and took a steadying breath.

Her infection had been worse than Sherry’s but not as bad as Leons, the scars it left behind made that clear. Both of her arms all the way up to nearly her elbows showed the proof of the antidote's success.

Ada fished for something around her neck before pulling out a chain. On it sat a ring, beautiful but simple. She took it off the necklace and put it on admiring it momentarily on the now unmarked skin.

“Thanks handsome.” She finally spoke again after a moment of silence.

Leon just hummed and cupped her face before pulling her into a quick kiss. “Anything for you.” He told her easily.

She stepped back and turned to Grace, Leon watching her the whole time like he couldn't bear to look away.

“Thank you for keeping him alive and breathing. Trust me I know it's not an easy thing to do.”

Grace fumbled over her words as she tried to find something to say. “I-it’s no problem h-he helped me more than I did him. There wouldn't be a cure if he hadn't k-kept me alive.”

Ada just nodded. Before looking back at Leon. “Mind telling me what happened then?”

“In the morning? I think we're all tired.” He suggested.

Ada thought it over for a moment but nodded. “Fine but I want to hear everything.”

Sherry nodded to herself. “I'll take the couch.”

Ada just grabbed Leon's arm and started dragging him down the hall. Leon for his part didn't seem like he wanted to be anywhere else.
