The pain swept over her body as her legs quickened. 'Run for your life.'

She looked back to see if anyone was behind her when she felt two strong hands pull her aside. She got déjà vu from the party.

Instinct first. She raised a knife at her unknown assailant, though she saw nothing where he locked her up. He took her hand quickly enough to protect himself.

"Hey, hey, it's me!"

Confused, she lowered the strength in her hand and the man cupped her face, approaching so she could focus his face. "Peter ..." she said, as if wondering if she was dreaming. He nodded with a slight smile. His eyes stared at her as if they couldn't believe she was in front of them. Those beautiful green eyes. "Peter!" She smiled with relief and wrapped her arms around his neck. He didn't back down and hugged her around the waist. Her head resting on his shoulder.

The voices on the outside of the door made them pull away. Gamora leaned against the wall behind her, feeling dizzy again. Peter wouldn't let go of her waist, being careful not to hurt her further. "Hey look at me, we're getting out of here." Her eyes stared lazily at his. "I have a plan."

"Please tell me it's more than twelve percent."

He found himself chuckle and leaned forward, placing a kiss on her forehead. Then he pressed his com with his finger. "Rocket, I need you to drill a hole in the base to distract the guards, but it must be away from us."

"Finally to the point." Rocket said amused.

Peter continued. "Mintis, you will have to guide us and Nebula on the road when the guards are gone."

“Okay!” Said Mantis.

"Once we're out we get Drax and fly back to the Milano. Everything clear?" It's true that Peter said he had a plan, but he made it up right now.

As soon as they heard Rocket's shooting, Mantis instructed them which way to go. "Let me carry you." Peter said.

Gamora, of course, was too stubborn to agree. "You're going to get tired too fast. I can walk."

He would keep insisting, but he knew that arguing with Gamora was not good. He sighed. "Okay, at least lean on me."

As much as she wanted to prove to him that she could do it alone, she knew that was not the case. She wrapped her arm around his neck, allowing him to be her support.

"Alright Gamora, Peter, go now. We don't know how long it will be clean." Mantis said.

They wasted no more time. They were listening to instructions, at least Peter was, and they were moving surprisingly fast.

However, Gamora was already bending. "How much more?" She asked, her eyes half open.

"Mantis?" Peter asked, and when he received an answer, he told her. "Very little. Hold on."

Easy to say. Yes, Gamora had survived worse, but she hadn't been in so much physical pain in a long time since she didn't have to endure Thanos' punishment. If it weren't for the adrenaline rush, her body would have completely given up.

"Soldiers are approaching Peter behind you! At this rate, you will not be able to arrive on time." Mantis shouted.

This time he was really about to pick her up, but they would still be slow. There was no way they could get there together. "Gamora," he put his hand on her cheek. "Gamora, listen to me, I need you to go to the left." He could see how weak she was, but he had no choice. "Once you get to the exit, Drax will be waiting for you on the other side."

"W-What about you?"

"I'll slow down the guards." She shook her head in disagreement. She did not want them to be separated. Again. Neither did he. "Go. There's no time."

He gently lowered her hands and held them, giving her the courage to continue on her own until he finally released her and she turned reluctantly. Peter watched as she leaned against the left wall. A blood trail behind her fingers.

And here they are at last. Security Guard after Security Guard. Peter started firing at each one with unprecedented hatred. But he hadn't noticed those coming from behind. He didn't do it until he felt a burning sensation in his shoulder.

He turned back, leaving another to hit him lightly in the thigh. He hissed and raised a weapon at him only to see that his head was missing. A sharp sword swung in directions, and as Peter saw the blue skin, he recognized his teammate.

He dealt with the other half of the guards. Apparently they weren't used to the shooting, because no one could hit a single target. Although he didn't make it any easier with his agility.

When he thought that everything was getting better, he heard a woman's groan on his left side. He looked at Nebula, who had been knocked to the ground. Aiming his blaster at her, he left his guard down. In a second he was on the floor as well.

The two fell down looked at each other. How the hell they allowed themselves to be brought down on the ground? It was acceptable to him, but Nebula? She was probably hit too.

Already at gunpoint, Peter covered his face with his hands, wanting to keep his face untouched. Already prepared for the blow, he heard the shot, but his hands were not yet fried. He moved them away only to see the guard's falling body.

New shot. Looking ahead, Peter was speechless. Gamora, leaning against the wall, barely able to stand on her feet, firing with a machine gun at the others. Apparently she also came across security guards and took the weapon from them.

This gave both him and Nebula an advantage. Soon they were both on their feet, finishing the other guards.

Gamora could stop for a while. Her head leaned against the wall. She felt the adrenaline rush out of her body. She felt weightless. It was as if no part of her body could move anymore. As if she was paralyzed. Without control. The only thing she felt was falling. Her body slid down.. and fell right into Peter's arms.

He picked her up and hurried to the exit, with Nebula beside them, watching for unexpected jumpscares.

Gamora was watching him from below. She saw how dirty his favorite leather jacket had become. Green blood soaking in. "I'm ruining your jacket." She said still in a somewhat dry voice.

"It was never my favorite anyway." Peter said, trying to cheer up the serious situation. To make her feel better. As always.

"I love you." She said, closing her eyes.

Her body was relaxed in his arms. Lifeless. Her eyes closed. "Babe? Hey, stay with me." Gamora showed no reaction. "Gamora, please. Don't close your eyes. Please. We're almost there."

Unconsciously, he quickened his pace. Nebula too. Gamora was loosing it. Everything was merging. Peter's words grew more and more distant. As in a dream. Or a nightmare. She needed rest. She couldn't keep fighting her body.

“I can’t.” She whispered before the darkness engulfed her.

Safe

Gamora had been healed at the Xandar's hospital for several days already. It was the safest place, as they sometimes worked with the New Corps. One of the few organizations that did not try to kill them.

Most of the guardians stood on the ship and visited it regularly. All but Peter. He hadn't even left.

He had pulled a chair next to her hospital bed and was not moving. In cases where he needed to leave, he called one of the other guardians to replace him. During her first few days in the hospital, her condition had suddenly worsened several times. After Peter was about to have a panic attack, he no longer wanted to risk leaving her alone.

***

Peter considered what to do when they returned to the ship. The doctors had told him Gamora was getting better and should wake up soon. He listed all the movies she had told him she liked. He would play them in their room and cook something to eat together while watching them. No, he wouldn't, he wasn't gonna separate from her for a moment. Drax would cook it! No, Drax can't cook. Mantis then!

And her favorite books! He had to think of picking one up for when she woke up. Maybe Rocket could-.

The movement of the door drove him out of his thoughts. At first he thought it was one of the doctors, but ... Nebula?

This was the first time she'd actually been inside this room. Otherwise she was standing outside, looking at Gamora through the glass window.

She went in and stared at her sister, leaning against the wall with her arms crossed. For a while, neither of them said anything. Nebula looked so .. She was lost in thought. After a few minutes, she spoke.

"She looks so ... peaceful."

Yea, she did. Such a contrast compared to what had happened. But Peter preferred to see her that way. Calm. After everything she's been through.

"Ironic, isn't it?" He caught her attention. "We went to save her, and in the end she saved us." He stumbled upon this thought.

Nebula lifted her chin. "She's strong."

He remembered the times Gamora had told him that Thanos had made them compare. And that Nebula has always been underestimated. He felt a little compassion for her at that moment. "And you're not for underestimating." But he had to ask her something that tickled his mind. "You said that if things went wrong, you would choose Gamora over me. Why didn't you go after her but came to help me?"

"I didn't know she was still inside. She should have been with Drax by then." She paused, but Peter knew that wasn't all. "She was still on my side, even when I tried to kill her on Knowhere. If I had let you die, she would have never forgiven me." So there was a person behind that rough personality, he thought. "I'm trying to make things better."

"Well I think it's working. I mean, if it weren't for you, we wouldn't be able to do it. Consider it a victory " She looked at him thoughtfully. "Do you know why else it's a victory?" "You and I haven't had any fight yet. It's almost hard to imagine."

She looked at him deadly. "I would not rush with the conclusions."

A faint smile on his face. "So kind of you. I guess it's a family thing."

Her expression was still stern. "If something like this happens to my sister again, I'll gut you alive. And I'll make it's painful."

"It will not happen. I am the best guardian in the galaxy." He replied with a contented dose of confidence.

"Don't force your luck." She said, but before Peter could respond with another biting comment-.

"Mm .. What a nice waking-up."

They both stared at the bed. Peter's face glowed when he finally saw Gamora's eyes open. "Good morning, beautiful."

She smiled. What a gorgeous view. Excluding the hospital bed.

“How are you feeling?” Nebula asked her.

"Compared to last time, much better." She replied.

"Good." Nebula replied. Seeing Gamora and Peter in front of her, she felt out of place. "I'll be outside." Peter nodded and she turned.

"Nebula-" She turned back to Gamora's voice. "Thank you."

Then something in Nebula's expression softened. Peter could have sworn he saw her smiling. Then she nodded, as if she fulfilled done her duty, and left.

Peter turned his full attention back to Gamora. He was still sitting next to her. He would hold her hand all the time, but he wanted to be careful with the wounds on her hands.

Peter didn't take the smile off his face, while he asked her out of curiosity. "Did you hear the whole conversation?"

"No." She gave him a light smirk. "Just the most of it." He chuckled. "How long have I been unconscious?"

"One week." A pure look of surprise upon her eyes. "The doctors said you were getting better. I think we can be back at Milano in a few days."

She frowned in displeasure. "I don't want to spend that much time here. I hate hospitals."

"Neither do I, but I won't risk taking you home where we don't have the medicine we need and making your condition worse."

"Cuddling with you would help me a lot more than some drugs." She said in a slightly offended tone that Peter found adorable in this context.

"Who said we can't do it here?" He asked. Gamora seemed to like the idea because she immediately tried to move aside to make room for him before he stopped her. "Sh, no moving." He said explicitly.

Peter didn't need much space. At least not for his whole body. He layed next to her with one foot still in the air and helped her to lean on his arm. Her head rested on his shoulder. He felt her body relax as he wrapped his arm around her.

But there was something about his conscience, and he knew he had to tell her if he didn't want it to keep harassing him. "I'm sorry."

"What for?"

"For leaving you alone." Gamora tilted her head to look at him. "If I had stayed you-"

"Hey." Her bandaged hand slid over his chin. "You did what you thought was right."

"But-"

"No" she said softly. "You had no way of knowing it was going to happen." Her fingers continued to tickle his skin. "Besides, you came for me."

"Yea." He said, a smile appearing on his face. "Thank God you have such a caring boyfriend."

She rolled her eyes, but she found herself smiling too. That's my arrogant Star Lord.

"Mm my hero." She mumbled ironically, but she really meant it. He was her hero. First saved her from Thanos. Then on Knowhere. And now this. He was beside her like no one else has been. The only man who made her reveal herself to him.

"What you did there was very stupid thought, you know that?" He spoke again. Gamora looked at him, not understanding.

"You returning to the fight," he explained. "It was dangerous. You could have gotten hurt even more."

A smile appeared on her face. "Anything in the name of love, right?"

He couldn't help but smile back at the way she used his own words.

Then the memory of the base came to his mind as he carried her in his arms to the exit. 'I love you,' she had said.

He looked down at her. Her chocolate eyes, staring at him with the same spark as before. As if he was all she had.

She was all he had. He placed his hand on her side, stroking her cheek with his thumb. "I love you too."

And he kissed her soft lips.

A sweet, loving kiss. That was it. That was all he wanted. Her. In his arms. Safe.

It was all finally over now.
