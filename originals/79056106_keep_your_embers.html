And I’d probably die there, thought Leon, but he knew that wasn’t something Hunnigan wanted to hear. So he decidedly shut up and took the file.

It didn’t take him long to finish reading the file, with a glass of whiskey beside him as he went through the pages. Words on a file never painted the full picture of the horrors he’d witnessed over the years, so the quickest way to find out was to head to Wrenwood Hotel himself.

Take care of yourself, Leon, her words bound him like a curse whenever he tried to give in to the temptation. The last thing she did for this world was saving him, his life was hers to give, and he couldn’t take that away.

He is burdened to live, to live for her, to live in memory of her.

The fragments of Ada slipped through his fingertips and fell into the darkness, the face of the tiny bear smiling at him for the last time.

“Goodbye Ada.”

I miss you.
