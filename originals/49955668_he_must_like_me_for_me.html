Summary

Peter Quill is adept at catching her off guard. He's done from the start, the very first day that they met, and every one afterward. She's a fool to think today would be an exception.

"Stay."

It's one word that brings her to a standstill.

Blood slicks her face, and muddies her sword. The body lies in front of her.

"Please, Gamora", Peter says, so quietly she almost doesn't hear him. "Stay."

She tries to breathe out carefully. Morat does not move at her feet. Orange blood pools around yellow skin and he stays utterly still.

Peter approaches her slowly, delicately. "Look at me."

She does. Her gaze flickers up slowly. Green eyes look back at her-- with concern, not disgust, not the hatred she knows is justified.

"He was", she says thickly, "going to kill you."

Peter's hand is soft when it brushes against her own, taking Godslayer and dropping it to the ground. The clatter makes her cringe.

"You saved me", Peter tells her. His hand feels warm, thumb rubbing soft circles on her skin.

"I endangered you", she says, shaking her head. "All of you. I... you would have died, just because- "

"Some assholes don't know the difference between you and a purple cunt", Peter finishes, interrupting her before she reaches her end. "That is not your fault."

"It is", she argues. "If I am here, my f… His enemies will always come after us." Referring to him as…that was a habit ingrained in her by force, but one she was making an effort to break because he didn’t deserve that title, not after what he did to her real father.

Peter was the only one who spoke up for her when people called Thanos her father, the only one who knew the entire truth of what he did to her.

He'd told her of some Earthling he'd pretended was his long-lost father, a man with a magical car, but that'd been when he was a child. Now, Peter's got no such illusions. He's an orphan like the rest of them, he's alone like the rest of them, he's--

Touching her face so tenderly it makes her want to cry. Looking at her with kindness she will never deserve. Acting like she's more than the daughter of the Mad Titan.

"We need you, 'Mora", he says. "I need you."

"You would be better off without me."

His thumb swipes at her cheek, flicking away any potential tears. "We are all better off with you."

She shakes her head in denial. Thanos will arrive one day, if his enemies don't annihilate them first. Whatever petty grievances the others have caused pale in comparison to her own crimes; there are entire species that call for her blood.

Morat is the first in a long, long line of attackers. One she'd admittedly slain before he was able to get close to the Guardians, but so what? She can't combat every entity in the galaxy that wants her dead. She's one person.

"I can't keep you safe", she admits, desperate for him to understand.

Peter Quill is adept at catching her off guard. He's done from the start, the very first day that they met, and every one afterward. She's a fool to think today would be an exception.

"We'll keep you safe", he says fiercely. "Flark these assholes-- we know who you are, Gamora."

"I'm Thanos's daughter!"

"You're our friend!" He shoots right back, equally as ferocious. "You're one of us, Mora!"

She can't keep looking at him. That kindhearted gaze makes her want to stay, makes her want to settle her roots with these people. She cannot do that to them.

"I don't want to hurt people anymore", she says shakily. "I don't want to be the reason people are hurt."

Peter's hands cup her cheeks and raise her face, making sure she can't look away. "You are not the reason people get hurt. You saved Xandar, you've saved hundreds of people since-- you're the reason millions are alive."

"And the reason billions more are dead."

"You are not", Peter replies, never letting her look away from him. "You think Drax'd stick around if even the slightest part of him thought you were responsible for what Thanos did? You think Groot'd treat you like his mother?"

"But--"

"You think I'd stick around, if you were evil?" He asks. "I'm here because I know you, because I want you in my life. You, Gamora. Not Thanos's daughter, not Ronan's lackey, you. My friend."

"I don't want you to be hurt because of me!"

"I won't be", he promises. She wants to believe him more than anything else in the universe. "If I get hurt, it's not gonna be your fault. Not ever."

"You don't know that!"

"I do!" He snaps back, like he's desperate, trying to make her understand something she'll never be able to accept. "You're my best friend, 'Mora-- I know you would never set out to hurt me."

"People will hurt you if you're anywhere near me!"

"It'd be worth it", Peter says. "I need you to get that-- you're worth it, Gamora. You always will be."

"I'm..."

"You're my best friend", he repeats, pulling her into a bruising hug. "There's nothing I wouldn't do for you."

"Peter, I can't ask you to--"

"You don't need to ask me", he says, cutting across her. "You don't need to ask any of us. We know who you are. We know the risks and we accept them, okay?"

None of them have ever been in a room with Thanos. None of them understand.

There's no chance of her making them understand either. She doesn't want them to, she never wants them to understand the deep well of fear that rests in her stomach. She doesn't want them to understand the terror she feels when they hear of Thanos's presence in the same solar system they've traveled through. She doesn't want them to get it, not these people, not a single one of them.

"I want you here", he says softly. "Every single one of us wants you here, Gamora."

"You don't know what you're saying."

"I do", he swears. "And you're worth it. Whatever ends up happening, you will always be worth it."

The conviction in his voice makes her want to pause. He's so convinced of it, sounding more certain about her than anything else in the galaxy.

"Just stay", he says, almost pleading with her. "'Cause it'll be worse without you. I don't want this without you."

This: friendship that feels unshakable. This: the closest thing to a family a sorry group of bastards like them will ever get.

And he'd trade it all away, for a moment with her. She could never let him do that.

She rests her forehead against his, breathing carefully.

"Just for a while", she promises. "Just until..."

He, as reckless and as careless as ever, kisses her on the forehead. "For as long as you want, Gamora. Could be a year, could be forever, I don't care-- we'll be here for it."

Her feet are planted firmly on the ground and her entire universe feels flung off its axis. "Do you promise?"

"I promise", he whispers to her. "You've got us forever, if you want."

Of course she wants forever. Of course she wants this.

She also wanted to stay with her family, she wanted a positive relationship with Nebula, she wanted to never hurt a soul-- Thanos has simply never cared for something as trivial as what Gamora would want.

But the Guardians care. Peter cares.

"I'll stay", she says, recklessly, selfishly. "I promise."

She'd stay forever, if she could.
