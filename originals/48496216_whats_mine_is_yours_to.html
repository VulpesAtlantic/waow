Peter thought his life had been completed when Gamora stepped into it. He was quite sure that as soon as he lay eyes on her, he never thought of another woman romantically again.

Then she became his girlfriend and the days moved forward as though he were permanently drunk.

The kisses he pressed to her lips weren't a daydream inside his head. He could hold her in public without her threatening to kill him. Life truly was blissful waking up to her beside him every morning. But there was just one thing.

He wished she'd stop stealing his fucking Zune.

Besides Gamora of course, the Zune was the love of his life. And it felt like he hadn't been anywhere near it in weeks.

Not that it was too much of a problem. Not really. He loved that Gamora was so invested in the music from his planet. He'd watch her often as she sat curled up somewhere, humming to whatever tune she was listening to. Although, despite longing to be in her position, he didn't want to disturb her contentment by taking the Zune from her for his own enjoyment.

Tonight was no different.

He'd walked into his bedroom, the room they now shared, and she was there, staring intently at his most prized possession. She sat in the middle of the bed, legs crossed, looking positively radient in one of his t shirts. It was strange seeing her in white, when she so often wore black. It brightened the emerald green of her skin, the silver markings on her face, the pink tips of her hair. Even in his old shirt, the print fading and the sleeves fraying, she was beautiful, so at peace, head bopping to and fro at whatever tune was coming through the headphones.

Gamora didn't acknowledge Peter as he walked in, so when he approached her, he did so gently, not wanting to risk startling her while she was distracted. He rested a knee on the bed behind her, to let her know he was there, then put his full weight on it, shuffling towards her to wrap his arms around her waist.

“Hey baby, what you listening to?”

Gamora lifted her head up, smiling as she accepted the kiss Peter pressed against her neck.

“Inks.”

Peter frowned. He glanced down over her shoulder at the small screen, wondering who on Terran the unknown band was that Gamora had discovered.

He smirked when he saw the name of the song and band spelled out in front of him.

Never Tear Us Apart- INXS

“Hate to break it to you, but it's pronounced in-ex-ess."

Gamora's head turned to face him rapidly, her silver eyebrows furrowed in confusion.

“Why? That's just ridiculous.”

Peter was bored of talking, pushing the neck of her t shirt aside and pressing his next kiss to her shoulder.

“Why don't you put that down?”

“I wanna finish listening to this song,” she replied, still looking adorably focused on the music.

”But you're so hot. In my clothes.”

“How self centred of you.”

“Fine.”

He grabbed the Zune from her and put it on his bedside table in one swift movement, attempting to return to his position behind her. She didn't let him however, pushing him into the pillows, pulling her shirt over her head and throwing it onto the floor. She crawled towards him, bare breasts on full display, only a small pair of shorts covering her up. As she slid up his body, hands fiddling with the button of his trousers, Peter thought he might be in heaven.

It was a relatively quick affair, at least for them, and before long, Gamora's naked body was pressed against his under the sheets, her soft snores breaking the silence of the room.

Peter couldn't sleep. Every time he tried, his mind was filled with images of the unoccupied Zune, which now lay discarded on the bedside table beside him. He sighed, almost frothing at the mouth at the thought of pressing a earplug to his ear, closing his eyes and getting lost in the voice of Bjorn Skifs.

A quick glance to his girlfriend cuddled up to him. A quick glance to the Zune, looking ever so lonely with no one to listen to it.

He hadn't had a chance in days. He was practically yearning for it.

When it became utterly irresistible, he reached out for it, as quietly as he could. He managed to flick the Zune onto his stomach, silently punching the air in success, his arm rapidly falling back to his side when he glanced at the sleeping Gamora beside him.

She still appeared sound asleep, so he began the next stage of his task, placing the earphones into each ear. Simple enough.

Not having any patience to flick through the songs, he pressed play on the song Gamora was listening to previously, Never Tear Us Apart, and sighed with relief.

He was barely 10 seconds seconds in before Gamora stirred beside him. He froze, not knowing whether to chuck the Zune on the floor to hide it, or act like nothing was out of the ordinary. There wasn't enough time to do anything, as Gamora's eyelids fluttered open. She looked up to him groggily, a face of surprise when she saw him still awake.

“What are you doing?” she murmured, rubbing her eyes.

Peter put on his most mundane smile.

“Couldn't sleep.”

Gamora's brow furrowed, not for the first time that night.

“You never struggle to- never mind.”

She just snuggled up to him more, her left knee coming across to rest on him, unfortunately for him on his pelvic bone, stirring something in his lower belly that he'd felt less than an hour before. He didn't want to disturb her again, her eyelids closed once again and instead he began to play with the ends of her hair, as the top of her head pressed comfortably against his neck. He soothed her back to sleep, before pressing play on the Zune and relaxing into his pillow, ever so content that he had his baby back.

But it wasn't for long.

The next morning, Peter woke up to find Gamora's side of the bed cold. He'd slept in probably, not often unusual for him, but the listening to music into the early hours of the day didn't help his sleep pattern. He got out of bed and dressed slowly, putting the Zune which he'd placed under his pillow into his left pocket.

The kitchen on the ship was empty, apart from Gamora, who was sitting at the table with her legs crossed, eating something out of a bowl. Upon further inspection, Peter realised it was Fruit Loops. They'd obtained a few boxes on a planet that adored Earth's lifestyle, which Peter found quite odd. Still, it meant he was able to buy things that reminded him of home.

Gamora finished her breakfast, placing it on the side, knowing that Mantis would happily wash it up later. She paced towards him, not even giving him time to breathe before she leant up to kiss him. There was nothing innocent about it, pushing her tongue inside his mouth almost instantly, and Peter felt that the only place his hands were meant to be were on the small of her back. She nibbled at his bottom lip, something she did that sent him weak in the knees. It wasn't any different this time and he had to press his back against the wall to steady himself. He sometimes wished she'd stop doing it; he wanted to be the dominant one in the relationship and yet, she really did have him wrapped around her finger.

“Rocket, you might want to wait for your breakfast. Gamora and Peter are about to fornicate in the kitchen.”

Drax's booming voice made both of them physically jump, Gamora slapping her hand on Peter's chest in shock. They hadn't heard him walk in. He was stood in the corner, holding a mug that looked positively doll like in his enormous hands, sipping it with a smirk on his face.

Peter groaned at the disruption, Gamora rolling her eyes at the sound of paws pattering down the corridor.

Rocket came into view mere seconds later, a look of disgust on his face at the couple pressed against one another.

“You know, your bedroom's about 10 feet over there,” Rocket waved a paw in the correct direction out the door, before marching across to the table, “Don't wanna have to watch you knawin' each others faces off while i'm tryna eat.”

Peter felt it was the correct situation to stick his middle finger up at Rocket, slipping his hand under Gamora's arm.

Rocket scowled, mirroring his rude signal. Gamora kissed Peter's cheek before leaving the room, light on her paws as she walked down the corridor.

As soon as she'd let go of him, Peter's clothes felt considerably lighter and he frowned. He slapped his left pocket, sighing when he felt it empty. The Zune was no longer in there.

She was sneaky, he'd give her that. Seducing him for long enough so she could take it. He hadn't even noticed.

It would've been extremely attractive, if he wasn't craving it, just for half an hour.

A couple of days later, he noticed it lying unoccupied on the dresser. Despite being consumed by guilt, he snatched it up quickly, and used some tape to attach it to the back of his bedside table.

He didn't quite know how he would explain himself. He knew Gamora would question why he seemed so unbothered about losing such a precious item of his, but he decided he would cross that bridge when it came to it.

It didn't take Gamora long to come asking for it. Peter was sat in the captain's chair, munching on a candy bar from a planet he couldn't quite recall.

“Peter?” she said, arms wrapping around his shoulders.

“Mhm,” he replied through mouthfuls.

“Where's your magic music box?”

“Dunno.”

She loosened her grip on him slightly, resting her chin on the top of his head.

“If it's not on you, it'll be in the bedroom. It always is,” There was a slight confusion in her tone.

“It's gotta be in there somewhere. Have you checked everywhere?”

“Of course I have.”

He just shrugged and let her go frowning with confusion, while he sat, seeping in his own guilt.

He desperately wanted the Zune to himself. But he didn't want to lie to her.

Peter knew he had to find Gamora her own somehow.

Luckily, they landed on a planet a week later that was fully loaded with Earth specialities. Peter, along with Rocket and Mantis, went to collect a few things, to stock up on American cereals especially. Peter wandered through the markets, eyes searching desperately for a stall that might sell what he wanted. He'd almost lost hope as he came to the end of the stalls, then one caught his eye.

Terran Gadgets.

He practically fell over his feet making his way towards the stall. He scanned the table in front of him, spotting strange modern items that he didn't recognise, as well as devices from his childhood.

After looking to and fro across the table multiple times, he spotted a little item next to an old Atari. It was a Zune.

He could've kissed the seller behind the stall, although the short little creature with sharp pincers protruding from the sides of his mouth would've probably shrivelled away in fear. Instead, Peter gave him double the asking price and began to turn away, content with his purchase.

Until he spotted something else to his liking.

When he returned to the ship, he found Gamora in the bedroom, lying on the bed, flicking through one of his old comics. The shirt she was wearing was rising up her hip, Peter catching a glimpse of her bare skin underneath. He held his breath, trying to push away any distracting thought from the task in hand. He'd appreciate it all later.

Gamora didn't look up at him when he walked in, appearing to find the comic book entirely fascinating. He waited to see if she would acknowledge him, and when she didn't, he spoke up first.

“I got you a present.”

Gamora raised an eyebrow, still not glancing in his direction.

“Is this like some weird Terran euphemism, because it really isn't turning me on...” she said, licking a finger briefly to turn to the next page.

Peter ventured forward slowly, and when he reached her, held out the new Zune in front of her. She closed the comic, eyes widening when she saw what he was gifting her with. She took it from him, watching the cogs whir quickly in her head as she came to the realisation that the Zune she was holding wasn't Peter's.

He wasn't an expert on them in any sense, considering he hadn't been on earth for multiple decades, but he was pretty sure it was a better model than his. It was smaller, and was an olive green colour instead of his own brown one.

Peter barely had time to move before Gamora smacked him on the arm with the comic.

“I knew you had a problem with me taking yours!”

In a useless attempt to try and defend himself, Peter began to splutter helplessly.

“I didn't have a problem with it! I like sharing-”

“You like sharing, how old are you twelve?” she scoffed.

“I just thought it would be easier if we had one each.”

He sat down on the bed next to her, rubbing a hand up and down her leg, hoping she'd see his point. She turned it round on her fingers, inspecting each and every part.

“It is much nicer than yours.”

Peter grinned, leaning forward, eyes on her lips. Gamora rolled her eyes at first, eventually giving in and returning his smile, being the person to initiate the kiss. When she pulled away, she stroked a finger across his cheek.

“Thank you.”

As Peter lent to her for another kiss, he remembered that he had kept his own Zune out of plain sight. He got up off the bed, moving around it to his side, under Gamora's watchful eye the entire time. He knew she'd make a comment when he revealed it, but still, he turned his dresser around, peeling it from the back. When he looked back up at Gamora, she was shaking her head, despite the small smile on her face.

“You really are twelve years old.”

“Ah ah ah, you're not allowed to be pissed at me just yet. Because I got us something else. Something for the both of us.”

He'd viewed the inside at the stall, but he couldn't wait to open it back up again. A couple of records rested on the top as he picked it up, carrying it into the room.

“What is that?” Gamora asked, as he placed it down on the dresser at the far end of the room.

“It's a real old Terran way of listening to music,” Peter said, as he unclipped the latches and opened up the top. The base was smooth and looked like it was made of oak. His eyes lit up, just like they had many years ago when he used to open up his Mom's very similar player.

“It's called a record player. My mom was bought one for her birthday one year, very similar to this one. I was obsessed with it.” He fiddled around with it, plugging it in, then carried on, “Apparently on Earth, they've come back into fashion.”

Swaying to and fro to the music, they twirled around the room in a messy dance, both chuckling softly. She smiled as wide as she had the first time he'd said it, even though he told her multiple times a day.
