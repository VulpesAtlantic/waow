The truth of the matter was that a regime was a sensible way to keep going, to meticulously go through life without having to worry about falling apart.

It was Monday, which meant she changed into a deep plum pair of trousers, with a crisp white shirt tucked into the waistband of her trousers. She scrutinised herself in the mirror of her bedroom briefly, making sure nothing about her appearance was different to usual, before heading downstairs.
