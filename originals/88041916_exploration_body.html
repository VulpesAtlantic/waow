"Do you?" Nathalie asked, her teasing.

Emilie was quiet for a moment, then sighed again. "No," she admitted dramatically. She patted Nathalie's shoulders like one might pat the haunches of a horse, then said, "Fine, have it your way, but don't forget we'll have you our way later!"

"We're too close to the resort for you to say that," Nathalie said, smiling. Ahead, the beach was drawing nearer, resort patrons enjoying the sun and the waves.

"Emilie, someone could hear you!" Gabriel said at the same time, a quiet whine to his voice as though he were imagining some socialite spreading the story later.

Emilie's laugh rang across the beach as they exited the trees and stepped onto the sandy white beach.
