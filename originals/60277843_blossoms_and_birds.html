So she wasn't surprised when Agatha started to become a little distant when the anniversary approached.

Rio gave her space, when it was clear she actually needed it, and smothered her in affection when it was clear that Agatha was trying to keep her at arm's length. A defense mechanism, to save her from more pain.

She also wasn't surprised when she did the latter, one day in mid-January, and Agatha had lost her temper.

“I asked you to stay away! Can't you even do that for me?” Agatha bit out, voice cold and icy, and Rio felt her heart break a little.

Not because of the words, but because of the aching sadness in Agatha's eyes.

The deep loss that only a mother who has lost a child can understand.

“Alright. I'll leave. Message me when you want me to come back, alright? I love you, Aggie.” She says gently, and tries not to let it sting when she gets a huff in reply and no verbal answer.

Rio is worried, so fucking worried, but she won't push. She knows from experience that pushing can only make it worse.

She goes to Alice and Lilia's, tries her utmost to distract herself with games and gossip, but her eyes never leave her phone.

Agatha doesn't message her.

She debates going home to Agatha. Maybe the woman had fallen asleep, tired from her grief.

However, she also knows that if Agatha shouts at her again, she might start crying. Or say something she'd regret.

Rio's come a long way from the girl who would let someone walk all over her, if it meant they'd love her.

And it's all because of Agatha.

She hates the idea of using the self-worth she'd gained through Agatha and her influence, her unwavering support, against her in a fight fuelled by grief.

She settles for a simple text, reminding Agatha that she loves her, and snuggles down on Alice and Lilia's couch to sleep.

God bless her habit of always carrying a change of clothes with her, honestly.

****

She goes home the next morning, and she notices that the bed is empty, not even having been slept in. Panic shoots through her body, wondering where the fuck Agatha is.

She wanders through the house, her house, until she notices a soft sound coming from the nest of blankets on her couch.

A sob.

“Oh, Aggie…” Rio says softly, and she gets a pained whine in response.

She falls to her knees next to the couch, carefully moving a blanket away from Agatha’s face.

She hasn't slept, clearly, and her eyes are red from crying all night.

“Can I touch you?” Rio asks, achingly gentle, and Agatha’s whole body trembles.

“I was horrible to you.” Agatha manages to get out, before another sob and more tears fall, and Rio can see how desperately she wants Rio's touch but refuses to allow herself to ask for it.

She risks wiping the tears away, and when no harsh words meet her, she kisses her girlfriend's forehead.

“Oh, sweetheart. Lashing out because you're hurting isn't the same as being purposefully horrible to me. Sure, I didn't exactly enjoy it, you know I'm more for praise than degradation, but I didn't take it personally.” She tells her, and suddenly she's being yanked up onto the couch and entangled in the nest of blankets that Agatha has formed for herself.

Agatha is sobbing hard, her whole body wracking with them, and Rio can actually feel her heart shattering in her chest when she hears the soft apologies leaving the other woman's lips.

“Hush, Agatha. Breathe for me. You don't need to apologise, I promise. I'm here, and I'm not going anywhere.” She says, pressing Agatha as hard as she can against her, providing grounding pressure that makes the sobs ease up a little.

“I'm so sorry, Rio.” Agatha says, and Rio smiles as she kisses the tip of her nose.

“No need, I swear. I mean, please don't do it again if you can help it, but I'm not angry with you. I love you so much, Agatha.” She promises, feeling the oath bind to her bones, to her soul.

Agatha is it for her, the love of her life.

Agatha's tears stop slowly, and truly, the woman looks awful. She's pale, dark shadows under her eyes, and red-rimmed eyes. Rio thinks she's beautiful, no matter what.

“Feel up to leaving the blanket nest so you can drink some water, and maybe sleep in an actual bed? The couch can't be good for your back.” Rio asks, and Agatha nods slowly.

Carefully, inch by inch, they extract themselves from the nest and stand up, Rio taking most of Agatha's weight when it becomes clear that the woman is too tired and wrung-out to hold herself up.

Any other day, Agatha would have had literally so many gay thoughts about how Rio lifts her easily, bridal style, and takes her to the bedroom.

Fuck, she's somehow managing to have a few now, even when she's the most depressed she's been in years.

She whines when Rio leaves, but Rio comes back quickly with bottles of water, snacks, and the blanket Agatha loves.

“I love you.” Agatha says, voice hoarse and scratchy, and Rio nods.

She knows.

“Here, I got the rag you love so much.” She teases gently, draping the messy, handmade blanket gently over Agatha, and Agatha snuggles into it with a yawn.

“My favourite.” She mumbles sleepily, and Rio smiles, moving to slide under the covers with her.

“I'm here. You can sleep. I'm not going anywhere.” Rio promises, and Agatha hums in acknowledgement.

“Thank you. For staying, for loving me. I don't deserve you.” Agatha says, and Rio huffs.

There's quite a few ways she can respond to that, but not many that Agatha would accept right now.

“That's not for you to decide. I've decided that I deserve to have you here, safe and warm, in my arms. So either safeword out or shut up and be loved.” She says, and it startles a sleepy laugh out of Agatha.

“Brat.” She responds fondly, tucking her head under Rio's chin and pressing a brief kiss to her collarbone.

Rio giggles, and runs her hand up and down Agatha's back. It's something Agatha had admitted to her before that makes her feel safe.

Soon, Agatha's breathing evens out, slow and soft.

Rio doesn't sleep, simply watches her with adoring eyes.

She loves this woman so fucking much.

And she has the ring, hidden at the back of her closet, to prove it.
