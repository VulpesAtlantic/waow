OR

Leon has a heart to heart with Grace

“You know I was just like you when this all started.” Leon said faintly, his eyes trained on the stars above them.

Grace had called him that night after waking up from a nightmare, and she couldn't quite manage to calm down. Leon had surprised her but driving over to where she was staying and picking her up

“You shouldn't be alone.” Is all he'd said on the matter before driving them to some far out of the way place she'd never seen.

Now they were sitting in the car windows down staring into the night sky.

“I don't believe that.” Grace grumbled, her voice still scratchy from crying.

How could she? Leon was unmovable and unshakable. A force of nature who never even flinched at all the horrors they experienced.

He just chuckled at her response, shaking his head lightly as he shot her a side-long glance. “I was a kid, only twenty-one fresh out of the academy. I decided not to listen to my superiors and came to Raccoon City when I was told to stay away.”

Grace took a sharp breath, head snapping towards him, “What?”

Leon just hummed and nodded, “Rookie cop, It was my first day.”

“You were a year younger than me.” She sounded horrified. Eyes searching him, trying (and failing) to imagine a young version of him in his place.

In response, the agent just shrugged, “Technically I've had two days of work now. Went back by the RPD…the welcome sign was still up.” The tone was melancholic, and it had Grace's heart clenching.

“How did you…how do you deal with it?” She didn't know if she meant that night or all of it in general. Probably both.

He seemed to ponder that for a second, “I didn't at first, hell it still haunts me. But I have people in my corner. Sherry for one, she was there that night too, she was just a kid then about Emily's age. Me and Claire saved her.”

“Claire?”

“The ones who saved us mentioned Captain Redfield, Chris, Claire is his sister. Met her at a gas station out of town. She was looking for him and we went in together.”

“So, you have someone with you during all of that too?” That thought had her heart at slight ease to know that he'd had someone just like she'd had him.

Leon snorted hard, “Not really, me and Claire got separated at the beginning, only met back up at the very end of the night. I wasn't completely alone however. Ran into someone who said they were FBI, I learned later that wasn't true, but she saved my ass many times that night.”

“Is that why you were so surprised when I said FBI?” Grace questioned.

“Yeah, it brought back some memories.”

“So, if not the FBI, then what was she?”

“Mercenary, I was young and dumb would believe anything, never even questioned it. Not until I was told by one of the scientists who started the whole mess.”

Grace didn't seem to know how to respond to that, but her eyes still brimmed with questions, so Leon took pity on her.

“Discovered a lot of stuff that night, stuff that you know now. Umbrella, the experiments, all of it. She promised me justice if I could get her a sample. Found the underground lab, because it's always an underground lab, and retrieved one. Got told by the scientist in no uncertain terms that if I handed it over it would go to the highest bidder.”

“You didn't hand it over.” Grace surmised.

“Nope confronted her and got a gun pulled on me.” He was smiling now looking…wistful?

“I told her I didn't think she could shoot me. We had grown too close that night and saved each other's lives too many times. I was right.”

Grace knew there was so much more to that story but didn't push on the specifics. “So, she made it out with you?”

Leon just sighed and shook his head, “The scientist shot her and she fell off the bridge. I wasn't strong enough to pull her back up, didn't have the leverage, and I was already so hurt. Not even mentioning how injured she was.”

“I…I'm sorry.”

“Don't be, besides she lived. I just didn't know that for 6 years. Still don't know how she got out though…” That last part he tacked on there like an afterthought.

“But I lived with that guilt for a long time. I still do. It's so easy to imagine the what ifs. And it's even easier than that to remember everyone I couldn't save that night, even if there was no reasonable way for me to do it.”

Grace blinked hard as tears came to her eyes. The statement felt like a gut punch with how accurate it was.

“How do you not do it then?” She choked out, unable to stop the tears.

“I'll tell you as soon as I figure it out kid. So far all I've got for you is that it gets better over time. I think the only trick is just to focus on other things that are tangible.

“What do you mean?”

“Emily, she needs you, she's someone you can take care of and give a better life. I did the same thing with Sherry to an extent, but there was only so much I could do given my circumstances.”

“Your...circumstances?” She didn't think she actually wanted to know not with the look in his eyes, but her curiosity got the better of her.

Leon just gave her a ruthful smile, “Like I said I knew too much. I was a liability thus was strong armed under threat of death to join the government with the added incentive of keeping Sherry alive if I agreed.”

Grace just stared wide eyed at him, “...I what?” He'd only been a kid just like her…Jesus Christ the idea that happening to her was horrific.

“Don't worry about it, I'm in an advantageous position now. No one's touching Emily and no one's touching you, they'd have to go through me first.”

Grace hadn't even considered it, hadn't imagined anyone would be cruel enough to do that. To know that it wasn't only possible but had happened…She didn't doubt him for a second however. Leon Kennedy was a force of nature even when he had a foot in the grave, he also kept his word. Even in the short time they'd known each other, she was sure of that.

“It’s so easy to catastrophize…I still wake up to nightmares of you or Emily dying… all because I wasn't able to save you both or I put in the wrong password.”

Leon just gave her a sad look. “I'm alive. You can call me anytime you need that confirmation.”

“BUT YOU WERE DYING!” Grace choked out. “You were throwing up blood, and you passed out on me. I found you collapsed and you could barely walk. You were dying Leon and there was nothing I could do!”

Leon for his part just gave her a small smile before reaching out and laying a hand on her shoulder.

“I'm sorry you had to see me like that, but I promise you it's not the first time and it probably won't be the last. You were strong, you persevered despite it all and you saved my life.”

Grace didn't want to imagine Leon dying in different ways, but it also gave her a weird sort of comfort. The knowledge that despite it all no matter how many times he got close to death he always persevered.

She just felt tears start to fall as she curled in on herself, Leon made a sad sound before getting out of the car and quickly making his way over to her side. He barely got the door opened before she was flinging herself at him.

“I have such a hard time looking at you and not seeing a younger version of myself reflected back. Hell, you nearly quoted me verbatim when you looked at me and said you wanted to come with me, that you didn't care what it took.”

Grace shuttered against him. God, she remembered the way he looked at her like a ghost. Were they really so alike? But she wasn't as brave as him, not nearly as strong and courageous. It felt like stolen valor but was it really if it was him saying it?

“You have romise me you won't wallow in all of this. You are going to reach out and talk to people. I don't care about the time or reason. You are not going to end up like me considering a bullet as an out.”

Grace just gripped onto him even tighter. “I promise.” She choked out between tears. The thought nearly making her sick.

“It's going to be hard. It's going to be hard for a long time. But you have me and the others you don't even know yet. You also have Emily to take care of. You have people that care and that's what makes it all worth it.”

Grace believed him.

oO0Oo

Grace made good on her promise, making sure to call Leon whenever she needed or wanted to. Sometimes multiple times a day, sometimes only just once or not at all. Leon answered happily every time, always ready and willing to talk.

The few times he couldn't because of missions he had her call Sherry instead. Grace had gotten to know her quite well too. She had such good advice on how to deal with Emily, having been in her place. She'd been visited by others as well.

All people who she'd saved from the virus and or worked with Leon in some way. Chris, Claire, Jill, Rebecca, and Carlos. There was more too she was told, all scattered around who all wanted to thank her.

She had no clue what to say but they didn't expect anything from her. They were just happy she survived and was thrilled that she'd helped Leon. She was surprised at how much they seemed to welcome her with open arms. It didn't matter to them that she wasn't a fighter, they couldn't have cared less.

It helped more than she could ever explain.

Leon had been right of course, about all of it. It helped having people take care of and that cared. It also was terrible, the pain never seemed to go away, the fear and terror all of it remained. It was muted sometimes but never gone. She wondered if it compounded the more you went through, she hoped it wasn't the case for Leon's sake.

She called Leon one morning on a particularly hard day, and he'd offered to get lunch with her. She agreed without much thought and Emily and she met him at some hole in the wall dinner.

They walked in Emily, bouncing excitedly at her side. She spotted Leon immediately and was slightly surprised to see an unfamiliar woman at his side. Leon looks up and waves her over.

Grace goes easily and releases Emily, who quickly runs over and gets a hug. Grace eventually manages to get her to sit down, not that Leon seemed to mind.

The mystery woman just watches the whole thing with twinkling eyes, “You know you really are good with kids.” She tells Leon who just huffs a small laugh.

“It's one of my talents.” He agrees, giving her one of the sweetest and most adoring looks she'd ever seen. It was almost startling to see the expression on the normally stoic man.

He then turned back to Grace and gestured at the woman. “This is Ada.”

Ada just nodded at her, “I have to thank you Grace not only did you keep this idiot alive, but you also cursed me.”

Grace blinked, “O-oh I didn't realize there were more Raccoon City survivors I hadn't met yet.”

Emily looked between them curiously as Leon watched on with a smile.

“I'm probably the last one you'll meet. You also wouldn't know about me considering I was never on any reports.”

Grace paused at that. That little detail felt…important. She looked at Leon questioningly but was only met with a knowing smirk. Looking back at Ada, she saw a glimmer of amusement in her dark eyes.

“W-well It's nice to officially meet you. And I would have never been able to get the cure if Leon hadn't kept me alive.” Grace explained.

“He is quite good at that.” She agreed looking over to the man in question, her eyes getting much softer. She brought up her left hand to grab his arm that was lying on the table.

Grace then spotted the ring on her hand. Ah, this was his wife. He'd never mentioned his own ring or his partner, but she had her answer now.

“Yeah, well sometimes I wish I knew my efforts weren't in vain.” He grumbled his tone teasing.

“Yes well, it was a busy six years.” Ada dismissed lazily.

Grace nearly choked.
