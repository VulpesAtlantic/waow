Summary

Peter finds information about Gamora's past and she worries this will be the end of their relationship Prompt: “You deserve so much better than what I can be.”

Gamora is still in shock after rescuing Peter from an enemy ship earlier that day. When she arrived on a ship, they were showing him footage she didn’t even know existed. Footage of her . The look on his face as he watched it showed he really had no idea what she was actually like before forming the Guardians. He seemed horrified by the videos of her torturing and killing innocent people. She can still hear the voice of his captor: “You see this?! This is your girl! This is the heartless killing machine you’ve chosen to fall asleep next to each night. Say she’s awful! Say she deserves to die!” She hasn’t been able to look him in the eye since rescuing him. It was a silent trip back to their ship. She’s now holed up in a coat closet because she’s too ashamed to enter the quarters she shares with Peter. When he finds her, she assumes he’s about to tell her to pack her things. She sees him and breaks down crying. “I know, I get it, we’re over and I need to leave, I’ll be out by this evening, just let me pack up. You deserve so much better than what I can be and I hope you find them soon.”

She stands up and Peter hugs her. “Slow down. You don’t need to leave and we aren’t over. I came in here to see if you were okay, Mora. I know earlier was intense.”

“Intense?! That doesn’t begin to cover it! I didn’t know that footage existed, and now, you’ve seen... You must hate me!”

“I could never hate you. It’s okay. You’re okay. I’ve got you,” Peter assures her.

“Why?! Why don’t you hate me?!”

“That wasn’t you, Mora. I know you. I was telling them the whole time. You aren’t who Thanos forced you to be. That wasn’t you. You wake up early on Sundays to make coffee so I don’t have to get out of bed until I’ve had some. You use your stitching skills from the battlefield to fix Groot’s stuffed animals. You bought Drax a zarg nut flavor of the month subscription just because. You started doing girls' nights with Mantis even though you hate going out because she said she felt like it was a rite of passage she never experienced. You call Nebula four times a week just to check in on her. You are one of the best people I’ve ever met, and your past doesn’t change that. I’ve never met the girl in those videos. You’re my girl, and I defended you the whole time they had me tied up. I’d defend you to the edge of the galaxy if I had to.”

“Then why didn’t you talk to me once you were free?”

“Honestly, I didn’t think you wanted to talk,” Peter says, “That was a mistake. I should’ve assured you I still loved you the second I was untied.”

Gamora kisses him through her tears. “I don’t deserve you…”

“If anything, I don’t deserve you . You are the strongest person I have ever met and I’m so lucky to have you. You’ve put up with so much of my nonsense and I love you more every single day.”

“You promise this doesn’t change anything?” Gamora asks.

“I promise,” Peter says, kissing her, “I love you so much.”

“I love you more, Peter.”
