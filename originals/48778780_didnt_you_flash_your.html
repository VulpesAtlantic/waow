Summary

"You'll be properly compensated, of course", Kallark says, and Gamora tries her very best to focus on him, because this is important, this could be huge.

Sure, they've been cleared by the Nova Corps, but this? A job for the Shi'ar Empress, coming directly from the Gladiator himself? It'll do wonders for their reputation.

Gamora would like that. Being known for something good... she wants to see what that would be like.

She can tell from the gleam in his eye that Rocket is thinking of the cash this is sure to send this way. No more scrimping and saving; they'll finally be able to pay off their growing tab at Starlins.

All she has to do is stop getting distracted by Peter and his ridiculous looks.

Notes

starmora + 3. exchanging secret smiles

Peter Quill is distracting.

It's the only word for it; he keeps catching her eye across the room, sending her little smiles,

looking at her like she's-- like Gamora's-Ugh.

She tries her best to focus on Kallark and the mission brief. Some precious jewel stolen from the Shi'ar, highly coveted by Empress Lilandra, don't they know? Very important to get it back, so important that they're willing to hire some scoundrels and miscreants (not how Gamora'd describe them), and--

There! He's doing it again!

She scowls in his direction. His lips still quirk into a smile.

"You'll be properly compensated, of course", Kallark says, and Gamora tries her very best to focus on him, because this is important, this could be huge.

Sure, they've been cleared by the Nova Corps, but this? A job for the Shi'ar Empress, coming directly from the Gladiator himself? It'll do wonders for their reputation.

Gamora would like that. Being known for something good... she wants to see what that would be like.

She can tell from the gleam in his eye that Rocket is thinking of the cash this is sure to send this way. No more scrimping and saving; they'll finally be able to pay off their growing tab at Starlins.

All she has to do is stop getting distracted by Peter and his ridiculous looks.

He does it all the time, sends her little smiles when nobody else is looking. He'll do it when they are, of course, but these ones she covets more. They're something private, something special, something that is solely hers.

She always expects them to be stolen away from her.

Then she raises her head, and catches him smiling at her again and it's like-- like all those terrible fears that run around her mind lessen, 'cause here it is, here's evidence that she can have something good and pure and hers.

And it-- he-- isn't going anywhere.

Those green eyes run over her as they move towards the Milano and he falls in step with her like it's second nature.

"D'you listen to much of that?" He asks. "'Cause I didn't catch half of it."

"Shiny rock", she says, "stuck in Kree space, stolen by the Corsair. We're getting it back."

"Right, right", he says, nodding along. "We could probably just ask for it back. I know Summers, we go way back, it'll be--"

"It's worth over ten million units."

"Ah. Yeah, we're probably gonna need to shoot someone. Or stab someone, in your case." She can feel her lips twitching towards a smile. "It is more my style."

"Yeah! Guns are my thing, swords are yours."

"And Rocket's?"

"Bombs", Peter says confidently. "Lots of bombs."

Gamora can't help it-- she laughs, a joyful little sound that still feels unfamiliar coming from her. He draws it out of her the most.

His arm falls over her shoulder, and she finds its weight comforting. "C'mon. Let's go shoot some old friends."

"Old friends of yours", she says, wrinkling her nose. "Hepzibah has tried to assassinate me numerous times."

"She doesn't stand a chance", he says without hesitation. "'Mora, you're the most badass woman in the galaxy! What's she gonna do, meow at you?"

She smiles in spite of herself. People have talked about her skills like she's a weapon, but Peter Quill talks about them like they're just one of the many, many attributes about her that he adores. Like she's more than that.

Gamora waits until the other Guardians march into the ship, and she leans up slightly, kissing him on the cheek. She'd seen it in those old movies he's insisted that they watched; she believes it's how Terrans show their affection.

"Come on, Star-Lord", she says, pulling him towards the Milano. "I'll need somebody shooting while I'm stabbing."

He follows her. He always will.
