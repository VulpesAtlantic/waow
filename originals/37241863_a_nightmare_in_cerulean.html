And Mayura is right here.

She’s not lost.

And that of course is the problem.

Voices emanate from Nathalie’s bedroom stopping Gabriel in his tracks outside her room. She’s not one to watch videos on speaker, preferring to use her headphones to avoid disturbing them. And moreover he’s sure one of those voices is Nathalie.

Only…the other voice sounds like it’s in the room too and it also sounds like Nathalie. Mostly.

“I’m the one who can help him.”

“You’re the one who got us stuck in this bed. I can’t just transform whenever anymore. Maybe you should have thought of that before breaking the Miraculous more.”

Whatever’s binding him here seems to both loosen and tighten. Nathalie and Mayura talking to each other, because who else would Nathalie say that to?

It feels like a dream as he wonders what he’d see inside. Mayura given a body of her own, with her hand on the wrist of a Nathalie whole and complete. Or Emilie woken from her sleep by a Nathalie desperate to push that parasite back to her first host?

“You know that wasn’t my aim.” One of them hisses.

His hand twitches. It’s only what he’d thought a moment ago, but to hear Nathalie, Nathalie who he’s implicitly trusted since Tibet, trusted when he didn’t trust Emilie, talk as if that would have been acceptable makes him suddenly dizzy. Lightheaded with rage, as his blood starts to rush for a fight.

Gabriel forces himself to take a deep breath. Nathalie is not herself. That’s why this conversation is happening. Mayura had turned Emilie from someone who thought you could get things from people by being nice to them, to something who blackmailed people in the most horrific ways and with the most perverse images.

That it might cause Nathalie to regret the possible loss of her life is nothing compared to that. This has been as hard on her as on him. He’s seen the pill bottles in her room which aren't for any purely physical ailment. Despite that he knows he has her loyalty, their bond has been forged over the pressure of the years, and then solidified as she sent Emilie to sleep while Gabriel held her down. That remains true. He has to believe that.

Clearly annoyed, Mayura snaps back and Gabriel can imagine the pout on her lips, “you don’t understand.”

“You’re right. I don’t. You know that. But,” Nathalie, he’s sure this speaker is Nathalie sighs, “I do understand love. And we do have at least one person in common there.”

Adrien of course.

The reminder of his son and how he’s depending on Gabriel to bring back his mother, the mother he was always supposed to have pushes him to finally open the door ready to confront-

Nathalie.

By herself.

Standing in front of her mirror.

Discomfited, he tries to cover his confusion with, “should you be standing?”

Nathalie spins round so fast she loses her balance and stumbles and he has to run to catch her.

“Probably not,” she concedes with a weary smile.

Like Hawk Moth with Mayura he carefully lifts her up in his arms carrying her back to the bed, “what were you doing?”

“I-“ her eyes dart sideways, and something in his stomach squirms to see his ever-ready Nathalie too off balance and worn out to have a lie prepared.

“Please tell me you weren’t transforming into her and back to yourself constantly.”

“No,” Nathalie shuts her eyes as he carefully places her onto the bed, “I don’t need to do that anymore. She’s in me now even when I’m just me.”

“Nathalie,” he sighs leaning down and brushing her hair off her face.

“It’s okay.”

“It’s not ok,” he disagrees as he lies down beside her. “You’re not using that Miraculous now, so I’ll take it away and-“

“Gabriel,” she interrupts, “it’s too late for that. It’s better…better to let it be like this. If I’m not fighting she won’t push me to transform. It’s not to her advantage if I die either. This way we, we can negotiate. Like you tried.”

He swallows, “hopefully not quite like I did.”

A blush flows up Nathalie’s face, “ah, no. Not quite like that. I don’t, we. Oh I don’t need to say it.”

Gabriel spares her the need to expand, “did she and Emilie…”

Nathalie’s eyes widen, “did she and Emilie,”

“Talk.” He rushes to clarify, “like you were.”

Emilie had never been lucid about it like this. Had never admitted to awareness of any of this until far too late. But then he and Nathalie have always shared their truths with each other, the admittances wrung out of them when they’d thought they were dying, never quite letting them build their walls back up. While he’d been scheming behind Emilie’s back to manage her for years. Perhaps she’d done the same, whichever of them had shattered their trust first. Perhaps that was the difference. Perhaps it wasn’t Mayura. Perhaps it was Emilie all along.

Nathalie frowns, and he can almost see her brain working behind her eyes. “I don’t think so?” “You don’t sound sure.”

“I remember more and more of what Mayura does, even when I think I’m the one in charge but I don’t think I get Emilie’s sense of it, and I still don’t know everything. But now. I don’t think so. Emilie, Emilie wasn’t aware of this the way I am. I walked into this with my eyes open knowing Mayura. Emilie didn’t.”

He entwines his fingers in hers, “if we ever get a chance to swipe the Rabbit I’m going to go back and destroy every bit of research there is on the Miraculous.”

“Thank you.” She mumbles, but she still seems forlorn.

Comfort is something alien to him. Emilie rarely needed it from him until the end, so certain and confident in everything. And she could always prevent Adrien from needing it, if she didn’t feel like handling herself in a more traditional way.

So he tries for distraction instead. Nathalie’s always been one to bury herself in work, in the challenge.

“Do you know what she wants?” He asks, “What she was trying to do with that brooch?”

Nathalie’s eyes are sad, “She wants to exist. I think she was trying to more permanently make Emilie look like her by changing her physical body but obviously that didn’t work out so well.”

The shadows below her cheekbone make it look sharper than it used to, but now he finds himself studying her face. Trying to work out if it is just the shadow. Trying to remember if the roots of Emilie’s highlighted hair are darker now than they’d once been.

“She does realise being blue would be a little unpractical does she not?” He attempts to, well, he’s not sure if it’s a joke or not.

“I think she could leave that if she got the rest,” Nathalie rolls over onto her back, “maybe I should cut my hair to appease her. Get laser eye surgery. Sort out a timeshare of my body.”

“You sound like you don’t expect me to win.”

“That’s not what I meant. You will win Gabriel. I believe in you, and I’m with you to the end.”

Or, rather, until she’s no longer Nathalie part of his brain reminds him.

Emilie’s sleeping figure seems to judge him as if somewhere under those closed eyelids her eyes themselves still stare at him in fear and horror.

“This was necessary,” he tells her.

The words echo around the cavernous space seeming twisted by the time they come back to his ears. He’d wanted to save Emilie and put an end to Mayura.

Instead he’s frozen Emilie in time below his house and Mayura still walks in the world above.

He wonders what would happen if he let Emilie out now. Would she stumble out on unsteady legs, unwell and needing to take to her bed but free of Mayura now that damned demoness has planted herself in Nathalie instead?

Could he abandon Nathalie to free his wife?

Or would Mayura climb out of that pod and leave him Nathalie herself again and more physically hale, to help him fight Ladybug and Chat Noir?

Or would he be left with Mayura in duplicate, both rushing to their deaths in their attempts to give her existence?

The thought makes him unwell.

It makes him the worst type of husband but even if he was sure that Nathalie’s battle with Mayura would mean Emilie could be free he’s no longer quite sure he’s brave enough for that. He’s relied on Nathalie for longer than he’s treated his beloved wife as an equal partner by now.

Gabriel can talk to Nathalie at any time. She’s always there, ready to take a call, even when she answers blearily startled from her sleep.

But it’s not the same.

Her empty chair is a yearning void in the corner of his office.

Throughout all of it, from the moment Emilie took it into her head to go after these Miraculous Nathalie has been his constant support.

And now he doesn’t know if he can trust her.

He doesn’t know who’s answering if he calls.

Nooroo curls round Duusu in what Gabriel can only think is an attempt at defence. Fair enough he supposes after what those accursed kwamis have done to him. If it wasn’t for the fact that his fingers would phase through Duusu he’d take wrap his fingers round the kwami’s body and squeeze until the bulbous head falls off.

Duusu whimpers, and he hopes his thoughts are why.

With those irritating imploring eyes Nooroo, looks up at him. “It’s not Duusu’s fault master, it’s”

“I don’t remember,” Duusu starts bawling “why don’t I remember?”

For once they have his attention, “she takes from you too?”

“Duusu wasn’t always like this,” Nooroo seems to wilt, and doesn’t have to explain his meaning, Gabriel’s seen the spaciness, the over-emotionality, he’d just thought it was how the Kwami was. “the things she used to create I, You need to fix the Peacock Miraculous master.”

“Do you think I’m not aware of that,” Gabriel snaps. “I’ve looked. Nathalie’s looked. We have for years. If that knowledge ever existed in the world it’s been lost.”

“You could ask the Guardian?”

“After what I’ve done?” The city thinks him a villain, some supercilious moraliser will hardly help now, “after Adrien?” Would the man even left his son live or call him an abomination?, “After what Emilie did?” He’s seen the photos and videos she’d kept on people. Ones that make even him ill, and in which he doesn’t know who’s the sentimonster and who’s real in a way that makes it impossible to meet the eyes of the people he’d once called friends and acquaintances.

He’d burnt what he’d found of himself. He couldn’t have such things in his house. But they’d been oddly tame given Emilie’s threats. An affair with his assistant was tawdry but hardly ground-breaking. It’s another reason no one could enter the house. The fear of there being something worse hidden they might stumble upon.

Nooroo leaves Duusu and flutters in front of his eyes, “it’s the job of the Guardians to care for the Miraculous Jewels. He has to fix it. If his vows bind him at all then he has no choice not too.”

It’s obvious Nooroo means what he’s saying. His Kwami isn’t stupid enough to lie to him much as he clearly disagrees with Gabriel’s actions, lacking the spine to do what’s necessary.

And Gabriel would like to accept the offer. Would like to take the easy route for once except.

“But he has no reason to save Nathalie and Emilie,” Nooroo quails back from him, “does he?”

“If it was repaired then,”

“We can’t know that’s enough. Not now.” Gabriel can’t fail them after all he’s done for them. Gabriel means to send Adrien away from Nathalie. He doesn’t think the boy’s presence is good for her mental state. There’s nothing like the boy she views as her son to bring Mayura out. But sometimes when he finds his son, sat next to her on her bed, while she expands on whatever bit of history he’s learning about in school he doesn’t quite have the heart to.

He hates Mayura. But. She does love Adrien. It seems a safe outlet for her, and it makes Adrien happy without Gabriel having to force him to be. Something he’s not sure he knows how to when he can’t remember being happy himself.

Right now he regrets this decision.

“She what?”

Adrien cringes in front of him, “referred to her husband. She’s never been married has she?”

“No,” Gabriel pinches at the bridge of his nose, “no. It’s her illness. She gets a little confused.”

“Right.” Adrien’s nose scrunches up, “I kind of thought she was talking about you, she was talking about you earlier, but then she said she was so excited for me to meet him properly so apparently no but,”

“Adrien, there’s nothing romantic between myself and Nathalie,” Gabriel stops him automatically. “perhaps it’s best you stay away from Nathalie until she’s feeling better.”

The uneasiness in his stomach that his son’s words spark lasts all day though.

Mayura has always acted as if he’s her husband. But Adrien hardly needs to meet him. So who on earth is she talking about?

Adrien’s out of the house so he doesn’t hesitate to transform in Nathalie’s room.

Regardless of whether it’s Nathalie or Mayura by his side she’s always so unfailingly encouraging. Given him little smiles. Complimenting his akumas. Suggesting further plans.

She laughs at one of his comebacks to Ladybug once, and it makes him feel warm. Nathalie has been such a dark pool of despair to the magic emanating from his chest even as she’s tried to keep up a determined front, and Mayura is many many different things but light has never been one of them, or at least not since he still thought she was Emilie and maybe she did too.

It’s nice to see them smile not smirk, and he can’t help but grin back.

Perhaps it’s only the facsimile of partnership while the woman he trusts fades away for one with her own motives, but it’s still the only thing that gets him through this day.

They both care for him. That much is true. So while he knows he’s married, and he knows it would be beyond wrong to use Nathalie’s body like Mayura’s still in his wife’s he doesn’t attempt to stop her as she runs her hand through his hair while he sits by her from his debrief. It’s mostly Nathalie anyway he thinks. Nathalie seeing where things went wrong. Nathalie coming up with ingenious plans that might let them win.

That has to be Nathalie.

Right?

Adrien sits obediently silent on the car journey. Typical Bourgeois nonsense having the event out of Paris, but Nathalie had hacked the guest list and there’s a man there they’ve dealt with before when trading for secrets about the Miraculous.

Usually of course Nathalie would handle all of this but he can hardly send her in his current state either physically or mentally.

However Adrien will be distraction enough for those who want to talk. Talking about his son tends to distract people from other things, and its good training for Adrien too. They’d wanted him to have Emilie’s gregariousness much as he fears that Mayura might have put her own personality in there instead. Not that she’s shy either.

The glow of Adrien’s phone illuminates his sons face as Adrien watches whatever he looks at intently.

“I’m guessing that’s not educational,” Gabriel says dryly.

Adrien takes out an earbud with the slightest flush to his cheeks, “It’s sort of a good idea to try and stay updated on the heroes of Paris isn’t it? Only so I know how to avoid them of course. I’m not going to try and impress Ladybug or anything stupid like that. That would be ridiculous.”

There’s a strain to Adrien’s smile that sets Gabriel on edge. One of them having to deal with the Miraculous, and a lady wearing a Miraculous is quite enough.

Though at this point he’ll take any input that might help him, “anything interesting?”

“Just about Mayura’s disappearance,” his heart stops, “it’s strange isn’t it? Maybe she and Hawk Moth got divorced.”

Carefully ignoring that can of worms Gabriel carefully says, “Whatever her reasons I suppose her absence is a good thing.”

Adrien smiles, almost seeming nervous for some reason, “One fowl villain down then?”

The corner of his mouth tips up without permission, “what a pea-ty for Hawk Moth.”

His son grins, “that was terrible.”

“It was rather, wasn’t it?” He smiles back and then both of them are laughing, and throwing worse and worse ones back at each other.

The journey is the closest he’s felt to his son in years. The first time they’ve seemed to have anything in common for even longer.

Turns out Hawk Moth’s colourful turns of phrase are actually good for something.

Her eyes glow with encouragement, “the user sets the limits Hawk Moth. You can do this.”

He tries. It feels like electricity shooting through his chest. But it works.

Ladybug’s charm fails.

Hawk Moth moves a step ahead of their enemies.

Nathalie cheers him on.

His eyes are heavy as he sits down next to Nathalie, and despite him not intending it at all he begins to doze off against her headboard.

She must think he’s asleep as her lips brush over his forehead. “Sleep for now darling. We’ll be together again soon.”

Her hand covers his Miraculous, comfortably warm against his skin.

“Nathalie no,” he mumbles, “remember who you are.”

“Just for a little longer,” she’s warm and filling the void by his side.

“For as long as you can.”

Her lips trail across his forehead again, “I’m with you to the end remember?”

Knowing Adrien is so heartbroken almost brings him to his knees. He has failed as a parent. Failed in his promises to Emilie. Failed to keep his son happy and satisfied when he’s literally had the power to keep him sanguine and satisfied all this time.

That’s neglect of duty isn’t it?

Emilie. Nathalie. Mayura. They’d all be disappointed in him.

So he does his best to push against his usual instincts, to put aside the laser-focus on the Miraculous that’s kept him steady to try to be more open.

“Adrien, since your mother disappeared, I must've been distant, but,” he swallows and wishes Adrien’s mother was here, “I want you to know that, if you needed to, you could,” it would be easier to use the ring but now he has the chance he thinks he understands why Emilie didn’t, “talk to me.”

“I’m fine father,” Adrien rebuffs him.

“Good, that’s good.”

Adrien pauses at the door, “is purple the new season colour?”

“What?”

Adrien gestures at him and it’s only then Gabriel notices todays trousers are a wine-dark purple rather than red.

“I haven’t decided.” Gruffly he deflects despite his rising unease.

That gains him a scrunched up face from his son. “It’s a little bit too Hawk Moth isn’t it?”

“I hadn’t noticed,” but Adrien’s right and he wonders how he didn’t notice it before, “probably best to stay away from it then.”

Once Adrien disappears he goes upstairs and changes his clothes, flinging them to the floor as if he can destroy them if he throws them hard enough.

They lie there on the floor mocking him.

He must have bought them long before anything he’d ever known to fear the Miraculous.

His skin prickles and he almost expects to see his hands encased in leather.

Gabriel takes a shower as if Hawk Moth’s stain is something so easy to wash off.

Nathalie looks up at him through the tablet screen with such devotion, “You can. You know that Hawk Moth. They’re nothing stopping you but yourself.”

Her trust in him is heady. He feels less like her friend or her employer than her god with the pure worship in her gaze

It’s a long time since Gabriel’s fenced, and months since he’s practised fighting now Nathalie isn’t around to spar with, but his time as Hawk Moth comes through thankfully.

It’s almost like he’s fought Adrien like this before.

Always Hawk Moth, with every exhalation he forces out of her with his lips against her skin, with every exclamation from his hands on her, always Hawk Moth.

He kisses her to shut her up.

But it had only been banked, not damped, and when she’d taken his face in her hands he hadn’t stopped her. Overcome by a desire for the closeness he’d been missing.

It had felt right.

Even the name doesn’t feel as wrong as it should.

That feels important now as it sparks in a way that looks oh-too-familiar as he lies next to his wife.

Or.

Not his wife.

Hawk Moth’s wife.

Al those little hints, why Mayura would encourage him on a quest which would end her existence.

No. It had been about giving Hawk Moth time to manifest.

Perhaps it’s no danger to them.

They don’t have a partner pushing them to push beyond their limits and speeding it up. Or maybe they do. Maybe Ladybug’s little shielding charms were as dangerous to her as breaking them was for him.

With tonight, with how she’d encouraged him to his destruction he knows Nathalie, trustworthy faithful Nathalie was gone.

Had Mayura deemed Nathalie more suited to her purposes than Emilie? Is Nathalie closer to the platonic image of her? Was it just the deeper rents in the Miraculous?

Maybe it’s Gabriel’s affection for Nathalie.

But Gabriel pulled himself up from mediocracy and having just-enough on bloody fingers. He made his own name one recognised worldwide. He put his new wife, and then son front and centre of the empire he made.

Beguiling as this life here, with her, is he’s not willing to disappear into nothing for another man to take his place.

He forces himself leave the bed.

To leave the woman occupying a corner of his heart that’s no longer his.

“I need your help.”

“Forgive me if this feels like a trick.” Ladybug says, guardedly and dismissively but not, if he’s reading her right, wholly unwilling to listen.

“I think Mayura’s trying to kill me.”

Like a chorus the heroes echo together, “Wait, what?”

He sighs and steels himself. It’s a tawdry tale and an embarrassing one. Especially in front of these two who look at him like he's dirt under their shoes.

They won’t agree with all he's done. They won't understand his reasons. They’ll condemn him and it’ll take painstaking work to keep his public image intact, to keep himself out of prison.

But they’re his only chance.
