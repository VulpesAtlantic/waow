Summary

A slow morning with Ada followed by Leon getting jealous

Leon loved times like these. The relief that came post mission, making it home safely, taking a well deserved shower and then melting into a slumber in her embrace… He enjoyed his private life and wouldn’t have it any other way. Years of building up, ages of miscommunication and yearning… Link ups in motel rooms and sneaky meetings in rooftops while both being injured… They all led to these days. Sometimes, he wanted to let everyone in the world know this woman was his partner, the one he vowed to; but he knew that being private was the safer option for both of them. However today, he was surely being tested.

It was a perfect day from the start, a slow morning with Ada tending his injuries from the latest mission while talking about how easy it had been to get on Chris’ nerves. Leon had noticed this was how the relationship between Ada and Chris was, both working on the same side didn’t mean that she couldn’t tease her new companion in the field. After that, Leon baked chocolate chip cookies to eat with their coffee later while Ada was watching some old movie. Her being a cinephile was one of the things only he knew about her.

“Oh, we have run out of coffee beans.” Leon sulked.

“Let’s go to the store.” Ada stopped the movie and got up from the couch. She looked absolutely stunning in her silky burgundy nightdress, which was a rare sight as she didn’t like having her sleepwear on during the day.

It took her pretty quick to change into her outdoor clothes and they soon left Ada’s— no, their loft house.

“I thought we’d better walk.” Ada mused, Leon was just glad she didn’t nag him about leaving the Porsche at Raccoon City this time. They did have other cars, but it made sense she wanted to walk after a slow morning.

The weather was chilly and there were brown leaves on the ground. He grabbed her hand and they linked their fingers, they weren’t the type of couple to often display physical affection publicly, but their last missions took a toll on both of them.

At the entrance of the store they were greeted with a sign that was showing the products on sale. The sound system Leon was recently obsessed with was down by 30 percent, Ada picked up on the smile slowly forming on his lips as he took in the view.

“Ada, we have to buy this!”

“We came here for coffee beans, Leon.” She rolled her eyes, she wasn’t in the mood for changing something in the living room.

“But this one is awesome!” He replied with a sparkle in his eyes, briefly reminding her of the young man he met in Raccoon city back in 1998. “It’ll play your jazz music even better!”

Ada sighed. “Fine, you can go check it out while I’m at the coffee section. Then we will talk.”

They separated after arriving at the tech aisle, but Ada stopped at the next section. There was a child around 5 years old desperately trying to tell something to the employees in tears, but they were confused. After paying attention, she realized the child was talking in French.

“I can’t see my mother anywhere, please help me find her!”

“I’ll tell them to announce your name from the speakers, okay?” Ada crouched and chimed in, speaking in French. This was a big store and it was likely a small child could get lost.

The boy nodded, wiping his tears.

“Gabriel! Gabriel, here you are!” A woman ran towards them and the boy sprinted to her. Her intervention wasn’t so necessary after all.

She got up and smiled at the stressed woman who looked thankful.

“You handled this so gracefully, madam.” A very good looking man in his 40s said, looking at Ada. She knew he was there the whole time, but he chose to not take a part in the incident.

“I figured I could help with my language skills.” She replied nonchalantly.

“You’re a very elegant and smart woman.” He continued, “A pity that I am seeing you around here only now.”

There were rare instances that Ada wouldn’t know what to do or say and this was one of them. Just as she started removing her gloves, she heard the familiar voice behind her.

“Darling, what happened? Why aren’t you at the coffee aisle?” Leon chimed in, wrapping his arm around her waist. He realized Ada was just removing her gloves to display her promise ring.

“My wife always helps people, I’m used to hearing good things about her, within respect.”

“Wife?” Ada thought to herself but it wasn’t like she was complaining. It just meant that Leon was irritated for real, she didn’t know how much he had heard but it was sure enough to put him off like that.

“Glad to hear that.” Fortunately, the stranger wasn’t looking for further trouble, perhaps he was a little intimidated by Leon. However he didn’t forget to make a remark, “You’re a lucky man.”

“I know that very well.” Leon responded coldly.

They walked away from there towards the aisle Ada originally wanted to go to.

“Come on, let’s buy the coffee and get the hell out of here.” Leon said, audibly annoyed. Ada wanted to tease him a little. “What about the sound system? Did it not turn out to be great?”

“It is great. I’m just not in the mood, either.” He puffed his cheeks and Ada decided to not press it further.

“Some coffee and your baked cookies will lift our spirits up. Let’s go.”

Their walk back home was rather silent, with Ada making remarks about the weather or what to eat for dinner here and there. However, their hands were still linked together. It could be just Ada but she felt like his grip was stronger than earlier that day. She knew he wasn’t mad at her, it wouldn’t be logical either. But he just needed his own space for the time being. The fact that he had just come back from such an intense mission didn’t help him at all. She kind of regretted not going and checking the sound system with him.

Ada prepared two cups of cappuccino. Leon was still deep in thoughts, his light blue eyes looked icy.

“Here, Leon.” She set their matching cups on the table, then brought the cookies.

“Hey, handsome. It’s valid, I understand you. But let’s try to cheer up, okay?” Leon finally looked up to meet her deep brown eyes that were filled with love and concern. He knew that, he didn’t doubt Ada’s feelings.

“It’s not just jealousy. I have to admit, a big part of my mood right now is because of that. It’s…” he took a deep breath and stared outside the window before continuing. The wind was scowling harder.

“I feel like I don’t do well enough as a partner sometimes, as if I’m lacking. Maybe I could have arranged things for us and we could be legally married—“

“Leon, we talked about this many times before. There are many factors that led us to this path.” She interrupted him, while inspecting her promise ring without taking it off.

“I know, but sometimes my thoughts are spiraling.” He sighed, it was not a secret that their situation over the years had been far from perfect. But Ada said she wouldn’t have it any other way, she thought the bumps on the road made them who they are.

“It’s fine, just know that I love you so much.” Outright love confessions from her weren’t common. Her love language was acts of service, usually behind the doors; or rather, saving his ass when he was in trouble. She must really be feeling for me, Leon thought.

“I should get jealous more often.” He replied with a light chuckle and Ada lightly tapped on his hand.

“I wouldn’t say no to it. You look so damn good when you’re angry.”

She closed the distance between them, their faces were now only a few inches apart. His wrinkles were more noticeable than ever and she had to admit that made him more attractive to her.

She whispered “And I mean it.” right before pressing her lips onto his. Leon liked this peck but he desperately needed more. His fingers traced over Ada’s jaw, pulling her closer once again. He noted how her eyes darkened and her cheeks flushed before their lips melted into another kiss. It was a longer one, they both wanted to savor the moment with all they had. They knew they were both yearning for each other, even if it had already been a few days since both of them were back from their missions. This time, they were apart from each other and both were pushed to their limits.

Shortly after they parted, Ada cupped his face and started peppering him with kisses. As she kissed his upper lip, Leon slowly got up from his seat and she wrapped her legs around his waist, then carried her to the couch. Just as he put her down, Ada ran her hand through his silky hair, pulling him closer for another kiss. It was a passionate and long one that left both of them out of breath once they were done.

Leon felt as if they were taken back to the time they had their second kiss, when they met at a motel room after the events of the Spain mission. He felt the same burning passion he did back then, one he could never forget or mistake with anything else.

—

“The cookies… Chef Kennedy has done it again.” Ada was very impressed with how the cookies turned out.

“And the coffee is nothing short of great.” Leon chuckled before continuing. “I think it was worth that we went to the store and whatever happened there. Other than what we just did.”

His words earned a light laughter from Ada. It was now dark outside, the city lights looked very pretty.

“We will still be buying that sound system though, gorgeous.” He finally said.
